# 2023 Forbes Holiday Cocktail Guide

*Source: Forbes*

This document contains 13 holiday cocktail recipes from acclaimed bartenders and bars around the world, including:

## Cocktails Included

1. **Untitled Amaro Cocktail** - Amaro Santoni, bourbon, dry vermouth
2. **Kindred Joy** (Connaught Bar, London) - Patrón Silver, Three Spirit Spark, Ancho Reyes, tomato liqueur, yuzu syrup, pink grapefruit soda
3. **The Turtledove** (Yacht Club, Denver) - Hamilton Black Jamaican Rum, Verdelho Madeira, Campari, pineapple juice, lime juice, cinnamon syrup
4. **Potter's Old Fashioned** (Martiny's, New York) - Chai tea and butter-washed bourbon, coconut palm syrup, Abbott's Bitters
5. **Coca-Cola Sour** (Lyaness, London) - Beefeater gin, lemon juice, orange juice, Campari, pomegranate-and-pepper caramel, egg white
6. **Parsnip** (Himkok, Oslo) - Parsnip-infused Buffalo Trace Bourbon, maple syrup, Angostura Cocoa Bitters
7. **Tannenbaum Negroni** (Swift Borough, London) - Gin, sweet vermouth, Port, simple syrup, Angostura bitters, allspice liqueur
8. **Bananarama Backflip** (Botanist Bar, Vancouver) - Aged rum, maple syrup, cream, banana, egg, cinnamon
9. **Fig Leaf & Pine French 75** (KOL, London) - Tequila Ocho reposado, fig leaf liqueur, spruce tip syrup, verjus, L'Arbre aux Abeilles, soda
10. **360 Kick-Flip** (Bar Banane, Toronto) - Johnnie Walker Black Label, alpine amaro, coffee liqueur, ristretto coffee, malted barley Guinness syrup, egg
11. **You Don't Gnome Me** (Three Dots and a Dash, Chicago) - Mijenta tequila, apple brandy, lime juice, pineapple juice, guava puree, almond orgeat, cinnamon syrup
12. **Beyond Smoke & Mirrors** (Atwater Cocktail Club, Montreal) - Patrón añejo tequila, manzanilla sherry, Cocchi Americano, Cocchi Rosa, lavender bitters, celery bitters, citrus bitters
13. **Apple Lit Punch** (Midnight Rambler, Dallas) - Vodka, apple brandy, aged rum, apple cider simple syrup, lemon juice, apple soda, Angostura Bitters
14. **Gnome Alone** (NoMad Bar, New York) - Cocchi Vermouth di Torino, Averna amaro, club soda, lime juice, Absolut Elyx, cinnamon, ginger, allspice
15. **Hōjicha Tea Punch** (Overstory, New York) - Tokki Soju, hōjicha tea, aromatic rich syrup, almond extract, orange blossom water, lemon juice, chocolate bitters, Angostura bitters
16. **Eggnog Fouetté** (Schmucks, New York) - Buttered Cognac, amontillado sherry-cinnamon syrup, black milk tea, half-and-half cream, egg, salt solution
17. **Piña Panela Sour** (Platform 18, Phoenix) - Spiced reposado tequila, egg whites, panela syrup, pineapple juice, lime juice, panela salt crumbles
