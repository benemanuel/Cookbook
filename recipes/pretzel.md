# pretzel

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 255g (1 cup + 1 Tbsp) warm water
- 13g (1 Tbsp) brown sugar
- 4g (1 tsp) active dry yeast
- 200g sourdough discard
- 567g (4½ cups) all-purpose flour
- 12g (2 tsp) salt
- 56g (4 Tbsp) unsalted butter, softened

### Boiling Bath
- 2kg (8 cups) water
- 192g (¾ cup) baking soda

### Topping
- 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)
- Flaky sea salt

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl or stand mixer, combine warm water, brown sugar, and yeast. Let sit 5 minutes until foamy.
2. Add sourdough discard, flour, and salt. Mix until a shaggy dough forms, then add softened butter. Knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
3. Cover and let rise at room temperature for 1 hour, or until noticeably puffed.
4. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment.
5. Bring water and baking soda to a boil in a large pot.
6. Divide dough into 8 equal pieces. Roll each into a long rope (about 50cm), then shape into a pretzel.
7. Working in batches, boil pretzels in the baking soda bath for 30 seconds per side. Transfer to prepared baking sheets.
8. Brush each pretzel with egg wash and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
9. Bake for 13–15 minutes until deep mahogany brown. Makes 8 pretzels.
