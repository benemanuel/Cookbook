# Slow Cooker Beef Bone Broth

*Source: The Healthy Foodie*

## Ingredients

- 1.36 kg mixed beef bones, oxtail, knuckles, neckbones and/or short ribs
- 2 medium carrots
- 3 celery stalks
- 2 medium onions
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 bay leaf
- 2.5 kg water

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 200°C.
2. Place the bones in a single layer on a baking sheet or roasting pan and roast for 60 minutes, flipping each bone over halfway through cooking time.
3. Roughly chop the vegetables without peeling them and place them along with the bay leaf and apple cider vinegar into a 6 quart slow cooker.
4. Add the bones as soon as they come out of the oven and fill the pot completely with water.
5. Set the slow cooker on low, cover and cook for 18 to 36 hours, or until the broth has reached a beautiful brown color.
6. Carefully remove the larger pieces with a slotted spoon and place them in a strainer set over a large bowl to collect the excess broth.
7. Strain the broth that's still in the crockpot through a fine meshed sieve or through a strainer lined with cheesecloth.
8. Transfer the bone broth into jars and refrigerate promptly.
9. Once the broth has fully cooled, remove some or all of the fat layer that formed at the top for use in cooking.
