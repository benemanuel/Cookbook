# Magic Three Layer Custard Cake

*Source: recipetineats.com*

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs, yolks and whites separated (at room temperature)
- 180ml (150g) sugar
- 5ml vanilla extract
- 1 stick (125g) unsalted butter, melted
- 180ml (115g) plain all purpose flour
- 480ml (500 ml) milk, lukewarm (full fat or low fat but not zero fat)
- Icing sugar (powdered sugar), for dusting
- Fresh strawberries, for serving
- Whipped cream, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 163°C/160C.
2. Butter an 8" x 8" square cake tin.
3. Beat egg whites with a mixer until stiff peaks form and set aside.
4. Place the egg yolks and sugar in a bowl and beat until pale yellow, about 1 minute.
5. Add the vanilla extract and melted butter and beat until well incorporated.
6. Add the flour and beat until just combined.
7. Pour the milk in slowly while beating until well combined.
8. Use a spatula to fold in the egg whites one third at a time until just incorporated.
9. Pour the batter into the prepared cake tin.
10. Bake until the top is golden brown and the cake does not jiggle when gently shaken, around 40 to 50 minutes.
11. Check the cake at 30 minutes and if the top is golden brown but the cake jiggles, cover loosely with foil and return to the oven for 10 minutes at a time until set.
12. Allow the cake to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a cooling rack and cool completely.
13. Cut into squares and dust with icing sugar just before serving.
14. Serve with whipped cream and strawberries if desired.
