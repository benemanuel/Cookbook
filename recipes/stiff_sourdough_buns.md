# Stiff Sourdough Buns

Simple sourdough burger/sandwich buns made with a stiff dough at 74% hydration.

## Ingredients

- 150g active sourdough starter (100% hydration)
- 565g bread flour
- 418g water
- 12g salt

**Total dough: ~1,145g**

## Instructions

1. Mix the starter and water together until combined. Add the flour and salt, and mix until no dry flour remains.
2. Rest covered for 30 minutes (autolyse).
3. Perform 3–4 sets of stretch-and-folds over the first 2 hours of bulk fermentation (every 30 minutes).
4. Continue bulk fermentation at room temperature until the dough has grown 50–75% and looks airy, typically 4–6 hours at 24°C.
5. Divide the dough into portions (see yield table below), pre-shape into rounds, and bench rest covered for 20–30 minutes.
6. Final-shape each bun into a tight ball and place on a parchment-lined tray, leaving room to spread.
7. Cover and proof at room temperature for 2–3 hours, or overnight in the fridge.
8. Preheat the oven to 220°C. If proofed in the fridge, bake straight from cold.
9. Optionally brush with egg wash or water and score lightly.
10. Bake for 18–22 minutes until deep golden brown. Cool on a rack before slicing.

## Notes

| Dough per bun | Number of buns |
|---------------|----------------|
| 80g           | 14 buns        |
| 90g           | 12–13 buns     |
| 100g          | 11 buns        |
| 115g          | 10 buns        |
| 140g          | 8 buns         |

- For a softer crumb, replace 50g water with 50g milk and add 30g butter during mixing.
- For a shinier crust, brush with egg wash (1 egg + 15ml milk) before baking.
