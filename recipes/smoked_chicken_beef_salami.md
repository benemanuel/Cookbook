# Smoked Chicken & Beef Salami

A deli-style cured loaf made from blended chicken and beef, cold-smoked low and slow. Pink-cured interior, firm slices, light smoke aroma — closer to a fine roast beef-ham hybrid than a sausage.

## Ingredients

### Meat (700 g total)

- 250 g beef, lean (chuck or shoulder), cubed
- 225 g chicken thighs, skin removed, cubed
- 225 g chicken breast, cubed

### Cure & Seasoning

- 12 g kosher salt (1.7%)
- 1.75 g curing salt #1 (0.25%)
- 8–10 g honey (optional)
- 2 g brown sugar (optional)
- 60–70 g ice-cold water

### Seasoning

- 0.5 g white pepper
- Small pinch nutmeg
- 0.5 g garlic powder — or 5 g strained garlic-infused honey (count toward the honey total)

### Optional Honey-Garlic Glaze

- Thin layer of honey-garlic mixture, brushed on in the last 60–90 minutes

## Instructions

1. Cube all meat. Chill in freezer 30–45 minutes until firm but not frozen.
2. Blend beef alone in food processor until a paste forms.
3. Add chicken and process until sticky.
4. Add salt, curing salt, sugar, and half the water. Blend until very sticky.
5. Slowly drizzle in remaining water while blending. Final texture should be smooth, glossy, sticky, and slightly elastic. If crumbly, blend longer.
6. Transfer to a loaf pan or shape into a freeform log. Press firmly to remove air pockets and smooth the surface.
7. Refrigerate 1–2 hours to firm up before cooking.
8. Cook in smoker (Ninja Woodfire or equivalent) at 70°C chamber temp on a rack with a drip tray underneath.
9. Add light smoke for the first 2–3 hours, then stop adding pellets.
10. Cook until internal temperature reaches 68–70°C. Do not exceed 72°C internal. Total time approximately 8–12 hours.
11. Optional glaze: in the last 60–90 minutes brush a very thin layer of honey-garlic glaze. Optionally raise to 120°C for 5 minutes at the end to set the surface.
12. Remove immediately. Cool 20–30 minutes, wrap tightly, and refrigerate overnight.
13. Slice cold only.

## Notes

- Keep flavors restrained — this is deli style, not sausage.
- At 700 g the loaf may reach internal temp faster than a full kilo; use a probe thermometer.
- Shelf life: 5–7 days refrigerated; freezes well sliced.
- **Alternative version available**: see `smoked_chicken_beef_salami_ninja.md` — a scaled-up (3 kg), commercial-grade variant for the Ninja Woodfire OG701. It uses an emulsified-paste method with a 24-hour color cure, a naked cold-smoke stage at 120°C, then a plastic-and-foil wrap finished low-and-slow, an ice-bath shock, and a 24-hour rest before paper-thin slicing — quite different from this recipe's simpler shape-smoke-glaze approach.
