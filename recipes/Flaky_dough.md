# Flaky Dough

## Ingredients

- 185g (1¼ cups) all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- 115g (8 Tbsp) cold unsalted butter, cut into cubes
- 2½ Tbsp ice water

## Instructions

1. Combine flour, sugar, and salt in a food processor.
2. Add cold butter cubes and pulse until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with pea-sized pieces of butter remaining.
3. Add ice water one tablespoon at a time, pulsing briefly after each addition, until the dough just comes together when pressed.
4. Turn dough out onto a sheet of plastic wrap.
5. Shape into a disc, wrap tightly, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
6. Roll out on a lightly floured surface to the desired thickness.
7. Drape dough over the pie tin, pressing gently into the edges.
8. Trim and crimp edges.
9. For blind baking, line with parchment and fill with pie weights.
10. Bake at 200°C/390°F for 15 minutes, remove weights, and bake another 5–10 minutes until golden.
