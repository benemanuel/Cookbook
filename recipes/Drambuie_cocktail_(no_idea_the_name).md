# Drambuie Cocktail

## Ingredients

- 1 part Drambuie
- 1 part Ginger Ale
- 1 part soda or sparkling white wine
- 1 part orange juice
- Grated ginger for garnish

## Instructions

1. Fill a glass with ice.
2. Add equal parts Drambuie, ginger ale, soda or sparkling white wine, and orange juice.
3. Garnish with grated ginger.
