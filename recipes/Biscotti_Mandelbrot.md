# Biscotti Mandelbrot with Chocolate, Hazelnuts and Orange

## Ingredients

- 250g plain flour
- 240g caster sugar
- 2 large eggs or 3 small ones
- 2 oranges, zested
- 2 tsp fennel seeds
- 200g hazelnuts (almonds or pistachios)
- 150g dark chocolate
- 1 tsp baking powder
- Icing sugar
- A pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Toast the nuts at 200°C until gently golden brown, then crush and chop into smaller pieces.
2. Bash the fennel seeds with a bit of the sugar in a pestle and mortar then chop up the chocolate.
3. Combine all the ingredients in a bowl and mix thoroughly.
4. Split the mixture into two and roll out into logs on a bed of icing sugar to stop them sticking.
5. Lay the logs on baking trays with parchment paper and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
6. Remove from the oven, leave to cool a little, then chop into biscuits at an angle while still warm.
7. Lay the biscotti out on the trays and bake again at 150°C for another 20 minutes or more, turning halfway through until they are dry but not too hard.
8. Store in a jar when cooled and they will last for ages.
