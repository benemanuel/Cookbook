# Cabbage Manchurian

## Ingredients

- 720ml cabbage finely chopped
- 5ml chilli powder
- 2ml pepper powder
- 2ml salt
- 5ml ginger garlic paste
- 60ml maida
- 30ml corn flour
- oil for frying
- 30ml oil
- 1 chilli chopped
- 5 clove garlic chopped
- 1 inch ginger chopped
- ½ onion chopped
- 30ml spring onion chopped
- ½ capsicum chopped
- 30ml tomato sauce
- 30ml vinegar
- 30ml soy sauce
- 15ml chilli sauce
- 120ml water
- 5ml corn flour

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl take 720ml cabbage, 5ml chilli powder, 2ml pepper powder, 2ml salt and 5ml ginger garlic paste.
2. Squeeze and mix well making sure everything is well combined.
3. Add 60ml maida and 30ml corn flour and mix well forming a dough without adding any extra water.
4. Grease the hand with oil and prepare small ball-sized balls.
5. Deep fry in hot oil on medium flame, stirring occasionally until the manchurian balls turn golden brown and crisp.
6. Drain off to remove excess oil and keep aside.
7. Heat 30ml oil, add 1 chilli, 5 clove garlic, 1 inch ginger and stir fry on high flame.
8. Add ½ onion, 30ml spring onion and ½ capsicum and stir fry on high flame.
9. Add 30ml tomato sauce, 30ml vinegar, 30ml soy sauce, 15ml chilli sauce, 2ml salt and 2ml chilli powder.
10. Stir and mix well until all the sauces are combined and add 60ml water to prevent the sauce from burning.
11. Prepare a corn flour slurry by mixing 5ml corn flour in 60ml water and pour into the sauce.
12. Mix well and cook until the sauce turns glossy.
13. Add fried cabbage balls and 30ml spring onion and mix gently coating the sauce uniformly.
14. Serve cabbage manchurian with tomato sauce.
