# Chimichurri Sauce

## Ingredients

- 1 small bunch flat leaf parsley
- 1 small bunch cilantro
- ¼ cup fresh oregano leaves
- 1 Tbsp. capers
- 1 small shallot, minced
- 4 large cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 small chili pepper, minced (optional)
- 2 Tbsp. sherry or red wine vinegar
- 2 Tbsp. lemon juice
- ⅔ cup flavorful olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Trim the bottoms off the parsley and cilantro.
2. Use a chef's knife with a rocking motion to finely mince the herbs, stems and all.
3. Add the oregano leaves and capers to the pile and mince into the rest of the herbs, then move them all to a mixing bowl.
4. Stir in shallot, garlic, chili if using, vinegar, lemon juice, and olive oil.
5. Add salt and pepper to taste, then allow to rest for at least 15 minutes, preferably longer.
6. Taste again and adjust with more salt, pepper, herbs, or acid as you see fit.
7. Refrigerate in a glass jar or tightly sealed plastic container for up to three days.
