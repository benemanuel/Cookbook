# Slow-Cooked Beef Bourguignon

*Source: null*

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces thick-cut bacon (5 to 6 slices), diced
- 2 1/2 to 3 pounds beef chuck roast, round roast, or other similar cut, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 2 cups good red wine, divided
- 2 medium yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 3 medium carrots, diced
- 3 whole stalks celery, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 3 to 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 cup chicken or beef broth, plus more if necessary
- 1 pound white button mushrooms, sliced
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Chopped parsley for garnish
- Cooked pasta or crusty baguette for serving

## Instructions

1. Warm a stainless steel or cast iron skillet over medium-high heat and add the bacon, cooking until the fat has rendered and the bacon is golden and crispy, then transfer to a paper towel-lined plate to drain.
2. Pour off all but a tablespoon of bacon fat from the pan and return the pan to medium-high to high heat.
3. Pat the beef cubes dry, sprinkle with salt and pepper, and sear in a single layer in batches without crowding the pan, allowing 1 to 3 minutes per side until golden-brown.
4. Transfer the seared meat to your slow cooker or a clean bowl and deglaze the pan with 1/4 cup of wine, scraping up the dark glaze and crispy bits, then pour the wine over the seared meat.
5. Continue searing the remaining meat in batches, deglazing between each batch with additional bacon grease and wine.
6. Add bacon grease to the pan, reduce heat to medium, and cook the onions with 1/4 teaspoon salt until soft and browned, then add carrots, celery, garlic, and tomato paste and cook until fragrant.
7. Transfer the vegetable mixture to the slow cooker or bowl with the meat.
8. Cook the mushrooms in the pan with 1/4 teaspoon salt until they release their liquid, the liquid evaporates, and they are golden brown, then transfer to a separate bowl.
9. For slow cooker method: stir the beef and vegetables together with 1 teaspoon salt, tuck in the thyme and bay leaf, pour in the stock and remaining wine until liquid comes 3/4 way up the ingredients, cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 hours until beef falls apart easily with a fork.
10. For oven method: preheat oven to 300°F, transfer beef and vegetables to a Dutch oven, stir in 1 teaspoon salt, tuck in thyme and bay leaf, pour stock and remaining wine so liquid does not quite cover the ingredients, cover and cook for 2 hours then check every 15 minutes until meat falls apart easily with a fork.
11. Once the meat is cooked, stir in the reserved bacon and mushrooms and continue cooking on high or simmering until the mushrooms are warmed through, about 10 minutes.
12. Serve in bowls over noodles or with crusty bread on the side and sprinkle with parsley before serving.
