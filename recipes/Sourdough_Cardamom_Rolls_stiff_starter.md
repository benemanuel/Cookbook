# Sourdough Cardamom Rolls

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 150g stiff starter (≈50% hydration, cold is fine)
- 400g bread flour
- 50g rustic / higher extraction flour
- 280g whole milk (room temp)
- 100g sugar
- 1 egg
- 100g soft butter
- 8g salt
- 3g freshly ground cardamom

### Filling
- 100g soft butter
- 100g sugar
- 4g freshly ground cardamom
- Pinch salt

### Optional Rescue
- 3g instant yeast
- 20g warm milk (≈35°C)

### Optional Syrup
- 100g sugar
- 100g water
- 2g cardamom

## Instructions

1. Mix milk, starter (cut small), and all flour until no dry flour remains and rest for 30 minutes.
2. Add egg, sugar, and cardamom to the dough and mix to combine.
3. Add salt and knead for 5 minutes.
4. Add butter gradually and knead until smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky but not sticky, for a total kneading time of 10–12 minutes.
5. Let the dough rise at 22–23°C for 5–7 hours until 60–70% risen with a smoother surface and slight gas visible, giving one fold after 2–3 hours.
6. If fermentation stalls, dissolve instant yeast in warm milk and gently knead into dough, then ferment at 26–27°C until 60–70% risen.
7. Roll dough to a rectangle approximately 5mm thick.
8. Spread filling over the dough and fold in thirds (letter fold).
9. Roll slightly thinner and cut strips, then twist into knots.
10. Place rolls in a pan with space between them and let sit at room temperature for 45–60 minutes until slight puffing begins.
11. Refrigerate the rolls for 8–14 hours at 4°C for the recommended overnight final proof.
12. Remove rolls from refrigerator and let sit at room temperature for 1–2 hours until visibly puffy and airy.
13. Preheat oven to 190°C and bake for 18–25 minutes depending on pan size, until internal temperature reaches 93–94°C.
14. If tops darken early, tent loosely with foil and reduce oven temperature to 170–175°C, then continue baking until internal temperature reaches 93–94°C.
15. Optional: boil sugar, water, and cardamom together, then brush the syrup immediately after baking.
