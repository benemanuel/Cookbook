# Grilled Lamb & Fruit Salad with Feta and Mint-Lime Dressing

## Ingredients

- 480ml romaine lettuce, chopped
- 240ml cherry tomatoes, halved
- 240ml cantaloupe, cubed
- 240ml mango, cubed
- 1 banana, cubed
- 200 g lamb chops, chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Touch of cinnamon for lamb
- 100 g feta cheese, crumbled
- 45ml extra virgin olive oil
- 15ml fresh lime juice
- 5ml honey
- 5ml Dijon mustard
- 15ml fresh mint, finely chopped
- Pinch of chili flakes or Aleppo pepper
- 1ml cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Grill lamb chops with a sprinkle of cinnamon, salt, and pepper until cooked to your liking.
2. Let the lamb rest for a few minutes, then chop into bite-size pieces.
3. In a small jar or bowl, whisk together olive oil, lime juice, honey, mustard, mint, chili flakes, salt, and black pepper until smooth and emulsified.
4. In a large bowl or platter, arrange the romaine, cherry tomatoes, cantaloupe, mango, and banana.
5. Top with the grilled lamb and sprinkle over the feta cheese.
6. Drizzle with the mint-lime dressing just before serving and toss gently if desired, or serve with dressing on the side.
