# Chuck Roast

*Source: sundaysuppermovement.com*

## Ingredients

- One chuck roast with good marbling
- Unsalted butter
- Vegetable oil
- One large yellow onion, cut into large wedges
- Fresh garlic cloves
- Whole carrots, cut into 2-inch pieces
- Celery, cut into 2-inch pieces
- Potatoes (Yukon Gold or Russet), cut into 2-inch chunks
- Fresh rosemary sprig
- Two bay leaves
- Two tablespoons tomato paste
- One cup red wine
- Two cups beef stock
- Kosher salt or sea salt flakes
- Black pepper

## Instructions

1. Pat the beef dry and season it generously with salt and pepper on all sides.
2. Heat butter and vegetable oil in a large pot over medium-high heat.
3. Sear the chuck roast on all sides until browned, then remove and set aside.
4. Add the onion, garlic cloves, carrots, and celery to the pot and cook until fragrant.
5. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for one minute.
6. Deglaze the pot with red wine, scraping up any browned bits.
7. Return the roast to the pot and add the beef stock, bay leaves, and rosemary.
8. Braise low and slow until the roast is very tender and falls apart easily.
9. Add the potatoes during the final portion of cooking until they are tender.
10. Remove the bay leaves and rosemary sprig before serving.
