# Fire Cider

## Ingredients

- 18 cm piece of freshly peeled and grated ginger root
- 18 cm piece of freshly grated horseradish root
- 18 cm piece of freshly grated turmeric root
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 10 cloves or 1 head of garlic, crushed or chopped
- 2 organic jalapeño peppers, chopped
- 1 whole lemon with zest and juice
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne powder
- 1 cinnamon stick
- A few allspice berries
- A few whole cloves
- 1 whole orange with zest and juice
- 2 tablespoons chopped rosemary and/or thyme and/or oregano and/or sage leaves
- 1/2 teaspoon black peppercorns
- Organic unfiltered apple cider vinegar
- 1/4 cup raw honey, or to taste

## Instructions

1. Add ginger, horseradish, onion, garlic, jalapeño peppers, lemon zest and juice, rosemary, turmeric, cayenne powder, and about 25 cranks of fresh black pepper into a food processor and pulse until all vegetables are in small, semi-uniform pieces similar to the texture of sauerkraut.
2. Put all the processed ingredients into a quart-sized glass jar.
3. Pour apple cider vinegar into the jar until all ingredients are fully covered and the vinegar reaches the top of the jar, ensuring all ingredients are submerged to prevent spoilage.
4. Use a piece of natural parchment paper under the lid to keep the vinegar from touching the metal, or use a plastic lid if available.
5. Shake the jar to combine all ingredients and store in a dark, cool place for 4-6 weeks, shaking the jar for a few seconds every day.
6. After one month, use a mesh strainer or cheesecloth to strain out the solids, pouring the vinegar into a clean jar and squeezing out as much liquid as possible.
7. Add honey to the liquid and stir until incorporated.
8. Taste the fire cider and add more honey if needed until desired sweetness is reached.
9. Store in a sealed container in the refrigerator or in a cold, dark place.
10. Drink 1-2 tablespoons when needed.
