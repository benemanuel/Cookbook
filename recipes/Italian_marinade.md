# Italian Marinade

*Source: Unknown*

## Ingredients

- 1 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 3/4 cup red wine vinegar
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1/4 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
- 4 cloves garlic, peeled, smashed and roughly chopped
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tablespoon thyme leaves
- 1 tablespoon oregano leaves
- 1 tablespoon basil leaves, rolled and chopped into chiffonade
- 1 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes, or to taste

## Instructions

1. Combine the olive oil, red wine vinegar, lemon zest, lemon juice, garlic, bay leaf, thyme, oregano, basil, sugar, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes in a bowl and whisk until well blended.
