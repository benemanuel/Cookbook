# White House Pecan Pie

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (115g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup dark corn syrup
- ½ tsp salt
- 1½ tsp vanilla extract
- 3 large eggs
- 2 cups chopped pecans
- 1 unbaked 9-inch pastry shell

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a large bowl, beat softened butter until creamy.
3. Add sugar and beat until light and fluffy.
4. Mix in dark corn syrup, salt, and vanilla extract.
5. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
6. Fold in the chopped pecans.
7. Pour the filling into the unbaked pastry shell.
8. Bake for 40–50 minutes, until the center is set and the top is golden, with a toothpick coming out mostly clean.
9. Cool completely before slicing.
