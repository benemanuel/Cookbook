# Saffron Vanilla Donuts

*Source: Instagram*

## Ingredients

- 60g sugar
- 23g cornstarch
- 4 egg yolks
- Pinch of salt
- 360g whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean pod
- 25g unsalted butter
- 150g heavy whipping cream, cold
- 0.25g saffron
- 10g sugar
- 30g whole milk, hot
- 445g bread flour
- 55g sugar
- 7g salt
- 185g whole milk, at 27C
- 1 whole egg plus 1 yolk
- 12g instant dry yeast
- 70g unsalted butter, softened and cubed
- Frying oil
- Extra sugar

## Instructions

1. Whisk together egg yolks, sugar, cornstarch, and salt for the diplomat cream.
2. Cut the vanilla pod in half and scrape off the beans.
3. Add milk and vanilla to a pot and heat over medium heat until tiny bubbles form.
4. Remove from heat and slowly pour the hot milk over the egg mixture a little at a time while whisking.
5. Place a strainer over the pot and strain everything back into the pot.
6. Heat over medium heat while constantly whisking until the mixture thickens.
7. Remove from heat and mix in butter.
8. Transfer to a bowl and cover with plastic wrap that touches the surface of the pastry cream, then refrigerate for at least an hour.
9. Add saffron threads to a mortar and pestle and grind until they break down.
10. Add 10g of sugar and continue grinding into a fine powder.
11. Add 30g of hot milk to the saffron mixture and stir, then rest for 15 minutes.
12. Add all dough ingredients except butter to the bowl of a stand mixer and knead on low for 10 minutes.
13. Add butter one cube at a time to the dough.
14. Knead for 10-20 minutes on low-medium until the dough passes the window-pane test.
15. Proof the dough for about 90 minutes.
16. Divide the dough into 15 pieces of 60g each and roll into balls.
17. Arrange the dough balls on parchment paper squares and proof for 30 minutes.
18. Fry the donuts at 340F/170C for 2 minutes on each side.
19. Transfer fried donuts to paper towels and toss in sugar.
20. Whisk 150g of cold whipping cream to soft peaks and combine with the cooled pastry cream.
21. Fill the donuts with the diplomat cream and serve.
