# Overnight Baked Blueberry Muffin Oatmeal

*Source: abeautifulmess.com*

## Ingredients

- 1¾ cups old-fashioned oats
- 60ml sugar
- 5ml baking powder
- 1ml salt
- 1½ cups milk
- 1 egg
- 30ml melted and cooled butter
- 5ml vanilla extract
- 240ml blueberries
- 60ml cold butter
- 60ml sugar
- 60ml all-purpose flour

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, stir together the oats, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. In a smaller bowl, stir together the milk, egg, melted and cooled butter, and vanilla extract.
3. Combine the wet ingredients with the dry in the large bowl and stir to combine, then stir in the blueberries.
4. Add this mixture to a pie pan, cover and store overnight in the refrigerator.
5. In a small bowl, cut together the crumb topping ingredients until the mixture is crumbly like small peas you can press together, then store overnight or make in the morning.
6. Top the oatmeal with the crumb topping and bake at 177°C for 30-35 minutes, or set under the broiler for a minute or two if the crumb topping doesn't look golden brown and slightly crispy.
