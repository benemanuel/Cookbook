# Old Fashioned Potato Knishes

*Source: kosherfromjerusalem.com*

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 pounds potatoes
- 1 large onion
- 2 tablespoons oil for sautéing
- 1 egg for egg wash

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F/180°C.
2. Whisk together the flour and salt in a medium bowl and make a well in the center.
3. Add the egg, oil, vinegar, and water to the well and stir together until all ingredients are incorporated.
4. Knead the dough in the bowl about six times, pressing and turning to ensure all ingredients are properly mixed.
5. Cover the bowl and let the dough rest for one hour at room temperature.
6. Boil the potatoes with a pinch of salt until very soft.
7. Transfer the potatoes to a bowl to cool.
8. Sauté the onions in oil until medium brown and add to the potatoes.
9. Mash the potatoes and onions together and add one teaspoon of salt, then stir well.
10. Beat the egg and pour half of it into the cooled potato filling, reserving the other half for brushing.
11. Divide the rested dough into 4 parts.
12. Roll out each portion of dough on floured parchment paper into a rectangle.
13. Place a layer of potato filling along the bottom short side of the dough, leaving a margin.
14. Fold the dough over the filling once and then use the parchment paper to lift and gently roll the dough over itself like a jelly roll.
15. Pinch the dough at intervals to create 4-5 knishes from each rectangle and twist at the pinches.
16. Cut at each twist and pinch the ends shut.
17. Place the knishes on their bottoms and gently poke the top to help seal and create a small indentation.
18. Arrange the knishes on parchment paper on a rimmed baking sheet.
19. Brush the knishes with the remaining egg mixture.
20. Bake for 30-40 minutes until golden brown.
21. Serve with mustard.
