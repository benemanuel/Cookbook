# Venetian Charoset

*Source: Joan Nathan*

## Ingredients

- 360ml chestnut paste
- 284g dates, chopped
- 340g figs, chopped
- 30ml poppy seeds
- 120ml chopped walnuts
- 120ml chopped almonds
- 120ml pine nuts
- grated rind of 1 orange
- 120ml white raisins
- 60ml chopped dried apricot
- 120ml brandy
- honey to bind

## Instructions

1. Combine all the ingredients, adding just enough brandy and honey to make the mixture bind.
