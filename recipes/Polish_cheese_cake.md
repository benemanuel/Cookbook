# Polish Cheese Cake

*Source: null*

## Ingredients

- 175 grams white sugar
- 110 grams butter
- 1 egg
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 375 grams flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 500 grams 9% white cheese
- 100 grams white sugar
- 1 egg
- 110 grams brown sugar
- 42 grams flour
- 80 grams butter
- 65 grams pecans

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 175 degrees C.
2. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
3. Beat 175 grams white sugar, 110 grams butter, and 1 egg in a bowl until smooth.
4. Add sour cream and vanilla extract, then mix well.
5. Stir in 375 grams flour, baking soda, and baking powder until mixture comes together in a sticky dough.
6. Spread half the dough evenly into the prepared baking dish.
7. Beat white cheese, 100 grams white sugar, and 1 egg in another bowl until smooth.
8. Spoon the cheese mixture into the baking dish over the dough.
9. Drop remaining half of dough by spoonfuls over the cheese mixture.
10. Mix pecans, brown sugar, 42 grams flour, and 80 grams butter in a bowl until mixture resembles a coarse crumble.
11. Sprinkle the crumble mixture over the dough.
12. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, about 45 minutes.
