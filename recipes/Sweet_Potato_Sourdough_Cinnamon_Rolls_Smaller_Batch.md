# Sweet Potato Sourdough Cinnamon Rolls — Smaller Batch

## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 120 g sweet potato
- 30 g milk
- 21 g flour
- 105 g milk
- 1 egg
- 150 g stiff sourdough starter
- 400 g bread flour
- 80 g sugar
- 48 g soft butter
- 4 g salt
- 35–50 ml milk

**Filling**
- 145 g butter
- 115 g demerara sugar
- 30ml pumpkin spice mix
  - 1¼ tsp cinnamon
  - 4ml nutmeg
  - 4ml ginger
  - 4ml cloves

**Frosting**
- 95 g cream cheese
- 95 g mascarpone
- 12 g butter
- 25 g powdered sugar
- 2ml vanilla

## Instructions

1. Blend sweet potato with 30 g milk until smooth.
2. Prepare tangzhong by combining 21 g flour with 105 g milk, heating until thickened, then cool.
3. Combine blended sweet potato, cooled tangzhong, egg, and stiff sourdough starter in a mixing bowl.
4. Add bread flour, sugar, soft butter, salt, and 35–50 ml milk, starting with the lower amount.
5. Mix until dough is soft, elastic, and slightly tacky, being conservative with milk as sweet potato varies.
6. Develop gluten fully after butter incorporation until the dough reaches proper consistency.
7. Mix filling ingredients together, combining butter, demerara sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and cloves.
8. Roll out proofed dough, spread filling evenly, roll into a log, and cut into 8-12 small rolls.
9. Proof rolls until puffy, 3–5 hours.
10. Bake at 175°C for 25–30 minutes, until internal 85°C.
11. Frost the warm rolls with the cream cheese & mascarpone frosting mixture.
