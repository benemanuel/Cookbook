# Beef Pot Pie

*Source: gooddinnermom.com*

## Ingredients

- 907g boneless beef chuck-eye roast, trimmed and cut into ¾ inch pieces
- Salt and pepper
- 45ml olive oil or avocado oil, divided
- 113g baby bella mushrooms, trimmed and quartered
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 carrots, peeled and cut into ½ inch pieces
- 30ml tomato paste
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 120ml dry red wine or 120ml water
- 45ml all-purpose flour
- 480ml beef broth
- 15ml soy sauce
- 15ml Worcestershire sauce
- 1 bay leaf
- 240ml frozen peas
- 8ml chopped fresh thyme, divided
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- 1 9-inch store-bought pie dough round

## Instructions

1. Adjust oven rack to the lower-middle position and heat oven to 177°C.
2. Pat beef cubes dry with paper towels and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Heat 22ml oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat and add half of the beef, cooking until well browned on all sides, about 7 minutes.
4. Transfer browned beef to a bowl or plate and add the remaining uncooked beef to the same bowl.
5. Reduce heat to medium, add the remaining 22ml oil to the pot, and stir in mushrooms, onion, and carrots, cooking until lightly browned, about 5 minutes.
6. Add tomato paste and garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
7. Stir in wine or water and cook until mostly evaporated, about 2 minutes.
8. Sprinkle flour over the vegetables and stir for 1 minute.
9. Slowly add broth, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, and bay leaf, scraping the bottom of the pot to release flavor.
10. Return all beef to the pot, bring to a simmer, cover, and transfer to the oven.
11. Bake until beef is tender, about 1 hour 15 minutes, testing a piece and adding 60ml water and returning to oven for 20–30 minutes if still chewy.
12. Remove from oven and increase oven temperature to 204°C.
13. If filling looks dry, transfer meat to a bowl, add ¼–120ml water to the pot, and scrape up browned bits to create a rich sauce, then stir back into the meat mixture.
14. Discard bay leaf and stir in thyme and peas, then taste and season with more salt and pepper as needed.
15. Spray a 9-inch deep-dish pie plate with cooking spray and add beef filling.
16. Brush the rim of the dish with beaten egg, place pie dough over the top so it slightly overhangs the edges, fold excess dough inward, and crimp edges with your fingers.
17. Cut a ½-inch hole in the center of the crust and cut six small slits around it to vent steam.
18. Brush the crust with remaining egg and sprinkle lightly with salt, pepper, and thyme.
19. Place pie on a rimmed baking sheet and bake until golden brown, 20–30 minutes.
20. Let pie cool on a rack for 15 minutes before serving.
