# Gin & Basil Smash

## Ingredients

- 45ml Ford's gin
- 30ml lemon juice
- 30ml simple syrup
- 5-8 basil leaves
- a couple drops of saline solution

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients into a shaker.
2. Muddle the basil leaves.
3. Shake with ice.
4. Double strain into a glass with a large rock.
