# Fried Cheese Balls From Crete

*Source: lemonandolives.com*

## Ingredients

- 15 ounces (425g) ricotta cheese
- 1 egg
- 4 tablespoons flour, plus extra for rolling
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 2 tablespoons dried parsley
- 1/2 teaspoon thyme
- salt
- pepper
- 2/3 cup oil for frying

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients except oil and mix well.
2. Place the mixture in the refrigerator to set for about 30-45 minutes.
3. Add oil to a pan and heat over medium heat.
4. Once the oil is hot, remove the cheese mixture from the refrigerator.
5. Add some flour to a plate for easy rolling.
6. Take a golf ball-sized amount of cheese mixture, place it in the flour, roll it around to coat and form a ball.
7. Shake off excess flour and place the ball in the pan.
8. Fry for 3-4 minutes on each side or until golden brown.
9. Remove and place on a paper towel.
10. Repeat with the remaining cheese mixture.
11. Serve warm.
