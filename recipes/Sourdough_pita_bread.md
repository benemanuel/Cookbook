# Sourdough Pita Bread

## Ingredients

- 500 g all-purpose flour
- 300 g water
- 100 g sourdough starter
- 30 g olive oil
- 30 g sugar
- 10 g salt

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients together.
2. Perform stretch and fold every 30 minutes for 4 rounds.
3. Rest the dough for 2 hours.
4. Divide the dough into 100 g portions.
5. Use a rolling pin to flatten each portion.
6. Rest the flattened dough for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
7. Pan bake on medium-low heat for 3 minutes until the pita puffs up.
8. Remove from pan and cover with kitchen towels to prevent drying out and keep the bread soft.
