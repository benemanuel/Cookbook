# Napoleon (Cream Cake)

*Source: domowe-wypieki.pl*

## Ingredients

- 300 g butter
- 300 g wheat flour
- 1 egg (size M)
- 1/2 tablespoon vinegar (about 5%)
- approximately 100 ml hot water
- 1 liter milk
- 50 g butter
- 4 egg yolks
- 2 eggs (size M)
- 200 g sugar
- 2 teaspoons vanilla sugar
- 120 g wheat flour
- 70 g potato starch
- powdered sugar for sprinkling

## Instructions

1. Prepare puff pastry by kneading 300 g butter and 100 g flour to a homogeneous mass, then place it between two pieces of food foil and flatten it by hand or roll out into a square about 2 cm thick, and refrigerate.
2. During this time, knead the dough from the remaining ingredients with enough water to make it resemble pierogi dough, then roll it out on a flour-covered surface into a circle.
3. Place the cooled butter mass from the fridge in the center of the rolled dough, fold the dough into an envelope, and glue the edges thoroughly.
4. Invert the dough with the joints facing downwards and roll out into a long rectangle approximately 1 cm thick, always rolling in one direction.
5. Fold the dough into three parts like a letter, lightly roll out just by pressing with a roller to make it thinner, wrap in foil, and place on a wooden board in the freezer for 15 minutes.
6. Remove the dough from the freezer, turn it 90 degrees, and roll it out again into an oblong rectangle, then re-fold as a letter, lightly roll out, wrap in foil, and place in the freezer for 15 minutes.
7. Repeat the folding and freezing process two more times, each time placing the dough in the freezer for 15 minutes.
8. Cut the finished dough in half, wrap one half in foil, and refrigerate.
9. Roll out the other half of the dough on a flour-covered countertop into a large rectangular cake, transfer it to a large flat oven tray lined with baking paper, trim the edges with a knife, and prick with a fork.
10. Bake in a preheated oven at 220°C with the top-down heater for about 15 minutes until golden brown, opening the oven after a few minutes to prick any bulges with a fork.
11. Bake the second cake in the same way and allow both cakes to cool completely.
12. Cut one cake intended for the top with a serrated knife into squares of the desired size for individual portions.
13. Prepare the pudding mass by boiling approximately 750 ml of milk with butter, sugar, and vanilla sugar, then mix the remaining milk with eggs, yolks, flour, and starch and add to the boiling milk while stirring to prevent lumps from forming, and cook until thick.
14. Allow the pudding to cool slightly while stirring briefly every now and then, then pour it still slightly warm onto the first puff pastry cake, wrapping the bottom with aluminum foil rolled several times to prevent the pudding from flowing out.
15. Arrange the cut squares on top of the pudding mass.
16. Place the cake in the refrigerator for at least a few hours.
17. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and cut with a sharp serrated knife.
