# Nutella Brownies

## Ingredients

- 400 g Nutella
- 40 g flour
- 10 g cocoa powder
- 1 whole egg plus 1 egg yolk
- 30 g hot milk

## Instructions

1. Mix the Nutella with the eggs until combined.
2. Mix in the flour and cocoa powder.
3. Stir in the milk.
4. Pour batter into a baking pan lined with parchment paper.
5. Bake for approximately 18 minutes at 160°C.
