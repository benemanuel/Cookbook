# Healthy Homemade Nutella

## Ingredients

- 2 cups raw hazelnuts
- 1 1/2 tbsp pure vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup cocoa powder
- 1/4 cup plus 2 tbsp pure maple syrup, xylitol, or unrefined sugar
- Pinch stevia or 1 tbsp additional sugar
- 1/4 tsp plus 1/8 tsp salt
- 2 tsp oil for extra smoothness (optional)
- 1/2 cup milk of choice

## Instructions

1. Roast hazelnuts for 6-8 minutes at 400°F.
2. Rub the roasted hazelnuts together in a paper towel to remove the skins.
3. In a Vitamix or food processor, blend the nuts until they have turned to butter.
4. Add all other ingredients and blend for a long time until the mixture is smooth like Nutella.
