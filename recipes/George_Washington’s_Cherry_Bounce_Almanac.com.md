# George Washington’s Cherry Bounce

*Source: almanac.com*

## Ingredients

- 10 to 11 pounds fresh sour or tart cherries, pitted
- 4 cups brandy
- 3 cups sugar, plus more as needed
- 2 cinnamon sticks, broken into pieces
- 2 to 3 cloves
- 1 (1/4-inch) piece fresh whole nutmeg

## Instructions

1. Note: The recipe lists fresh cherries, but you can also buy jars of preserved cherries, specifically&nbsp;3 jars (1 pound, 9 ounces),&nbsp;preferably Morello cherries.
2. Or if jarred cherries are&nbsp;not available, thawed pie cherries can be substituted.Mash cherries to extract as much juice as possible.
3. Strain the juice through a large fine-mesh strainer, pressing the fruit with a sturdy spoon.
4. You should have about 8 cups. (Keep the mashed cherries; don't throw them out!).&nbsp; Note: If using jarred cherries, drain the fruit and set the juice aside before halving and mashing the cherries.
5. Add any pressed juice to the reserved jarred juice.In a 1-gallon glass jar (or glass container) with a lid, combine the juice with the brandy and sugar.&nbsp; Stir to dissolve the sugar.
6. Cover and set aside in the refrigerator for 24 hours, occasionally stirring or carefully shaking the jar.Bring 2 cups of the juice to a simmer over medium heat.
7. Taste the sweetened juice and add more sugar if desired.
8. Stir in the cinnamon sticks, cloves, and nutmeg.Cover and simmer for five minutes; remove from heat and set aside to cool to room temperature.
9. Strain, and discard the spices.Stir the spiced juice back into the 1-gallon glass jar with the reserved sweetened juice.Cover loosely with the lid, and set aside for at least 2 weeks before serving, occasionally shaking the jar with care.Serve at room temperature in small cordial or wine glasses.
10. Store extra in fridge.
