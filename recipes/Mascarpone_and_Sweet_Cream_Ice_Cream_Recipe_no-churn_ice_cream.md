# Mascarpone and Sweet Cream Ice Cream

## Ingredients

- 1 cup mascarpone cheese
- 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- A pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Whip the heavy cream in a large bowl until soft peaks form.
2. In another bowl, whisk together the mascarpone cheese, sweetened condensed milk, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt until smooth.
3. Gently fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture, making sure not to deflate the whipped cream too much.
4. Pour the mixture into a container and cover with plastic wrap.
5. Freeze for at least 4 hours or overnight.
6. Once frozen, scoop and enjoy.
