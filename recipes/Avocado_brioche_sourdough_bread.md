# Avocado Brioche Sourdough Bread

*Source: wellandgood.com*

## Ingredients

- 10 grams sourdough starter
- 30 grams water
- 60 grams bread flour
- 300 grams bread flour
- 150 grams ripe avocado
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 100 grams sweetened condensed milk
- 1 large egg
- 30 grams milk
- 15 grams sugar (optional)
- 30 grams soft butter
- 60 grams stiff starter
- 6 grams salt
- 0.5 grams dry yeast (optional, to reduce sourness)

## Instructions

1. At 10pm, add starter to the water and whisk together, add flour, mix well, and form a ball.
2. Roll it into a tight roll, place in jar, cover with water, and let sit at room temperature 74°F to 78°F until it increases in size and starts to float on the top of the water.
3. In about 8 to 10 hours, the top of the starter will dry and the bottom will start to melt in water; use the center part of it.
4. Keeping stiff starter in the water will help to reduce its acidity and sourness of the final product.
5. At 8am on day 2, cut fresh ripe avocado and add it to the food processor along with lemon juice, sweetened condensed milk, regular milk, and an egg, then purée together.
6. If using dry yeast, add it to the food processor as well.
7. Add purée, stiff sourdough starter, and sugar to flour and mix on low speed for 2 to 3 minutes until well incorporated.
8. Add salt and mix for a couple more minutes until the dough forms a ball and seems stiff.
9. Add soft butter and mix for 10 more minutes until the dough is well incorporated and comes together.
10. Cover and let it proof for 3 to 4 hours at 76°F to 80°F.
11. During that time, perform two stretches and folds; the dough should become slightly puffy.
12. At 12pm to 1pm, transfer the dough to the fridge for cold fermentation for 8 to 9 hours.
13. At 10pm, remove the dough from the fridge.
14. Shape as desired into a loaf pan or divide into 3 or 4 pieces and braid.
15. Put a sheet of parchment paper on the bottom of your loaf pan.
16. Transfer the shaped loaf into the loaf pan.
17. Cover the dough and let it proof overnight for 8 to 9 hours at 70°F to 72°F until it doubles in volume.
18. Sprinkle some flour on top of your loaf or egg wash it.
19. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
20. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes until golden brown.
21. Enjoy your avocado brioche sourdough loaf.
