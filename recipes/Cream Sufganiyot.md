# Cream Sufganiyot

*Source: null*

## Ingredients

- 38 g active sourdough starter (white, 100% hydration)
- 38 g water
- 76 g bread flour
- 390 g bread flour or 50/50 bread/AP
- 152 g stiff levain
- 100 g eggs (~2 eggs)
- 128 g whole milk (cold)
- 75 g sugar
- 7 g salt
- 75 g unsalted butter (softened, cubed)
- 360 g whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean pod or 5ml extract
- 60 g sugar
- 23 g corn starch
- 4 egg yolks
- pinch of salt
- 25 g unsalted butter
- 2 L frying oil
- 200 g sugar for coating

## Instructions

1. Mix starter, water, and flour and cover to rise at 75-26°C (24-26°C) for 8-10 hours until tripled.
2. Mix flour, levain, eggs, milk, sugar, and salt with dough hook until just combined and autolyse for 30 minutes.
3. Mix for 5 minutes more.
4. Slowly add butter cubes and mix on medium for 15-25 minutes until the dough passes the windowpane test.
5. Perform bulk proof for 6 hours at 75-26°C with folds at 1.5 hours and 3 hours.
6. Cold-proof for 6-8 hours.
7. Whisk egg yolks, sugar, cornstarch, and salt together.
8. Halve vanilla bean and scrape the seeds.
9. Heat milk with vanilla until almost boiling.
10. Slowly pour the hot milk over the yolks while whisking constantly.
11. Strain the mixture back into the pot and heat while whisking until thick and shiny.
12. Remove from heat, add butter, and add extract if using vanilla extract instead of bean.
13. Cover pastry cream with plastic wrap touching the surface and cool, then refrigerate.
14. Divide dough into 15 pieces of 60g each, shape into rounds, and place on floured parchment squares.
15. Proof in oven with light on at approximately 26°C for 10-14 hours until doubled.
16. Heat oil to 180-185°C (356-185°C).
17. Fry each doughnut for approximately 2 minutes per side, top down first.
18. Drain on a rack with paper towels and toss in sugar while warm.
19. Stir cooled pastry cream, pierce doughnut centers, and pipe filling generously.
20. Serve the same day as frying.
