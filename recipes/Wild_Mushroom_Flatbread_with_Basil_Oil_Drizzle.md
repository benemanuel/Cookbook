# Wild Mushroom Flatbread with Basil Oil Drizzle

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup loosely packed basil leaves
- 1 clove garlic
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/4 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp ground pepper
- 1 tbsp water
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 tsp sea salt, divided
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper, divided
- 16 oz mixed wild mushrooms (cremini, oyster, shiitake), roughly chopped
- 1 large whole-grain or whole-wheat flatbread pizza crust
- 4 oz fresh mozzarella, torn into pieces
- arugula, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Place basil, garlic, olive oil, salt, pepper and water into a mini food processor and blend until smooth.
2. Preheat oven to 400°F and line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium, add onions and cook, stirring frequently, until soft and translucent, 3 to 4 minutes.
4. Reduce heat to low, season with half of the salt and pepper, and continue cooking, stirring occasionally, until onions are very soft and begin to stick to the pan, 7 to 9 minutes longer.
5. Transfer onions to a bowl and wipe out the skillet.
6. Heat remaining 1 tbsp oil on medium-high, add mushrooms and remaining salt and pepper, and cook, stirring frequently, until softened slightly, 3 to 4 minutes.
7. Place flatbread on prepared sheet and bake until it begins to brown, 5 to 7 minutes.
8. Top with mushrooms, onions and cheese and bake until cheese has melted, about 5 minutes longer.
9. Transfer flatbread to a cutting board, drizzle with basil oil, and garnish with arugula.
10. Cut flatbread into 16 squares.
