# Danish Loaf Bread - 3 Ways (Cocoa/Matcha/Ube)

*Source: The Glutton*

## Ingredients

- 180 grams bread flour
- 50 grams cake flour
- 3 grams instant yeast
- 15 grams milk powder
- 1 egg
- 90 grams cold water
- 25 grams caster sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 10 grams butter, softened
- 115 grams unsalted butter to make a 5 x 5-inch square butter insert
- 8 grams cocoa powder (Valrhona) for cocoa version
- 6 grams matcha powder (Uji) for matcha version
- 6 grams ube/purple yam powder for ube version

## Instructions

1. Knead dough until it passes a window pane test, about 20 to 30 minutes, using manual kneading, stand mixer, or bread machine
2. Chill dough in fridge overnight to proof
3. Take out dough from fridge and form into a smooth, round ball
4. Cover with tea towel and let rest for 20 minutes
5. Cut butter into pieces and lay them down side by side on parchment paper
6. Sprinkle cocoa/matcha/ube powder on both sides of butter, pound with rolling pin to soften, roll evenly and shape into a 5-inch square
7. Chill butter insert overnight before using
8. Roll out dough into 8 x 8-inch square, place butter at an angle like a diamond
9. Roll out each flap/side into about 4-inch length and fold each flap to encase butter within dough, pressing edges to seal
10. Sprinkle flour on work surface and top of dough to prevent sticking
11. Press rolling pin up and down, creating ridges on top of dough
12. Roll out dough into 8 x 15-inch rectangle and do 3-fold by folding one short side at two-thirds of the rectangle length, then the other short side over it making 3 layers
13. Rotate dough 90 degrees with opening on the right side, creating a 5 x 8-inch rectangle
14. Repeat the turn 3 times total, wrapping dough in plastic wrap and freezing for 20 minutes after each turn
15. Fluff/lift dough often as you roll to prevent sticking, sprinkling flour as needed and brushing off excess before folding
16. Freeze dough for 20 minutes after the 3rd turn
17. Lightly grease an 8x4-1/2x4-1/4 inch non-stick pullman tin
18. Roll out dough into 6 x 16-inch rectangle
19. Cut dough into 3 equal strips and cut strips into 3 equal pieces, not cutting the top part through
20. Do a 3-strand braid, making sure the layered part is exposed at the top
21. Turn the braid upside down and fold into 3 layers with the top of braid in the middle
22. Place the stacked braid middle side up in the prepared pullman pan and spray with water to prevent drying
23. Cover pan with plastic wrap or tea towel, place on baking sheet and let proof until it fills about 90% of tin or nearly to the rim, 4 to 4 1/2 hours depending on ambient temperature
24. Preheat oven to 210°C
25. Put the cover on the pullman tin and let proof for another 10 to 20 minutes
26. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes until crust is golden brown
27. Take danish loaf immediately out of tin and place on wire rack to cool completely before slicing
