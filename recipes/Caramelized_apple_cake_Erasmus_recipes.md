# Caramelized Apple Cake

*Source: Erasmus recipes*

## Ingredients

- 5 green apples
- 250g butter
- 250g white sugar
- 5 eggs
- 250g flour
- 5ml cinnamon
- 5ml baking powder

## Instructions

1. Peel all five apples and clean them by removing the seeds, then dice the apples.
2. Put 50g of white sugar in a dry pan and let it caramelize, watching carefully so it does not burn.
3. When the sugar dissolves completely, add the diced apples and stir well, ensuring they are coated with caramelized sugar and turn golden brown, but do not cook them completely.
4. Finish with some butter and cinnamon on top of the caramelized apples for extra depth of flavor.
5. Soften the butter in the microwave for a couple of minutes.
6. Add 200g of white sugar and mix until homogeneous and creamy.
7. Add five whole eggs and mix well until everything is combined, ensuring eggs are at room temperature.
8. Add the flour, cinnamon, and baking powder and mix well until everything is combined.
9. Add the caramelized apples to the cake mix once they have cooled down and give a final good mix.
10. Cover an 18-20cm baking tin generously with butter so the cake will not stick.
11. Bake in a preheated oven at 170°C until the surface becomes golden brown and a knife inserted comes out clean, approximately 45 minutes.
