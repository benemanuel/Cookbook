# Murder Cookies

## Ingredients

- 300g brown sugar
- 190g butter
- 170g molasses
- 1 egg
- 400g plain flour
- 12ml bicarbonate of soda
- 8ml cinnamon
- 5ml mace
- 60 ml milk

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 175°C fan.
2. Cream together brown sugar and butter.
3. Add molasses and egg to the mixture.
4. Combine plain flour, bicarbonate of soda, cinnamon, and mace in a separate bowl.
5. Mix dry ingredients into the wet mixture alternately with milk.
6. Use a dessert spoon to portion cookies onto a baking tray.
7. Bake for 10 to 11 minutes until golden.
