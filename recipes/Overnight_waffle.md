# Overnight waffle

## Ingredients

### Overnight Sponge
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sourdough starter (unfed/discard)
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- 2 cups buttermilk

### Morning Batter
- All of the overnight sponge
- 2 large eggs
- ¼ cup vegetable oil or 4 Tbsp melted butter
- ¾ tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda

## Instructions

1. The night before: combine flour, sourdough starter, sugar, and buttermilk in a large bowl. Stir until smooth. Cover loosely and let rest at room temperature overnight (8–12 hours).
2. In the morning, the sponge will be bubbly and have expanded. Whisk together eggs, oil or butter, salt, and baking soda in a small bowl.
3. Add the egg mixture to the sponge and stir gently until just combined. The batter will bubble as the baking soda reacts.
4. For pancakes: heat a griddle over medium heat and lightly grease. Pour batter in ¼-cup portions. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and edges look set, about 2–3 minutes. Flip and cook 1–2 minutes more.
5. For waffles: pour batter into a preheated, greased waffle iron. Cook according to manufacturer's instructions until golden and crisp.
6. Serve immediately. Keep finished pancakes or waffles warm in a 200°F oven while cooking remaining batches.
