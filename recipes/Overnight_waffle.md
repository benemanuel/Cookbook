# Overnight Waffle

## Ingredients

- 480ml all-purpose flour
- 240ml sourdough starter (unfed/discard)
- 30ml sugar
- 480ml buttermilk
- 2 large eggs
- 60ml vegetable oil or 60ml melted butter
- 4ml salt
- 5ml baking soda

## Instructions

1. The night before, combine flour, sourdough starter, sugar, and buttermilk in a large bowl and stir until smooth.
2. Cover the bowl loosely and let rest at room temperature overnight for 8–12 hours.
3. In the morning, whisk together eggs, oil or butter, salt, and baking soda in a small bowl.
4. Add the egg mixture to the sponge and stir gently until just combined.
5. For waffles, pour batter into a preheated, greased waffle iron and cook according to manufacturer's instructions until golden and crisp.
6. Serve immediately and keep finished waffles warm in a 93°C oven while cooking remaining batches.
