# Himmas Kassa

## Ingredients

- 240ml boiled chickpeas
- 30ml tahini
- 30ml water
- 30ml wine vinegar
- 60ml finely ground walnut
- 30ml lemon juice
- 5ml wine vinegar
- 120ml chopped parsley
- 60ml chopped mint
- 45ml olive oil
- 1ml caraway
- 1ml coriander
- 1ml black pepper
- 1ml ginger
- 1ml cinnamon
- 2ml salt
- Olive oil for garnish
- Olives for garnish
- Chopped pistachios for garnish
- Flatbread for serving

## Instructions

1. In a mixing bowl, mash the boiled chickpeas until they form a coarse paste.
2. Stir in the tahini mixture and the walnut mixture until combined.
3. Fold in the parsley, mint, olive oil, and spices, then taste and adjust salt as needed.
4. Transfer to a shallow serving dish and garnish with olive oil, olives, and pistachios.
5. Serve with flatbread.
