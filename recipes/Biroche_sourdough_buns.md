# Sourdough Brioche Burger Buns

Perfectly soft, fluffy, and sturdy enough to hold a juicy burger. Rich, flavorful, and ideal as hamburger buns as well as sandwich buns. Makes 12 buns. Prep: 1 hr | Cook: 15 mins | Proofing: 1 day 4.5 hrs.

## Ingredients

### Levain
- 30 g active sourdough starter
- 40 g all-purpose flour
- 40 g whole milk, cold from the fridge
- 10 g sugar

### Tangzhong
- 140 g whole milk
- 35 g all-purpose flour

### Main Dough
- 460 g bread flour
- 30 g sugar
- 250 g eggs (approximately 5 eggs)
- all the tangzhong
- all the levain
- 12 g salt
- 150 g unsalted butter, softened, in cubes

### Egg Wash
- 1 egg
- splash of water

## Instructions

### Levain
1. Add all the ingredients to a small mixing bowl and mix until just combined. Cover and let rise in a warm spot (78-80°F/26-27°C) for four hours, or until doubled in size.

### Tangzhong
1. Add milk and flour to a small pot. Heat over medium heat while stirring with a whisk until thickened and the mixture reaches 150°F/65°C.
2. Transfer to a clean bowl. Cover with plastic wrap touching the entire surface. Cool to room temperature before continuing.

### Main Dough
1. Add all ingredients **except salt and butter** (including levain and tangzhong) to the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook. Mix until just combined. Rest/autolyse for 30 minutes.
2. Add the salt and knead on medium speed for 5–10 minutes.
3. Slowly add the cubed softened butter one cube at a time. Once all butter is added, scrape down the sides and continue mixing at medium-high speed until the dough passes the windowpane test (10–25 minutes depending on your mixer).
4. Transfer to a clean, lightly oiled bowl and proof for 3 hours at 75°F/24°C.
5. Transfer to the fridge and cold-proof for 8–15 hours.

### Shape
1. Line two baking trays with parchment paper.
2. Take the dough out of the fridge and divide into 12 equal pieces (~90 g each).
3. Roll each piece into a seamless round and arrange on the trays with space between them. Cover lightly with plastic wrap.
4. Proof for 6–10 hours at 75-78°F/24-26°C, until doubled in size.

### Bake
1. Preheat oven to 360°F/180°C.
2. Brush the tops with egg wash.
3. Bake for 15–20 minutes until golden brown. Cool completely on a rack before slicing.
