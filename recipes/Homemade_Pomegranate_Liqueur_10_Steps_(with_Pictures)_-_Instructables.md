#  Pomegranate Liqueur

*Source: Instructables*

## Ingredients

- Roughly six pomegranates, depending on their size
- Peel of 1 lemon (Meyer preferred)
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 720ml vodka
- 360ml white sugar
- 180ml water

## Instructions

1. Cut the pomegranate in half vertically and fill a large bowl with water.
2. Turn the pomegranate half seed-side down and push on the center with your thumbs to turn the half inside-out, working under water to minimize juice spraying.
3. Allow the membrane, flesh, and pith to float to the top while seeds fall to the bottom, then rinse and agitate to free any extra membrane.
4. Place a fine mesh metal strainer in a bowl and press pomegranate seed pods down against the strainer with a spoon to crush the pods and extract the juice, reserving both seeds and juice.
5. Using a vegetable peeler or paring knife, carefully peel the lemon while avoiding the white pith to preserve the oils in the peel.
6. Sterilize a large glass jar in boiling water or a dishwasher rinse cycle, then add the pomegranate juice and seeds, lemon peel, and cinnamon stick.
7. Pour in the vodka, wipe the rim clean, and seal the jar.
8. Store the jar in a cool, dark location and agitate daily with a good swirl for two to three weeks.
9. Carefully strain the liquid through several layers of moistened cheesecloth and gently wring to extract all liquid, then discard the rind, pulp, and spices.
10. Return the strained liquid to the jar.
11. Gently heat the sugar and water on medium-low to create a simple syrup, stirring only until the sugar is dissolved, then allow it to cool completely.
12. Add the cooled simple syrup to the jar with the strained liquid.
13. Store the jar in a cool, dark, and dry location, agitating every few days, and allow to age for at least three more weeks, ideally four to five weeks.
14. Sterilize glass bottles, a funnel, and any transfer utensils in a boiling water bath or dishwasher sterilizing rinse.
15. Carefully scoop the liqueur into a glass measuring cup, avoiding any sediment at the bottom.
16. Pour the liqueur into sterilized bottles through a funnel, wipe the lids, and seal quickly.
17. Serve chilled straight up, mix one-to-one with vodka for a pomegranate martini, or add an ounce to champagne for a festive drink.
