# Sourdough Stiff Babka (50% Hydration Starter)

Yield: 1 Large Loaf | Prep Time: 24 Hours | Bake Time: 45 Minutes

## Ingredients

### 1. The Sweet Stiff Levain

- 75g active 50% hydration sourdough starter
- 105g all-purpose flour
- 55g water
- 20g sugar

### 2. The Final Dough

- 375g bread flour
- 105g milk (lukewarm)
- 80g water (lukewarm)
- 2 large eggs (room temperature)
- 50g sugar
- 8g fine sea salt
- 85g unsalted butter (softened to room temperature)

### 3. The Chocolate Filling

- 95g semi-dark chocolate (chopped)
- 70g unsalted butter
- 65g powdered sugar
- 18g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 pinch salt

### 4. The Sugar Syrup (Optional Finisher)

- 50g water
- 50g sugar

## Instructions

### Step 1: Build the Levain

1. Combine the 50% starter, flour, water, and sugar in a small bowl.
2. Knead briefly by hand until smooth. It will be dense like a stiff dough.
3. Cover tightly and let ferment at room temperature (23°C–26°C) for 10–12 hours, or until it triples in volume and dome peaks.

### Step 2: Mix and Knead the Dough

1. Tear the active levain into small pieces and place them into a stand mixer bowl.
2. Add the bread flour, milk, water, eggs, sugar, and salt.
3. Mix on low speed with the dough hook attachment for 5 minutes until a cohesive dough forms. Let it rest for 10 minutes.
4. Turn the mixer to medium-low speed. Add the softened butter one tablespoon at a time, letting each piece absorb before adding the next.
5. Knead for 10–15 minutes until the dough pulls away from the sides, clears the bottom of the bowl, and passes the windowpane test.

### Step 3: Bulk Fermentation & Cold Proof

1. Place the dough into a lightly greased bowl and cover it.
2. Let it rise at a warm room temperature for 6–8 hours until puffy.
3. Transfer the covered bowl to the refrigerator for 12–16 hours overnight. This chills the butter and makes shaping much easier.

### Step 4: Make the Filling & Shape

1. Melt the chocolate and butter together in a double boiler or microwave. Stir in the powdered sugar, cocoa powder, and salt until smooth. Let it cool slightly to a spreadable consistency.
2. Roll the chilled dough on a lightly floured surface into a 30×40cm rectangle.
3. Spread the chocolate filling evenly across the dough, leaving a 1cm border.
4. Roll the dough into a tight log, starting from the long side.
5. Cut the log completely in half lengthwise using a sharp knife to expose the inner layers.
6. Twist the two strands over each other to form a braid. Tuck the ends under and lift it into a greased 23×13cm loaf pan lined with parchment paper.

### Step 5: Final Rise & Bake

1. Cover and let the babka rise for 4–6 hours at a warm room temperature (around 27°C) until it fills out the pan and looks swollen.
2. Preheat your oven to 190°C.
3. Bake for 40–45 minutes. If the top browns too quickly, tent it loosely with aluminum foil. The babka is done when a digital thermometer reads 93°C in the center.

### Step 6: Syrup and Cooling

1. While the babka bakes, boil the 50g water and 50g sugar until dissolved.
2. Brush the hot syrup over the hot babka immediately when it comes out of the oven.
3. Let it cool in the pan for 20 minutes, then lift it out onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.

## Timeline

**Wednesday Night (9:00 PM)** — Build the Levain: Mix your 50% hydration starter, flour, water, and sugar. Ferment 11 hours overnight at room temperature.

**Thursday Morning (8:00 AM)** — Mix the Final Dough: Mix the dough ingredients and your active levain in the stand mixer. Incorporate the butter and knead until smooth (~25 minutes).

**Thursday Day (8:30 AM – 2:30 PM)** — Warm Bulk Rise: Let the dough ferment in a warm spot for 6 hours.

**Thursday Afternoon (2:30 PM)** — Cold Proof: Transfer the covered dough into the refrigerator for ~16.5 hours.

**Friday Morning (7:00 AM)** — Shape: Make the chocolate filling. Roll out the cold dough, spread the filling, roll into a log, slice, and braid. Place into the lined loaf pan (~30 minutes).

**Friday Morning (7:30 AM – 11:15 AM)** — Final Proof: Cover the pan and let the babka rise in a warm spot until swollen and puffy (~3 hours 45 minutes). Preheat oven to 190°C at 10:45 AM.

**Friday Mid-Morning (11:15 AM – 12:00 PM)** — Bake: Bake 40–45 minutes until the center reaches 93°C. Brush with warm sugar syrup immediately out of the oven.
