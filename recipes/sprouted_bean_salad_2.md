# Sprouted Bean Salad

*Source: aprettylifeinthesuburbs.com*

## Ingredients

- 240ml dry sprouted beans or lentils
- 960ml water
- 60ml olive oil
- 45ml fresh lemon juice
- 2ml cumin (or more to taste)
- salt and pepper to taste
- 160ml chopped fresh parsley
- 60ml chopped fresh mint
- 3 green onions, chopped
- 160ml chopped purple cabbage
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 small cucumber, cut into chunks
- 1/2 red or yellow pepper, chopped

## Instructions

1. Cook the sprouted beans or lentils in 960ml of water, simmering for about 15-20 minutes until the beans are soft.
2. When the beans are cooked, drain and rinse with cold water.
3. While the beans are simmering, make the dressing by whisking together the olive oil, lemon juice, cumin, salt and pepper.
4. Drain the cooked beans well after rinsing with cold water.
5. Combine the cooked beans, vegetables and dressing in a bowl and mix well.
6. Serve and enjoy.
