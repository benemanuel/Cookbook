# Brine Calculator - Brine Salt to Water Ratio

*Source: myfermentedfoods.com*

This is a tool page rather than a recipe. The content explains how to use a brine calculator to determine the correct salt-to-water ratio for fermenting vegetables, and provides reference information about brine percentages for different vegetables and the role of salt in fermentation.

**Brine Percentage Guide for Common Vegetables:**

| Vegetable | Brine Percentage |
|-----------|------------------|
| Broccoli | 2% – 2.5% |
| Cauliflower | 2% |
| Celery | 1% – 2% |
| Cucumber | 3.5% – 5% |
| Garlic | 3% |
| Green beans | 2% |
| Onions | 5% |
| Potatoes | 2% |
| Radish | 5% |
| Tomatoes | 2% |
| Olives | 10% |

**General Rule of Thumb:** For vegetable ferments, use 1-3 tablespoons of salt per quart of water.
