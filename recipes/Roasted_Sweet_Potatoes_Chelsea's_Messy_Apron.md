# Roasted Sweet Potatoes

*Source: chelseasmessyapron.com*

## Ingredients

- 907g sweet potatoes (about 3 large, I like Red Garnet or Jewel sweet potatoes best, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch pieces)
- 30ml melted coconut oil
- 4ml  fine sea salt (use less if using table salt)
- 15ml light brown sugar (firmly packed)
- 907g sweet potatoes (about 3 large, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch pieces)
- 30ml olive oil
- 4ml fine sea salt (use less if using table salt)
- 1ml pepper
- 2ml ground chili powder (see note 1)
- 2ml paprika
- 2ml ground cumin
- 2ml garlic powder

## Instructions

1. Plain Roasted Sweet Potatoes: Preheat oven to 218°C. Peel and cube sweet potatoes into 1/2-inch pieces. Add to your largest sheet pan and add the melted coconut oil, salt, and brown sugar on top. Toss to coat all the sweet potatoes, then spread out in an even layer. You don’t want any potatoes overlapping or you’ll end up with steamed potatoes instead of roasted potatoes. Flip every 10–15 minutes, cooking for a total of 27–35 minutes. (I flip every 10 minutes and bake around 30 minutes.)
2. Seasoned Roasted Sweet Potatoes: Preheat oven to 218°C. Peel and cube sweet potatoes into 1/2-inch pieces. Add to your largest sheet pan and add the olive oil, salt, pepper, chili powder, paprika, cumin, and garlic powder on top. Toss to coat all the sweet potatoes, then spread out in an even layer. You don’t want any potatoes overlapping or you’ll end up with steamed potatoes instead of roasted potatoes. Flip every 10–15 minutes, cooking for a total of 27–35 minutes. (I flip every 10 minutes and bake around 30 minutes.)
