# Hungarian Goulash

*Source: spendwithpennies.com*

## Ingredients

- 10ml salted butter, oil, or lard
- 2 medium yellow onions, diced
- 5ml caraway seeds
- 30ml paprika
- 1½ pounds stewing beef or chuck, trimmed and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 30ml all-purpose flour
- 480ml beef broth or water
- 240ml canned diced tomatoes, drained
- 5ml salt or to taste
- 1ml black pepper
- 2 medium potatoes, peeled and diced (optional)
- 3 medium carrots, diced (optional)
- Chopped fresh parsley and sour cream for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a Dutch oven or large pot, melt the butter over medium-low heat, add the onion and cook until translucent about 5 minutes, then stir in caraway seeds and paprika.
2. In a bowl or zip-top bag, dredge the beef with flour and shake off any excess, then add the beef to the onion mixture and cook for 2-3 minutes.
3. Slowly add 60ml of beef broth while scraping up any brown bits from the bottom of the pan, then add the remaining broth and diced tomatoes, and add potatoes and carrots if using, and season with salt and black pepper.
4. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce the heat to a low simmer, cover, and cook for about 1½ to 2 hours or until the beef is fork-tender.
5. Garnish with parsley if desired and serve with a dollop of sour cream.
