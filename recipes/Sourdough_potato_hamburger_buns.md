# Sourdough Potato Hamburger Buns

*Source: baking-sense.com*

## Ingredients

- 1 medium (8oz, 224g) russet potato
- 1 1/2 cups (12 oz, 360ml) whole milk
- 2 tablespoons (1 oz, 28g) unsalted butter
- 2 tablespoons (1 oz, 28g) granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons table salt
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup (8oz, 224g) active sourdough starter (100% hydration)
- 3 cups (15 oz, 425g) bread flour
- 1 egg for brushing
- Sesame seeds for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Peel and chop the potato into 1" dice to yield 1 heaping cup of diced potatoes.
2. In a small saucepan, combine the milk and diced potato, bring to a simmer, and cook until the potato is very tender, stirring frequently and watching for boil overs.
3. Remove the pan from the heat and transfer the milk to a large mixing bowl or stand mixer bowl.
4. Add butter and use a fork or potato masher to mash the potato into the milk.
5. Add the sugar, salt, and egg, then check the temperature and cool if needed until the mixture is about 110°F or slightly warmer than body temperature.
6. Add the sourdough starter and 1 cup of the bread flour and mix on low speed to form a thick batter.
7. Switch to the dough hook or use a wooden spoon or spatula to mix by hand, adding the remaining bread flour until the dough clings to the hook and clears the sides of the bowl.
8. Knead for 5 minutes to develop the dough, then dump onto a floured surface and knead to form a smooth ball that is soft, smooth, and slightly sticky.
9. Transfer the dough to a lightly oiled bowl, turning once to coat, cover, and set aside at room temperature for 3 hours, folding the dough over itself every hour to redistribute the yeast.
10. After about 3 hours when the dough is lively, elastic, and airy, turn it out onto a lightly floured surface and knead to form a smooth ball.
11. Divide the dough into 10 equal portions and roll each portion into a ball.
12. Set the balls on a parchment-lined baking sheet, wet your hands, and flatten each ball to a disc about 1" thick.
13. Cover the sheet pan and allow the rolls to rise until almost doubled in size, about 1 1/2 hours or longer if the dough is cold, testing readiness by pressing with your finger to see if the dent slowly fills in.
14. Preheat the oven to 400°F while the rolls are rising.
15. Brush the tops of the buns with egg and sprinkle each bun generously with sesame seeds.
16. Bake until the buns are golden brown and feel light when lifted off the sheet pan, about 10-15 minutes.
17. Allow the buns to cool completely before slicing.
