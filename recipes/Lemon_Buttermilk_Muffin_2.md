# Lemon Buttermilk Muffins with Greek Yogurt & Honey

## Ingredients

- 200 ml buttermilk
- 60 g oat flakes
- 200 g wholemeal flour
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 80 g whole cane sugar
- 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 100 ml canola oil
- 2 organic lemons (zest and juice)
- 2–3 tsp full-fat Greek yogurt (10%)
- 1 tbsp honey

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (fan/convection) and place paper muffin liners in your tray.
2. Wash the lemons, grate the zest, then squeeze out the juice and set both aside.
3. In a bowl, mix buttermilk, oat flakes, and lemon zest and let it sit for about 5–10 minutes to soften.
4. Beat the eggs, then stir in the soaked oat mixture, sugar, vanilla, canola oil, and lemon juice.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the wholemeal flour and baking powder.
6. Add the dry mixture to the wet mixture and stir until just combined without overmixing.
7. Divide the batter evenly between the muffin cups.
8. Bake on the middle rack for 20–25 minutes, or until golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean.
9. Let the muffins cool in the tray for 5 minutes, then remove and cool completely on a rack.
10. In a small bowl, mix Greek yogurt with honey until smooth.
11. Spoon or pipe a little of the topping on top of each muffin before serving.
