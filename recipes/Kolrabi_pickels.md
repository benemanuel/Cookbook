# Kolrabi pickels

## Ingredients

- 3 cups kohlrabi, peeled and cut into sticks
- 2 medium carrots, peeled and cut into sticks
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 bay leaf
- ¾ cup white vinegar
- 1¼ cups water
- 3 Tbsp sugar
- 1 Tbsp pickling spice mix
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes

## Instructions

1. Pack the kohlrabi sticks, carrot sticks, garlic clove, and bay leaf into a clean quart-sized mason jar.
2. In a small saucepan, combine vinegar, water, sugar, spice mix, salt, and red pepper flakes. Bring to a boil, stirring until sugar and salt dissolve.
3. Pour the hot brine over the vegetables in the jar, making sure they are fully submerged.
4. Let cool to room temperature, then seal and refrigerate. Ready after 24 hours and keeps for up to 2 weeks. Makes 1 quart.
