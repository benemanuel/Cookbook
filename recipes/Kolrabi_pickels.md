# Kohlrabi Pickles

## Ingredients

- 720ml kohlrabi, peeled and cut into sticks
- 2 medium carrots, peeled and cut into sticks
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 bay leaf
- 180ml white vinegar
- 1¼ cups water
- 45ml sugar
- 15ml pickling spice mix
- 5ml salt
- 1ml red pepper flakes

## Instructions

1. Pack the kohlrabi sticks, carrot sticks, garlic clove, and bay leaf into a clean quart-sized mason jar.
2. In a small saucepan, combine vinegar, water, sugar, spice mix, salt, and red pepper flakes, and bring to a boil while stirring until sugar and salt dissolve.
3. Pour the hot brine over the vegetables in the jar, making sure they are fully submerged.
4. Let cool to room temperature, then seal and refrigerate until ready after 24 hours.
