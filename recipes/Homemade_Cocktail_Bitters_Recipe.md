# Homemade Cocktail Bitters

*Source: The Spruce Eats*

## Ingredients

- 1 cup 151-proof grain alcohol
- 1 tablespoon quassia bark
- 1 teaspoon cardamom seeds
- 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
- 1 teaspoon dried orange peel, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon caraway seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon dried gentian root
- 2 cups water
- 1/3 cup sugar

## Instructions

1. In a 1-pint jar or larger, combine 1 cup 151-proof grain alcohol, 1 tablespoon quassia bark, 1 teaspoon cardamom seeds, 1 teaspoon coriander seeds, 1 teaspoon minced dried orange peel, 1/2 teaspoon caraway seeds, and 1/2 teaspoon dried gentian root.
2. Seal the jar, shake, and let the mixture stand in a cool, dark place for 14 days, shaking vigorously once a day.
3. Strain the infused alcohol into a clean jar through cheesecloth to remove the botanicals, gathering the cloth into a ball and squeezing to release as much liquid as possible.
4. Use a muddler or mortar and pestle to break up the strained botanicals as much as possible.
5. In a medium nonreactive saucepan, add 2 cups water and the botanicals, then bring to a boil.
6. Cover and lower the heat, allowing the mixture to simmer for 5 to 7 minutes.
7. Pour the mixture without straining into a 1-pint jar or larger, cover, and allow it to sit for 5 days in a cool and dry place, shaking vigorously once a day.
8. Strain the infused water through cheesecloth and discard the botanicals.
9. Measure the infused alcohol and combine it with an equal amount of aromatic water in a 1-pint jar or larger.
10. In a small saucepan over medium-high heat, add 1/3 cup sugar, stir constantly, and allow the sugar to caramelize until dark brown, about 3 to 4 minutes.
11. Add the caramelized sugar to the alcohol and water mixture and let it dissolve within a few minutes.
12. Shake the bitters, then strain through cheesecloth to remove any sediment.
13. Pour into a bitters bottle or small decanter with a tight-sealing lid and store for up to 12 months unrefrigerated in a cool place.
