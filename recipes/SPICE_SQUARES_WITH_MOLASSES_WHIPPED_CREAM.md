# Spice Squares with Molasses Whipped Cream

## Ingredients

- 600ml sifted cake flour
- 2ml baking soda
- 10ml baking powder
- 1ml allspice
- 1ml mace
- 1ml nutmeg
- 2ml cloves
- 10ml cinnamon
- 120ml butter or other shortening
- 240ml brown sugar, firmly packed
- 2 eggs, well beaten
- 240ml sour milk or buttermilk
- 30ml molasses
- 120ml heavy cream, whipped

## Instructions

1. Sift flour once, measure, add soda, baking powder, and spices, and sift together three times.
2. Cream butter thoroughly, add sugar gradually, and cream together until light and fluffy.
3. Add eggs, mixing well.
4. Add flour mixture alternately with milk, a small amount at a time, beating after each addition until smooth.
5. Bake in a greased 8x8x2 inch pan in a moderate oven at 177°C for 50 minutes.
6. Make molasses whipped cream by folding 30ml molasses into 120ml whipped cream.
7. Serve cake in squares with molasses whipped cream.
