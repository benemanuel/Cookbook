# Crispy Baked Chicken Wings

## Ingredients

- Chicken wings
- Cornstarch
- Chicken soup mix

## Instructions

1. Mix chicken wings with cornstarch and chicken soup mix.
2. Toss wings a second time to ensure even coating.
3. Bake for 20-30 minutes until crispy.
