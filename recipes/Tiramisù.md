# Tiramisu

## Ingredients

- 7 large egg yolks
- 71 grams (80ml) white sugar
- 454 grams (454g) mascarpone cheese, chilled
- 1½ cups brewed espresso (or very strong coffee), cooled to room temperature
- 1 package (about 200 grams) crisp Italian ladyfingers (savoiardi) or Petibers / Petit Beurre Biscuits
- Unsweetened cocoa powder, for dusting
- Dark chocolate shavings, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a stand mixer with the whisk attachment, beat the egg yolks and sugar on high until pale, fluffy, and doubled in volume, about 10 minutes, until the mixture falls in a thick ribbon.
2. Reduce speed to low and add mascarpone a spoonful at a time, letting it incorporate gently, then scrape the bowl and beat for 10–20 seconds more until smooth without overmixing.
3. Pour the espresso into a wide bowl and quickly dip each ladyfinger, one at a time, into the coffee for 1–2 seconds and place them in a single layer in a 9x13-inch dish.
4. Spread half the mascarpone mixture over the layer of dipped ladyfingers and smooth it, then repeat with another layer of dipped ladyfingers and the remaining mascarpone mixture.
5. Cover and refrigerate at least 6 hours or overnight.
6. Just before serving, sift cocoa powder generously over the top and optionally garnish with chocolate shavings.
