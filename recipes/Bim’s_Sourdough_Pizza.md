# Bim's Sourdough Pizza

## Ingredients

- 1000 g bread flour (or 35% wholewheat and 65% bread flour)
- 200 g active starter
- 700 g water
- 20 g olive oil
- 20 g lard (or additional olive oil as substitute)
- 20 g salt
- 30 g honey, sugar, or maple syrup
- Extra flour for dusting
- 2 tablespoons pizza sauce per pizza
- Mozzarella cheese
- Pizza toppings of choice

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, add starter, water, sugar, and flour and mix until everything is combined well.
2. Rest the dough for 30 minutes.
3. Add olive oil, lard, and salt, then mix well and ensure all salt is combined evenly in the flour.
4. Rest the dough in a controlled temperature of 25 degrees Celsius for 30 minutes.
5. Perform a stretch and fold, then rest for 30 minutes.
6. Use coil folds evenly for 30 minutes and repeat this step 3 more times for a total of 4 sets.
7. Proof the dough for 2 to 4 hours in a controlled temperature.
8. After 2 to 3 hours when the dough has doubled in size, separate it into small balls of 250 to 300 g each.
9. Brush olive oil all over the inside of small plastic round containers and shape the dough into balls.
10. Place the dough balls in containers and cover the lids after brushing them with oil as well.
11. Rest the dough in the refrigerator overnight or freeze for up to 2 months.
12. Defrost in the refrigerator for 6 to 8 hours for slow defrosting, or leave on the counter for 2 to 3 hours.
13. Dust the top of the dough with bread flour and pour the dough out from the container.
14. Use your hands to spread the dough and form the edges to create a unique sourdough pizza crust, shaping it into a nice circle of about 10 inches.
15. Heat a pan on the stovetop until it is hot and place the pizza dough in it.
16. Immediately add 2 tablespoons of pizza sauce in the middle and use the back of a spoon to spread it evenly.
17. Top with your favorite pizza toppings and a generous amount of mozzarella cheese.
18. Let the pizza cook in the pan for 3 to 4 minutes until the crust begins to create bubbles.
19. Transfer the pizza to a hot preheated oven with the top heat set to 250 degrees Celsius for 5 minutes.
20. Check frequently to ensure the pizza does not burn while the cheese melts and turns golden brown.
21. Serve the pizza while hot.
