# Cranberry Ginger Shandy

*Source: thespruceeats.com*

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups cranberry juice
- 1 (12-ounce) bottle ginger beer
- 1/2 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice, from 2 to 3 lemons
- 2 (12-ounce) bottles pale ale or wheat beer
- Ginger sugar, for optional rim
- Lemon wedges, for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a large pitcher, combine the cranberry juice, ginger beer, and lemon juice and stir well.
2. Chill the mixture until you're ready to serve.
3. Add beer to the pitcher just before serving.
4. Before serving, rim tall glasses with a ginger-sugar rim by wetting the rim with lemon juice and rolling it in a mix of ground ginger and sugar.
5. Pour the shandy into the prepared glasses over ice if desired and garnish with a lemon wedge.
