# Garlic Buns

## Ingredients

- 1 packet yeast
- 120ml warm water
- a little sugar
- 120ml shortening
- 80ml sugar
- 2 eggs
- 4 rounded cups plain flour
- 5ml baking powder
- 5ml salt
- 1 rounded cup plain flour
- Parmesan cheese
- 180ml softened butter
- garlic

## Instructions

1. Dissolve 1 packet yeast in 120ml warm water with a little sugar and bloom for 10 minutes.
2. Beat together 120ml shortening, 80ml sugar, and 2 eggs until lightly combined.
3. Add the bloomed yeast to the beaten mixture.
4. Mix in 4 rounded cups of plain flour and proof for 1 hour.
5. Add 5ml baking powder, 5ml salt, and 1 rounded cup of flour to bring the total to 1200ml of flour.
6. Make herby garlic butter by mixing 180ml softened butter with garlic to taste.
7. Proceed as you would for cinnamon rolls, filling with the garlic butter mixture and topping with Parmesan cheese.
