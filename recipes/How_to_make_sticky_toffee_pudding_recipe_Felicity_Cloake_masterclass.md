# Sticky Toffee Pudding

*Source: The Guardian*

## Ingredients

- 50g unsalted butter, plus extra to grease
- 175g medjool dates
- 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 75g walnuts (optional)
- 80g golden caster sugar
- 80g dark muscovado sugar
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 cloves
- 175g flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 2 eggs
- 115g unsalted butter (for sauce)
- 75g golden caster sugar (for sauce)
- 40g dark muscovado sugar (for sauce)
- 140ml double cream (for sauce)

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200C (180C fan)/390F/gas 6 and soften the butter. Stone and roughly chop the dates with a small, sharp knife.
2. Put all sauce ingredients in a small pan over low heat until the butter melts, stirring regularly. Turn up the heat and bring to a boil. Simmer for about four minutes until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.
3. Grease a 24cm x 24cm baking dish with butter. Pour half the sauce into the dish, tip it to coat the base completely, then place in the freezer. Boil a kettle of water.
4. Put the dates and bicarbonate of soda in a heatproof bowl and pour over 300ml boiling water to cover. Leave to soften. If using walnuts, toast them in a dry frying pan over medium-high heat until fragrant, then roughly chop.
5. Put the butter and both sugars into a large bowl or food mixer with a pinch of salt. Beat vigorously until very light and fluffy. Grind the cloves to a fine powder and whisk with the flour and baking powder in a separate bowl. Beat the eggs in a cup or jug.
6. Beat the eggs into the butter and sugar mixture a little at a time until thoroughly incorporated. Fold in the dry ingredients with a large metal spoon, being careful not to knock out too much air. Finally, fold in the dates with their soaking water and the walnuts if using.
7. Take the dish out of the freezer and pour the batter on top of the sauce. Level out the batter and bake for about 30 minutes until just firm to the touch in the centre. Reheat the remaining sauce in the pan towards the end of cooking.
8. Heat the grill to medium. Poke small holes across the top of the baked pudding with a skewer, then pour the sauce over the top. Put the dish under the grill until the top is molten and bubbling, watching carefully so the sugar doesn't burn.
9. Serve immediately with ice-cream, custard, crème fraîche or Greek yoghurt. Alternatively, bake the pudding ahead of time without the remaining sauce, cover with foil, reheat in a 180C (160C fan)/350F/gas 4 oven for about 20 minutes, then continue with the grilling step.
