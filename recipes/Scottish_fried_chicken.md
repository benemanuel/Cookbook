# Scottish Fried Chicken

*Source: null*

## Ingredients

- 480ml apple cider
- 120ml salt
- 1 (4 1/2 to 5-pound) chicken, cut into 10 pieces
- Water
- 2 eggs
- 30ml cayenne pepper
- 5ml freshly ground black pepper
- 240ml all-purpose flour
- 60ml cornstarch
- 2 quarts vegetable shortening, for frying

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, whisk together cider and salt until salt dissolves.
2. Add chicken pieces and enough water to coat and soak 1 hour to overnight in the refrigerator until chicken is completely submerged in brine.
3. In a large pot, heat vegetable shortening until a deep-fry thermometer reaches 163 degrees Celsius.
4. Line a sheet tray with a wire rack.
5. In a large bowl, whisk together eggs, cayenne and black pepper.
6. In a paper bag, shake together flour and cornstarch.
7. Drain chicken from brine and dip in batches first in egg mixture then in flour mixture to coat.
8. Set coated chicken on wire rack to let coating sit for 10 minutes.
9. In 2 batches, fry chicken until golden brown on both sides and cooked through, 15 to 20 minutes depending on the size of the piece.
10. Remove chicken from oil and drain on paper towel-lined plate.
