# Raspberry and Apricot Shortcake

*Source: The Guardian*

## Ingredients

- 6 apricots
- 175g softened butter
- 175g golden caster sugar
- 2 eggs
- 80g ground almonds
- 175g self-raising flour
- a few drops vanilla extract
- 150g raspberries
- a little icing sugar to finish
- 100g plain flour (for topping)
- 75g butter (for topping)
- 3 tbsp jumbo oats
- 2 tbsp demerara sugar
- 2 tbsp flaked almonds

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 160C/gas mark 3 and line the base of a 22cm round spring-form cake tin with baking parchment.
2. Process the flour and butter to coarse crumbs using a processor or by rubbing them together between your fingers and thumbs.
3. Add the oats, sugar and flaked almonds to the crumbs, then add a little water and shake the bowl until the mixture comes together in crumbs of different sizes.
4. Halve the apricots and remove their stones.
5. Beat the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.
6. Beat the eggs briefly to mix the yolks and whites, then add gradually to the mixture with the beater on a slow speed.
7. Fold in the ground almonds and flour, then add the vanilla extract.
8. Transfer the mixture to the lined tin and smooth the surface.
9. Place the halved apricots cut side down on the surface of the cake mixture, then scatter over the raspberries.
10. Scatter the crumble mixture loosely over the fruit and bake in the preheated oven for 1 hour and 15 minutes.
11. Check the progress with a metal skewer inserted in the centre; it should come out moist but without any uncooked mixture attached.
12. Bake for a further 5 minutes if necessary and remove from the oven and leave to settle for 20 minutes.
13. Run a palette knife around the edge of the cake to free it from its tin, then ease open the tin and transfer the cake to a plate.
14. Dust lightly with icing sugar.
