# Aviary's Bloody Mary

## Ingredients

- 20g Fresno chili ice
- 10 celery marbles
- Worcestershire cube
- 20g Fresno chili ice
- 10 celery marbles
- 180g bloody mary batch
- 3 Fresno chilies, stems and seeds removed
- 400g water
- 25g Fresno juice
- 14 celery stalks
- 18g sugar
- 2g kosher salt
- 75g Worcestershire sauce
- 150g water
- 1000g heirloom tomatoes
- 296g vodka
- 39g banyuls vinegar
- 25g St. George Green Chile Vodka
- 22g grated fresh horseradish
- 20g lemon juice
- 70g sugar
- 14.5g kosher salt
- 3g black pepper, coarsely ground

## Instructions

1. Extract Fresno juice using a juicer or blender and nut milk bag.
2. Combine 25g of Fresno juice with 400g water and stir to mix thoroughly.
3. Pour into a metal tin and freeze completely.
4. Using an ice pick, break into small shards and reserve in freezer.
5. Boil 14 celery stalks for 1 minute, then transfer to an ice bath to blanch.
6. Drain excess water and extract juice using a juicer or blender and nut milk bag.
7. Measure 400g celery juice and whisk in 18g sugar and 2g kosher salt.
8. Using a syringe or seasoning injector, fill 16mm spherical molds and freeze until completely solid.
9. Reserve the celery marbles in the freezer.
10. Combine 75g Worcestershire sauce and 150g water in a small bowl and mix.
11. Fill 1.25" cube ice molds with the mixture and freeze until solid.
12. Reserve the Worcestershire ice cubes in freezer.
13. Preheat oven to 500°F.
14. Halve 1000g heirloom tomatoes and place on a sheet tray with a silicone baking mat.
15. Roast in oven for 30 minutes and let cool completely.
16. Transfer 700g roasted tomatoes to blender.
17. Add 296g vodka, 39g banyuls vinegar, 25g St. George Green Chile Vodka, 22g grated fresh horseradish, 20g lemon juice, 70g sugar, and 14.5g kosher salt to the blender.
18. Blend on high speed for 1 minute.
19. Strain through a nut milk bag or chinois.
20. Add 3g black pepper and transfer to a glass bottle.
21. Reserve in refrigerator to chill thoroughly.
22. Place a metal straw in a chilled collins glass.
23. Layer 20g Fresno chili ice, 10 celery marbles, Worcestershire cube, 20g Fresno chili ice, and 10 celery marbles in the glass in that order.
24. Pour the bloody mary batch over the contents of the glass and serve.
