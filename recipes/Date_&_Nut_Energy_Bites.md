# Date & Nut Energy Bites

*Source: null*

## Ingredients

- 500g seedless dates (Medjool or vacuum-packed)
- 100g raw or lightly roasted pistachios
- 100g raw or lightly roasted cashew nuts
- 100g raw or lightly roasted almonds
- 4 tablespoons ghee or coconut oil
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- ½ teaspoon ground cardamom or cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons cacao nibs
- 1–2 tablespoons unsweetened shredded coconut

## Instructions

1. Coarsely chop the pistachios, cashews, and almonds or pulse them in a food processor a few times.
2. Optionally lightly toast the nuts in a dry pan over medium heat for 3–5 minutes, stirring constantly, until just fragrant, then let cool.
3. If your dates are dry, soak them in warm water for 5–10 minutes, then drain well.
4. Add the softened dates and ghee to a food processor and blend until you get a thick, sticky paste, scraping down the sides as needed.
5. Add vanilla or spices to the date paste if using.
6. Transfer the paste to a mixing bowl and mix in the chopped nuts, chia seeds, and cacao nibs if using.
7. Knead everything together with your hands or a spatula until it is thick and moldable.
8. Roll the mixture into small balls, about 25–30 pieces depending on size.
9. Optionally roll the balls in shredded coconut, cocoa powder, or ground nuts for coating.
10. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for 2–3 weeks or freeze for up to 3 months.
