# Chocolate Frosting

<https://www.simplyrecipes.com/hersheys-busy-day-cocoa-icing-review-8665547>

## Ingredients
The Busy Day Cocoa Icing Recipe
Ingredients:

90ml boiling water
60ml (1/2 stick or 60ml) butter
10ml vanilla
120ml Hershey’s Cocoa
720ml powdered sugar

## Instructions

The steps couldn’t be simpler. Pour the boiling water over the butter, and immediately add the vanilla and cocoa. Beat until well blended. Then, add the powdered sugar. Beat until creamy, adding more water if necessary, until the icing is thin enough to spread.

Spread on a warm cake (more on that later). The recipe is perfect for a generously frosted 8 or 9-inch round cake or a 9x13-inch sheet cake.
