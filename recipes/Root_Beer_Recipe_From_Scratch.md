# Root Beer Recipe From Scratch

## Ingredients

- 1L water
- 35g fresh ginger, unpeeled and chopped
- 5g cinnamon stick
- 20g dried sassafras root bark
- 10g dried sarsaparilla root
- 10g dried liquorice root
- 7g dried cherry bark
- 2 mL salt
- 450g Demerara brown sugar
- 75g lactose (optional)
- 15 mL vanilla extract
- 75 mL active ginger bug (for every 2L) or 2 tsp beer yeast

## Instructions

1. Mix 1 part syrup to 3 parts water.
2. Mix in active ginger bug or beer yeast.
3. Pour into pressure safe bottles, filling to within 2 inches of top but no higher.
4. Cap and set aside at room temperature to let ferment for 2 to 3 days.
5. Check fermentation and then chill or store in refrigerator.
