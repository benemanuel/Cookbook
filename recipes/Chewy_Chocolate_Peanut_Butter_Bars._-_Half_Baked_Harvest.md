# Chewy Chocolate Peanut Butter Bars

*Source: Half Baked Harvest*

## Ingredients

- 120ml honey
- 60ml pure maple syrup
- 60ml creamy peanut butter (just peanuts and salt)
- 15ml coconut oil
- 1440ml brown rice krispies
- 300ml creamy peanut butter (just peanuts and salt)
- 80ml pure maple syrup
- 60ml plus 15ml coconut oil
- 10ml vanilla extract
- Sea salt
- 340g dark chocolate, chopped

## Instructions

1. Line a 9x13 inch baking dish with parchment paper.
2. Add the brown rice krispies to a large bowl.
3. In a medium pot, combine the honey, maple syrup, peanut butter, and coconut oil, bring to a gentle boil over medium heat while stirring constantly, cook for 2 minutes, then remove from heat and pour the mix over the brown rice krispies.
4. Stir well to combine and spread the mix onto the prepared pan, packing it in tightly, then place in the freezer.
5. In a medium pot, mix the peanut butter, maple syrup, and 60ml coconut oil and bring to a gentle boil over medium heat while stirring constantly.
6. Cook for 2 minutes, then remove from heat and stir in the vanilla and salt.
7. Pour the peanut mixture over the krispies layer and freeze for 30 minutes until firm.
8. Melt together the chocolate and 15ml coconut oil in the microwave, let cool for 5 minutes, and spread over the peanut butter layer.
9. Return to the fridge to set for about 10 minutes, then slice and keep in the fridge.
