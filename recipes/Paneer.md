# Homemade Paneer

## Ingredients

- Full fat milk
- Lemon juice or vinegar
- Cheesecloth

## Instructions

1. Heat milk to 80–85 degrees Celsius using a food thermometer, being careful not to let it boil or overflow.
2. Add lemon juice or vinegar one teaspoon at a time, stirring for a minute between each addition.
3. Use a maximum of 2 tablespoons of lemon juice or vinegar per liter of milk.
4. Stop adding the acidic agent once the milk has started curdling well.
5. Pour the curdled milk through cheesecloth to strain.
6. Place the curds in cheesecloth and apply approximately 10 kg of weight on top to press the paneer.
