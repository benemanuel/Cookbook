# Vintage Cocktail Books - A Timeline

*Source: Difford's Guide*

This is a bibliography and reference guide rather than a recipe, documenting the history and timeline of vintage cocktail books published from 1800 onwards. The page provides links to historical cocktail recipe books organized chronologically by publication period, many of which are available to read online through resources including the Exposition Universelle des Vins et Spiritueux digital library, Archive.org, and Cocktail Kingdom's library.

The timeline begins with Richard Cook's first known dedicated English-language book recording drinks recipes in 1827 and continues through 1999, helping readers understand the origin and development of classic cocktails that appear on bar menus today.
