# Zucchini Cauliflower Summer Gratin

*Source: Relishing It*

## Ingredients

- 1 large head cauliflower
- Kosher salt
- 1¼ lbs zucchini, coarsely grated
- 30ml olive oil
- 180ml chopped onion
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2ml finely ground black pepper
- 30ml all-purpose flour (or gluten-free)
- 60ml milk
- 80ml heavy cream
- 160ml grated parmesan, divided
- 15ml chopped fresh rosemary
- 45ml chopped fresh parsley

## Instructions

1. Core and break cauliflower into pieces, then pulse in food processor in batches to rice-grain size, yielding about 3½ cups.
2. Preheat oven to 191°C and lightly oil a shallow baking dish.
3. Mix zucchini with 5ml salt and place in a colander over a bowl, then squeeze to remove water (about 160ml shreds) and reserve the liquid.
4. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat, add onion with a pinch of salt, and cook for 4-5 minutes.
5. Reduce heat to medium, add cauliflower, garlic, drained zucchini, 2ml salt, and pepper, then cook for 7-8 minutes.
6. Sprinkle flour and stir, then gradually add the reserved zucchini water, milk, and cream over medium heat, stirring for 2-3 minutes to prevent curdling.
7. Remove from heat and stir in 80ml parmesan, rosemary, and parsley, then pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish and bake for 20 minutes.
8. Add the remaining parmesan and bake for 10 more minutes until golden, with optional broiling to brown the top further.
