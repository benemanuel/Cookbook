# The Guardian: Nigel Slater's recipes for raspberry and apricot shortcake, and apricot and harissa chutney

Glossy, rich and fleeting, apricots always make summer special. Two ways to use a glut of them: a buttery shortcake layered with raspberries, and a glossy chutney spiked with harissa.

## Ingredients

### Raspberry and apricot shortcake (serves 8-10)

- 6 apricots
- 175 g softened butter
- 175 g golden caster sugar
- 2 eggs
- 80 g ground almonds
- 175 g self-raising flour
- a few drops vanilla extract
- 150 g raspberries
- icing sugar, a little to finish

For the topping:

- 100 g plain flour
- 75 g butter
- 45 ml jumbo oats
- 30 ml demerara sugar
- 30 ml flaked almonds

### Apricot and harissa chutney (makes 3 x 400 ml jam jars)

- 500 g apricots, fresh
- 500 g tomatoes
- 1 medium onion
- 300 ml white wine vinegar
- 250 g golden granulated sugar
- 90 g sultanas
- zest of 1 small lemon
- 5 ml salt
- 30 ml harissa
- 60 ml honey

## Instructions

### Raspberry and apricot shortcake

1. Preheat the oven to 160°C/gas mark 3. Line the base of a 22 cm round spring-form cake tin with baking parchment.
2. Make the topping: process the flour and butter to coarse crumbs (in a processor or by rubbing between your fingers and thumbs). Add the oats, sugar and flaked almonds, then a little water (no more than a tablespoon), and shake the bowl until the mixture comes together in crumbs of different sizes. Set aside.
3. Halve the apricots and remove their stones.
4. Beat the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy. Beat the eggs briefly to mix, then add gradually to the mixture with the beater on a slow speed.
5. Fold in the ground almonds and flour, then add the vanilla extract. Transfer the mixture to the lined tin and smooth the surface. Place the halved apricots cut-side down on top, then scatter over the raspberries.
6. Scatter the crumble mixture loosely over the fruit and bake for 1 hour and 15 minutes. Check with a metal skewer inserted in the centre - it should come out moist but without uncooked mixture attached. Bake a further 5 minutes if necessary. Remove from the oven and leave to settle for 20 minutes.
7. Run a palette knife around the edge to free the cake from its tin, then transfer to a plate and dust lightly with icing sugar.

### Apricot and harissa chutney

1. Halve the apricots and discard their stones. Put them into a large, deep, heavy-based saucepan. Cut the tomatoes in half and add to the pot, then peel and roughly chop the onion and stir it in.
2. Add the vinegar, sugar, sultanas, lemon zest and salt and bring to the boil. Stir well, lower the heat to a simmer, partially cover, and leave to bubble gently for an hour or so.
3. Stir in the harissa and honey and continue cooking for 10 minutes. Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt, honey or lemon juice as needed. Ladle into sterilised jars, seal, and store in a cool, dark place.
