# Garlic Pickle (Lahsun Achar)

*Source: tarladalal.com*

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup garlic cloves, peeled
- 3 tbsp mustard oil
- 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp finely chopped jaggery
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp split mustard seeds
- 1/4 tsp split fenugreek seeds
- 1/4 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/4 tsp crushed coriander seeds
- 1/4 tsp asafoetida

## Instructions

1. Transfer split mustard seeds, split fenugreek seeds, cumin seeds, crushed coriander seeds, and asafoetida to a small mixer jar.
2. Without using any water, blend them in a mixer jar to a smooth powder and keep aside.
3. Heat the oil in a non-stick kadhai and add the garlic cloves.
4. Add turmeric powder and sauté on a slow flame for 3 to 4 minutes or until the garlic cloves are soft, stirring continuously.
5. Add the lemon juice and cook on a slow flame for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Add the chilli powder, jaggery, and salt and cook on a slow flame for another 2 to 3 minutes or until the jaggery has dissolved, stirring occasionally.
7. Add the prepared masala powder, mix well, and cook for another minute.
8. Remove from the flame, cool completely, and store in a sterilized glass jar.
9. Store the garlic pickle in a cool dry place and stir it with a clean and dry spoon once a day after storing in the glass jar.
10. The garlic pickle is ready to serve after 1 week, though it tastes better the longer it is preserved.
