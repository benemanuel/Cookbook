# cream cheese coffee cake

## Ingredients

### Cake
- 2½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 cup (225g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup buttermilk

### Cinnamon Filling
- 6 Tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 2 Tbsp cinnamon
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup brown sugar

### Cheesecake Layer
- 2 packages (450g total) cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Streusel Topping
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ cup (115g) unsalted butter, melted
- 2 cups all-purpose flour

### Glaze
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2–3 Tbsp milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x13 baking pan.
2. Make the cake batter: beat butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, then vanilla. Alternately mix in flour mixture (flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt) and buttermilk, beginning and ending with flour.
3. Make the cinnamon filling: mix butter, cinnamon, flour, and brown sugar until crumbly.
4. Make the cheesecake layer: beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth. Add eggs and vanilla, mix until combined.
5. Make the streusel: combine brown sugar, cinnamon, salt, melted butter, and flour until crumbly.
6. Layer in the pan: spread half the cake batter, then scatter cinnamon filling, then spread cheesecake layer, then remaining cake batter, then top with streusel.
7. Bake for 45–50 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool completely.
8. Whisk together glaze ingredients and drizzle over the cooled cake.
