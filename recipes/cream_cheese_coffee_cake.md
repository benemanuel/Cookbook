# Cream Cheese Coffee Cake

## Ingredients

- 2½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 cup (225g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 6 Tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 2 Tbsp cinnamon
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 2 packages (450g total) cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ cup (115g) unsalted butter, melted
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2–3 Tbsp milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13 baking pan.
2. Beat butter and sugar until fluffy, add eggs one at a time, then vanilla, and alternately mix in the flour mixture and buttermilk, beginning and ending with flour.
3. Mix butter, cinnamon, flour, and brown sugar until crumbly to make the cinnamon filling.
4. Beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth, add eggs and vanilla, and mix until combined for the cheesecake layer.
5. Combine brown sugar, cinnamon, salt, melted butter, and flour until crumbly to make the streusel.
6. Spread half the cake batter in the pan, scatter cinnamon filling, spread cheesecake layer, add remaining cake batter, and top with streusel.
7. Bake for 45–50 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean and cool completely.
8. Whisk together powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla extract and drizzle the glaze over the cooled cake.
