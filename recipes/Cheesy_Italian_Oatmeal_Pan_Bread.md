# Cheesy Italian Oatmeal Pan Bread

## Ingredients

- 2 cups water
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 4-5 cups all-purpose flour, divided
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 (1/4 oz) packages active dry yeast
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 tablespoon grated parmesan cheese
- 1/2 teaspoon dried basil leaves
- 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 4 tablespoons butter, melted

## Instructions

1. Spray a 13x9 inch baking pan with non-stick spray.
2. Bring water to a boil in a medium saucepan and stir in rolled oats and 3 tablespoons butter.
3. Cool the oatmeal mixture to 100-110°F.
4. Combine 1 1/2 cups flour, sugar, salt, and yeast in a large mixing bowl and blend well.
5. Add the cooled oats mixture and egg, then blend at low speed of an electric mixer until moistened.
6. Beat at medium speed for 3 minutes.
7. By hand, stir in 1 3/4 to 2 1/2 cups flour and mozzarella cheese to form a stiff dough.
8. Knead in 1/2 to 3/4 cup flour on a floured surface until the dough is smooth and elastic, about 5 minutes.
9. Shape the dough into a ball and cover with a large bowl, then let rest 15 minutes.
10. Punch down the dough and press it into the prepared pan.
11. Using a very sharp knife, cut diagonal lines 1 1/2 inches apart, cutting completely through the dough, then repeat in the opposite direction to create a diamond pattern.
12. Cover loosely with greased plastic wrap and a cloth towel, then let rise in a warm place (80-85°F) until light and doubled in size, about 45 minutes.
13. Heat the oven to 375°F and uncover the dough.
14. Redefine the diamond pattern by cutting in an up and down motion through to the bottom of the pan with a sharp knife.
15. Combine parmesan cheese, basil, oregano, and garlic powder in a small bowl and set aside.
16. Spoon 2 tablespoons of melted butter over the cut dough.
17. Bake for 15 minutes.
18. Brush the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter over the partially baked bread.
19. Sprinkle the parmesan mixture over the bread.
20. Bake for an additional 10 to 15 minutes or until golden brown, then serve warm or cool.
