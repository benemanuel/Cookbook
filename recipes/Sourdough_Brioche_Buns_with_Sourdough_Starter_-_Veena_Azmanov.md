# Sourdough Brioche Buns

*Source: veenaazmanov.com*

## Ingredients

- 150 ml whole milk, room temperature
- 200 grams eggs, room temperature
- 560 grams bread flour
- 180 grams sourdough starter
- 50 grams sugar
- 10 grams kosher salt
- 170 grams unsalted butter

## Instructions

1. Refresh your sourdough starter by combining 50g starter, 50g water, and 50g flour in a bowl, stirring well, covering, and leaving to rise for 3 to 5 hours at room temperature.
2. In a large bowl of a stand mixer, add the milk, eggs, sourdough starter, and bread flour, stirring well with a wooden spoon or spatula, then cover and rest for one hour.
3. Add the sugar, salt, and butter to the dough and knead with a dough hook attachment on medium speed for 10 to 15 minutes until smooth and elastic.
4. Transfer the dough to an oiled bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and leave to bulk ferment in the refrigerator at 3°C for 6 to 8 hours.
5. Transfer the dough to a clean work surface and divide into 12 buns of approximately 110 grams each.
6. Shape each portion into a tight ball by rolling it, tucking seams under, and rolling on an unfloured surface to create tension.
7. Place the buns on a parchment-lined baking sheet, flatten each bun slightly, cover with plastic wrap or a kitchen towel, and proof until doubled in size for 3 hours at room temperature or 8 to 10 hours in the refrigerator.
8. Preheat the oven to 191°C.
9. Brush each bun with egg wash and optionally sprinkle with sesame seeds or sugar pearls.
10. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes for small buns or 20 to 25 minutes for larger buns until golden brown.
11. Remove the buns from the oven and immediately cover with a clean kitchen cloth to keep them soft, then transfer to a cooling rack.
