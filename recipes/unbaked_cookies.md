# Unbaked Cookies

## Ingredients

- 8 tablespoons butter (1 stick)
- 1 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup milk
- 4 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/8 teaspoon kosher salt
- 3 cups old-fashioned oats
- 1/2 cup shredded coconut
- 1/2 cup peanut butter

## Instructions

1. In a medium saucepan, bring butter, sugar, milk, cocoa powder, vanilla and salt to a boil and cook for 1 minute, stirring continuously, then remove from the heat.
2. Stir in the oatmeal, peanut butter and coconut and let cool for 5 minutes.
3. Spoon 1-inch balls onto parchment paper and cool for 20 minutes.
4. Store in an airtight container.
