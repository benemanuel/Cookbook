# Tarka Dhal

*Source: Instagram*

## Ingredients

- vegetable oil
- 1 onion, peeled and finely chopped
- 320g red lentils, rinsed
- 10ml turmeric
- 10ml ground coriander
- salt and pepper
- 8 garlic cloves, peeled and sliced
- 8 green chiles
- 10ml cumin seeds
- 2ml fennel seeds
- 10ml black mustard seeds
- 1 thumb of fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
- 1 small handful of fresh coriander, optional
- cooked rice or Indian bread, to serve, optional

## Instructions

1. Heat a little oil in a big pot on medium heat, then add the onion and fry for 8 minutes or until soft.
2. Add the rinsed red lentils to the pot along with 1.5 liters of water, turmeric, coriander, and 5ml of salt.
3. Stir, then bring to the boil and reduce the heat to a simmer and cook with the lid on for 15 minutes.
4. Remove the lid, reduce the heat, and leave to cook on a low heat for a few minutes while you make the tarka.
5. Heat 90ml of oil in a frying pan on medium-high heat, then add the garlic and fry for 1-2 minutes.
6. Trim a tiny bit off the ends of the chiles to allow the heat to escape into the dhal.
7. Add the chiles to the oil along with the cumin seeds, fennel seeds, mustard seeds, and ginger and fry for 1-2 minutes.
8. Working quickly, add the oil mixture to the pot with the lentils and stir to combine.
9. Simmer for 5 minutes or longer if you prefer your dhal to be a thicker consistency.
10. Serve with chopped fresh coriander if desired.
