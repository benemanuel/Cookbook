# Focaccia Genovese

## Ingredients

- 325 g bread flour
- 275 g water
- 50 g active starter
- 6 g fine sea salt
- 8 g olive oil
- 1-2 small russet potatoes, julienned
- 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
- Drizzling olive oil
- Large handful fresh herbs such as parsley or thyme
- 50 g water for brine
- 40 g olive oil for brine
- 8 g fine sea salt for brine

## Instructions

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour and water, mix well until incorporated and cover, then let it rest for 20 minutes.
2. Add the active starter, sea salt, and olive oil to the rested dough, then using wet hands, combine and mix the dough for 5 minutes using the Rubaud method.
3. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes after mixing.
4. Grease a separate bowl and using wet hands, coil fold the dough a few times, rotating the bowl as needed, until it comes together into one blob.
5. Transfer the dough to the greased bowl, cover, and let it double in size on the counter for around 7-8 hours depending on kitchen temperature.
6. Preheat the oven to 500°F (260°C) once the dough has doubled in size.
7. Using well-oiled hands, split the dough into two equal parts and transfer one piece into a well-oiled large cast iron skillet or baking sheet.
8. Using your fingers, spread the dough out into a thin circle or rectangle and let rest for 10 minutes.
9. In a large shallow bowl, whisk together the water, olive oil, and salt to make the brine and set aside.
10. Use a julienne peeler or knife or mandoline to prepare the potato or slice the onions.
11. Add the potato or onions to a small bowl with a drizzling of olive oil and toss well to coat.
12. Use your fingers to sprinkle and spread the toppings over the dough and gently press them in, or dimple the dough with your fingers if not using toppings.
13. Whisk the brine again and pour it evenly over the bread.
14. Bake for 13-15 minutes, then broil for around 3-5 minutes until the potatoes or onions and edges are slightly golden.
15. Transfer the focaccia to a wire rack to cool for 10 minutes using a thin spatula.
16. Sprinkle with roughly chopped fresh herbs if using, slice, and enjoy.
