# Beetroot and Apple Salad with Yogurt Dressing and Hazelnuts

*Source: tobiascooks.com*

## Ingredients

- 500 grams boiled beetroot
- 250 grams peeled apples
- juice of half a lemon
- 150 grams Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon honey
- pinch of salt
- 100 grams hazelnuts, chopped

## Instructions

1. Grate the beetroot and the peeled apple into a bowl.
2. Sprinkle the lemon juice over the grated vegetables and mix well.
3. For the dressing, dissolve the honey and salt with the lemon juice.
4. Dissolve the Greek yogurt into the dressing mixture.
5. Crush the hazelnuts with a knife.
6. Place salad on a plate, pour some dressing over it, and sprinkle nuts on top.
