#  Phyllo (or Filo) Dough

*Source: allrecipes.com*

## Ingredients

- 480ml all-purpose flour, plus more for kneading
- 25ml olive oil
- 2ml fine salt
- 180ml warm water (43°C / 43 degrees C)
- 10ml white wine vinegar
- 120ml cornstarch
- 30ml all-purpose flour

## Instructions

1. Place 480ml flour in a mixing bowl and make a well in the center, then drizzle in olive oil and add salt.
2. Pour in warm water and white wine vinegar, then mix until dough just comes together and pulls away from the sides of the bowl, about 1 to 2 minutes.
3. Transfer dough to a lightly floured work surface and knead until smooth, using just enough flour to keep it from sticking, for 2 or 3 minutes, then continue to knead until dough is supple and elastic for about 5 more minutes.
4. Wrap dough ball in plastic wrap and let rest at room temperature for 1 to 2 hours.
5. Use a kitchen scale to divide dough into twenty 25-gram portions and roll each portion into a ball, then place on a plate and cover with plastic wrap to prevent drying out while working in batches of 5.
6. Mix cornstarch and 30ml flour together in a bowl, then dust a work surface and the first dough ball with the cornstarch mixture.
7. Flatten and roll out into a circle about 5 inches in diameter, dust again with cornstarch mixture, then roll out 4 more dough balls to about the same diameter and stack them on the first one, dusting each layer with more cornstarch mixture to keep them from sticking together.
8. Roll out the 5-circle stack to a larger circle about double in size, turning as you go to maintain a round shape, then separate each layer and lay out the circles, reapplying more cornstarch mixture where needed and restacking them.
9. Roll again until the 5-layer stack is paper-thin, about 10 to 12 inches in diameter, then place on a sheet of parchment paper, top with another piece of parchment, gently roll up the dough, wrap it in plastic wrap, and refrigerate.
10. Repeat with the remaining 15 balls of dough in batches of 5.
