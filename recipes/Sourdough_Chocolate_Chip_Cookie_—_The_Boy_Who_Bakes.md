# Sourdough Chocolate Chip Cookie

*Source: The Boy Who Bakes*

## Ingredients

- 225g unsalted butter
- 334g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 220g caster sugar
- 220g light brown sugar
- 3 large egg yolks
- 150g sourdough starter (50% hydration)
- 83ml milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 350g 70% dark chocolate, roughly chopped or chips
- 1 tsp flaky sea salt and/or chili flakes for topping

## Instructions

1. Place the butter in a saucepan over medium heat and cook until melted, bubbling, foaming, and the milk solids turn golden brown and smell nutty, then remove from heat and allow to cool for about 30 minutes.
2. In a large bowl whisk together the flour, baking powder, and baking soda, then set aside.
3. Place the cooled browned butter, caster sugar, and brown sugar into a mixing bowl and beat until thoroughly combined and any sugar lumps are broken up.
4. Add the egg yolks and beat for 2–3 minutes until the mixture looks smooth and glossy.
5. Add the sourdough starter, milk, and vanilla extract and mix until smooth and fully incorporated.
6. Add the flour mixture and mix on low speed only until a dough forms, being careful not to overmix.
7. Fold in the chopped dark chocolate until evenly distributed.
8. Cover the dough and refrigerate for at least 4 hours and up to 24 hours, or freeze as balls.
9. Portion the dough onto parchment-lined baking trays using 40g portions and sprinkle each cookie lightly with flaky sea salt and chili powder.
10. Bake at 180°C in a conventional oven or 160°C in a fan oven for 12–15 minutes depending on cookie size, adding 1–2 minutes if baking from frozen, until the edges are set and lightly browned while the centers remain slightly soft.
11. Allow the cookies to cool on the tray for 10 minutes before transferring to a rack.
