# Sourdough Chocolate Chip Cookie 
based on 
<https://www.theboywhobakes.co.uk/recipes/2020/5/7/sourdough-chocolate-chip-cookie>   

### (150g Stiff Starter • 350g 70% Dark Chocolate)

## Ingredients

* 225g unsalted butter
* 334g plain flour
* 1 tsp baking powder
* 1 tsp baking soda
* 220g caster sugar
* 220g light brown sugar
* 3 large egg yolks
* 150g sourdough starter (50% hydration)
* 83ml milk
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 350g 70% dark chocolate, roughly chopped or chips
* 1 tsp flaky sea salt and/or chili flakes for topping

---

## Method

### 1. Brown the butter
							   

Place the butter in a saucepan over medium heat.

Cook until melted, bubbling, and then foaming. Continue cooking until the milk solids turn golden brown and smell nutty.

Remove from the heat and allow to cool for about 30 minutes.
					
				  

You should end up with approximately **170g browned butter**.

---

### 2. Prepare the dry ingredients

In a large bowl whisk together:

* 334g Flour
* 1 tsp Baking powder
* 1 tsp Baking soda

Set aside.

---
				
					  
											   

### 3. Mix butter and sugars

Place the cooled browned butter, caster sugar, and brown sugar into a mixing bowl.

Beat until thoroughly combined and any sugar lumps are broken up.

---

### 4. Add the yolks

Add the egg yolks and beat for 2–3 minutes until the mixture looks smooth and glossy.

---

### 5. Add starter, milk, and vanilla
													  

Add:

   * 150g stiff starter (50% hydration)
   * 83ml milk
   * 1 tsp vanilla extract

Mix until smooth and fully incorporated.

---

### 6. Add dry ingredients

Add the flour mixture and mix on low speed only until a dough forms.

Do not overmix.

---

### 7. Add chocolate

Fold in the chopped 70% dark chocolate until evenly distributed.

---

### 8. Chill

Cover the dough and refrigerate for at least **4 hours** and up to **24 hours**. 
Freeze as balls.

---

### 9. Portion and salt

Portion the dough onto parchment-lined baking trays.

For evenly sized cookies:

* 40g portions → about 40 cookies

Sprinkle each cookie lightly with flaky sea salt and chili powder.

---

### 10. Bake

Bake at:

* **180°C conventional oven**, or
* **160°C fan oven**

for **12–15 minutes**, depending on cookie size. can be baked from frozen balls, just add a minute or two.

The edges should be set and lightly browned while the centers remain slightly soft.

Allow the cookies to cool on the tray for 10 minutes before transferring to a rack.

### Notes
- Halve recipe with 1 yolk
- Dough balls freeze 1 month; bake from frozen +1-2 min
- Keeps 4-5 days sealed
