# spicy fish balls

## Ingredients

- Vegetable oil, for frying
- 3 shallots, finely diced
- ½ yellow pepper, diced
- ½ orange pepper, diced
- ½–1 jalapeño, minced (to taste)
- 1 can (450g) diced tomatoes
- 800g tomato sauce
- 1 loaf gefilte fish, cut into balls or slices
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp dried basil
- ½ tsp dried parsley
- ½ tsp bay leaf (ground) or 1 whole bay leaf

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large skillet or pot over medium heat. Sauté shallots, yellow pepper, orange pepper, and jalapeño until softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Add diced tomatoes and tomato sauce. Stir in salt, basil, parsley, and bay leaf. Bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes.
3. Add the gefilte fish pieces to the sauce. Simmer on low heat for 20–25 minutes, spooning sauce over the fish occasionally, until heated through and flavors meld. Serves 10.
