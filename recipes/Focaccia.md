# Focaccia

*Source: null*

## Ingredients

- 50 g to 100 g sourdough starter
- 10 g salt
- 428 g water
- 512 g bread flour
- Olive oil for drizzling
- Coarse sea salt
- Fresh, chopped rosemary

## Instructions

1. Whisk the starter and water together in a large bowl.
2. Mix in salt and flour, stirring with a spatula until incorporated.
3. Perform one fold 30 minutes after mixing the dough, folding 8 to 10 times.
4. Drizzle with a splash of olive oil and rub the surface to coat.
5. Cover the bowl and set aside to rise at room temperature until the dough reaches 50% rise.
6. Pour about 30ml of olive oil into a 9×13-inch pan.
7. Wet the dough with a tablespoon of olive oil, gently deflate it, and scoop into the center of the oil in the prepared pan.
8. Fold the dough in from all sides to the center and rub the top with oil.
9. Leave uncovered for 4 to 6 hours until puffy and nearly doubled.
10. Heat the oven to 218°C.
11. Rub your hands lightly with oil and use all ten fingers to press gently into the dough to dimple and stretch it to nearly fit the pan.
12. Sprinkle generously with sea salt and rosemary.
13. Transfer the pan to the oven and bake for about 25 minutes until golden all around.
