# Soft Sourdough Sandwich Bread

## Ingredients

- 300 grams sourdough starter
- 650 g warm water
- 50 g honey
- 40 g avocado oil
- 20 g salt
- 1000 g bread flour

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients and let rest for one hour.
2. Perform four stretch and folds thirty minutes apart.
3. Flour the dough, cover it, and place in the refrigerator overnight for eight to twenty-four hours.
4. Remove the dough from the refrigerator and let it rest at room temperature for about two hours.
5. Prepare two loaf pans and divide the dough in half.
6. Roll and flatten the first loaf into a rectangle, then roll it into a log and place it into a pan.
7. Repeat with the remaining dough and score the tops.
8. Cover the loaves with a damp towel and rest for one hour.
9. Preheat the oven to 205°C and spray the tops of the loaves with water.
10. Loosely cover the loaves with foil and bake for thirty-five minutes.
11. Remove the foil, reduce the oven temperature to 175°C, and bake for another fifteen minutes until the internal temperature reaches 93°C.
12. Allow the loaves to cool before serving.
