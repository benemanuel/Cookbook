# Mozzarella

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups water, divided
- 1½ tsp citric acid
- ¼ rennet tablet
- 1 gallon whole milk or 2% milk (not ultra-pasteurized/UHT)
- 5ml kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Dissolve citric acid in 240ml cold water and dissolve rennet in 60ml cold water, then set aside.
2. Pour milk into a large pot, add citric acid solution and stir well, then heat over medium heat to 32°C (32°C) while stirring gently.
3. Remove from heat, add the rennet solution and stir gently for 30 seconds, then cover and let sit for 5 minutes undisturbed.
4. Check the curd to ensure it looks like soft tofu, letting it sit another 5 minutes if too soft, then cut the curd into ½-inch cubes with a knife.
5. Return pot to medium heat, heat curds to 41°C (40°C) while gently stirring, then remove from heat and continue stirring slowly for 2–3 minutes.
6. Scoop the curds into a microwave-safe bowl using a slotted spoon, draining off as much whey as possible and reserving the whey.
7. Microwave curds on high for 1 minute, drain off excess whey, and fold curds over themselves several times.
8. Microwave again for 30 seconds, drain whey and fold again, then repeat once more until the cheese reaches 57°C (57°C) and is smooth and stretchy.
9. Add salt and knead it into the cheese.
10. Stretch and fold the cheese until smooth and glossy, like taffy.
11. Form into a ball or desired shape, then place in cool water or salted whey to firm up, and use immediately or refrigerate.
