# Mozzarella

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups water, divided
- 1½ tsp citric acid
- ¼ rennet tablet
- 1 gallon whole milk or 2% milk (not ultra-pasteurized/UHT)
- 1 tsp kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Dissolve citric acid in 1 cup cold water. Dissolve rennet in ¼ cup cold water. Set aside.
2. Pour milk into a large pot. Add citric acid solution and stir well. Heat over medium heat to 90°F (32°C), stirring gently.
3. Remove from heat. Add the rennet solution and stir gently for 30 seconds. Cover and let sit for 5 minutes undisturbed.
4. Check the curd: it should look like soft tofu. If it's too soft, let sit another 5 minutes. Cut the curd into ½-inch cubes with a knife.
5. Return pot to medium heat. Heat curds to 105°F (40°C) while gently stirring. Remove from heat and continue stirring slowly for 2–3 minutes.
6. Scoop the curds into a microwave-safe bowl using a slotted spoon, draining off as much whey as possible. Reserve the whey.
7. Microwave curds on high for 1 minute. Drain off excess whey. Fold curds over themselves several times.
8. Microwave again for 30 seconds. Drain whey and fold again. Repeat once more until the cheese reaches 135°F (57°C) and is smooth and stretchy.
9. Add salt and knead it into the cheese.
10. Stretch and fold the cheese until smooth and glossy, like taffy.
11. Form into a ball or desired shape. Place in cool water or salted whey to firm up. Use immediately or refrigerate. Makes approximately 1 lb.
