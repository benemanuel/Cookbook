# PICKLED ARTICHOKES

## Ingredients

- 4 lemons (juice used for prep and brine)
- 1 cup white vinegar
- ½ cup olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 dried chiles
- A dozen juniper berries (optional)
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 tsp sea salt
- 25 baby artichokes

## Instructions

1. Prepare an acidulated water bath: fill a large bowl with cold water and squeeze in the juice of 2 lemons. Drop in the squeezed lemon halves.
2. Trim the baby artichokes: remove the tough outer leaves, cut off the top third, trim the stem, and halve or quarter them. Immediately drop into the lemon water to prevent browning.
3. Make the brine: in a large saucepan, combine vinegar, olive oil, juice of remaining 2 lemons, garlic, dried chiles, juniper berries, bay leaf, and sea salt. Bring to a boil.
4. Drain the artichokes and add to the boiling brine. Cook for 5–7 minutes until just tender but not mushy.
5. Pack artichokes into sterilized mason jars, ladle brine over them, and seal. Process in a water bath for 15 minutes for shelf-stable storage, or refrigerate immediately. Makes approximately 1 quart.
