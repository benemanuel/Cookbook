# Pickled Artichokes

## Ingredients

- 4 lemons (juice used for prep and brine)
- 240ml white vinegar
- 120ml olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 dried chiles
- A dozen juniper berries (optional)
- 1 bay leaf
- 10ml sea salt
- 25 baby artichokes

## Instructions

1. Prepare an acidulated water bath by filling a large bowl with cold water, squeezing in the juice of 2 lemons, and dropping in the squeezed lemon halves.
2. Trim the baby artichokes by removing the tough outer leaves, cutting off the top third, trimming the stem, and halving or quartering them, then immediately drop into the lemon water to prevent browning.
3. Make the brine by combining vinegar, olive oil, juice of remaining 2 lemons, garlic, dried chiles, juniper berries, bay leaf, and sea salt in a large saucepan and bringing to a boil.
4. Drain the artichokes and add to the boiling brine, then cook for 5–7 minutes until just tender but not mushy.
5. Pack artichokes into sterilized mason jars, ladle brine over them, and seal, then process in a water bath for 15 minutes for shelf-stable storage or refrigerate immediately.
