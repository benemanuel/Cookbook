# Mac and Cheese

## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons flour
- 2 cups milk (low-fat is OK)
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon hot sauce (optional)
- 1 teaspoon yellow mustard, or ½ teaspoon mustard powder
- Ground black pepper to taste
- Salt to taste
- 2¼ cups water
- ½ pound elbow macaroni, uncooked
- 2 cups (½ pound) shredded cheddar cheese
- ¾ cup (3 ounces) shredded mozzarella cheese

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the flour and cook for 1 minute, stirring frequently, until the flour is lightly cooked.
3. Whisk about half of the milk into the flour mixture until it becomes a thick roux.
4. Add the remaining milk and whisk until smooth.
5. Add the smoked paprika, garlic powder, hot sauce (if using), yellow mustard or mustard powder, pepper, and salt, and stir to combine.
6. Add the elbow macaroni and stir occasionally until the mixture comes to a simmer.
7. Cook the pasta at a simmer, stirring often, for 8-11 minutes or until the sauce thickens and the macaroni is just cooked al dente.
8. Turn off the heat and stir in the cheddar and mozzarella cheeses.
9. Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper to taste.
