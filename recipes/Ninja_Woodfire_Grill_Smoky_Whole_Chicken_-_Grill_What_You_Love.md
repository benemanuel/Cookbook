# Ninja Woodfire Grill Smoky Whole Chicken

*Source: grillwhatyoulove.com*

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken, 3-4 pounds
- 2 tablespoons Montreal Chicken Seasoning or your favorite chicken rub
- Wood pellets (apple, cherry, or pecan recommended)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your Ninja Woodfire outdoor grill to the Grill setting on high heat.
2. Add pellets to the hopper and press the Woodfire button to add smoke.
3. Set the cook time for 35 to 40 minutes.
4. Spatchcock the chicken by placing it breast side down, cutting along both sides of the backbone with kitchen shears, removing the backbone, and pressing the chicken flat.
5. Pat the chicken skin completely dry with paper towels.
6. Sprinkle both sides generously with Montreal Chicken Seasoning.
7. Place the chicken on the grill skin-side down and cook for 5 to 6 minutes to sear the skin.
8. Flip the chicken over and reduce the heat to low.
9. Continue cooking the chicken cut-side down for 15 to 20 minutes.
10. Flip the chicken back to skin-side down and continue cooking until the internal temperature reaches 160°F.
11. Remove the chicken from the grill and let it rest for a few minutes so the juices can redistribute.
12. Carve and serve.
