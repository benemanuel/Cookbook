# Easy One-Bowl Yougrt Coffee Cake Recipe

## Ingredients

### Streusel Topping
- 115g (½ cup packed) brown sugar
- 70g (½ cup) whole wheat flour
- 1 Tbsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp salt
- Shy ¼ tsp cardamom
- 55g (4 Tbsp) unsalted butter, melted

### Cake
- 395g (2 cups) sugar
- 170g (¾ cup) unsalted butter, softened
- 4 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ oz vanilla extract
- 3 eggs
- 340g (2⅔ cups) all-purpose flour
- 395g (1⅔ cups) plain Greek yogurt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 baking pan.
2. Make the streusel: combine brown sugar, flour, cinnamon, salt, cardamom, and melted butter. Mix until crumbly and set aside.
3. In a large bowl, beat together sugar, butter, baking powder, salt, and vanilla until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each.
4. Alternately fold in flour and Greek yogurt, beginning and ending with flour, until just combined.
5. Pour batter into prepared pan. Sprinkle streusel evenly over the top.
6. Bake for approximately 40 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool before serving.
