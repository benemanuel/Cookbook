# Lemon Buttermilk Muffins with Greek Yogurt & Honey

## Ingredients

- 200 ml buttermilk
- 60 g oat flakes
- 200 g wholemeal flour (e.g., spelt)
- 2 eggs
- 10ml regular baking powder
- 80 g whole cane sugar
- 5ml pure vanilla extract
- 100 ml canola oil
- 2 organic lemons (zest and juice)
- 2–15ml full-fat Greek yogurt (10%)
- 15ml honey

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (fan/convection) and place paper muffin liners in your tray.
2. Wash the lemons, grate the zest, then squeeze out the juice and set both aside.
3. In a bowl, mix buttermilk, oat flakes, and lemon zest and let it sit for about 5–10 minutes to soften.
4. Slice the vanilla pod and scrape out the seeds, or use vanilla extract.
5. Beat the eggs, then stir in the soaked oat mixture, sugar, vanilla, canola oil, and lemon juice.
6. In a separate bowl, whisk together the wholemeal flour and baking powder, then add to the wet mixture and stir until just combined without overmixing.
7. Divide the batter evenly between the muffin cups and bake on the middle rack for 20–25 minutes, or until golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean.
8. Let the muffins cool in the tray for 5 minutes, then remove and cool completely on a rack.
9. In a small bowl, mix Greek yogurt with honey until smooth and spoon or pipe a little on top of each muffin before serving.
