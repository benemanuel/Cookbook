# Ginger Snaps with Brandy Cream

## Ingredients

### Biscuits
- 50g butter
- 50g caster sugar
- 30ml golden syrup (30ml)
- 50g plain flour
- 5ml ground ginger (5ml)
- Lard for greasing

### Brandy Cream Filling
- 150ml double cream
- 30ml brandy (30ml)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (Gas Mark 4).
2. Melt butter, caster sugar and syrup in a saucepan over low heat. Remove from heat and stir in sieved flour and ginger. Allow to cool until the side of the pan can be touched comfortably.
3. Thoroughly grease 2 baking trays and the handles of 2 wooden spoons with lard. Place heaped teaspoons of the biscuit mixture at least 10cm apart on one tray (max 4 per tray).
4. Bake for about 5 minutes until bubbling and evenly golden. Remove from the oven and cool for a few minutes until almost set.
5. Meanwhile, place 4 more teaspoons of mixture on the second tray and bake as before.
6. Quickly lift a cooling biscuit and wrap it around a wooden spoon handle with the bubbled side uppermost. Allow to set. Repeat with the remaining biscuits. (If biscuits harden before shaping, return to the oven briefly to soften.)
7. Continue cooking and shaping until all the mixture is used.
8. Whip the double cream and brandy together until thick. Fill the cold biscuit rolls with the brandy cream.

## Notes

- Unfilled ginger snaps can be stored in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks, or frozen for several months.
