# ׃tuna steak

## Ingredients

30ml orange juice
30ml soy sauce
15ml. olive oil
8ml lemon juice
15ml chopped fresh parsley
1/3 clove garlic
1ml chopped fresh oregano
1/4 ground black pepper
2  115 gr tuna steaks

## Instructions



Short marinade of no more than 20 minutes

Breadcrumb the tuna before skillet

hot pan for 3\-4 minutes on each side.
