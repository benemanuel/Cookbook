# ׃tuna steak

## Ingredients

2 tbsp orange juice
2 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp. olive oil
1.5 tsp lemon juice
1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
1/3 clove garlic
1/4 tsp chopped fresh oregano
1/4 ground black pepper
2  115 gr tuna steaks

## Instructions



Short marinade of no more than 20 minutes

Breadcrumb the tuna before skillet

hot pan for 3\-4 minutes on each side.
