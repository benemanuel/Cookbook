# Best Sourdough Pizza Crust (No Steel or Stone!)

*Source: The Clever Carrot*

## Ingredients

- 50 g active sourdough starter
- 250 g water
- 360 g bread flour
- 7 g fine sea salt
- 240 g pizza sauce (quick no-cook, Sicilian tomato sauce, or pomodoro sauce)
- Fine sea salt
- Black pepper
- Handful fresh basil
- Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- Olive oil
- 226 g whole-milk mozzarella block cheese, shredded (about 2 1/2 cups)

## Instructions

1. In the evening, combine the sourdough starter and water in a large mixing bowl and mix with a fork.
2. Add the flour and salt and continue mixing, then finish by hand to fully incorporate the flour.
3. Cover with plastic wrap or a clean kitchen towel and let rest for 30 minutes at room temperature.
4. Return to the bowl and fold the dough over itself several times to create a rough ball.
5. Transfer the dough to a lightly oiled 2 quart dough tub, cover, and let rise overnight at room temperature (about 68 F) for 10-12+ hours until it has almost doubled in size.
6. Coat a non-stick 9x13-inch baking pan with 1 tablespoon of olive oil and rub the bottom and sides.
7. Remove the dough directly into the pan and with oiled fingertips, gently dimple and stretch the dough to reach the corners and sides of the pan.
8. Cover the pan with an inverted baking sheet and rest in a warm spot (such as a proofing box set to 78F or a turned off oven with the light on) for 30 minutes.
9. Return to the pan and dimple the dough again, gently lifting and stretching it as you go, then cover and rest for another 30 minutes while preheating the oven to 450 F.
10. Place the baking pan on the bottom rack and bake the dough at 450 F for 15-18 minutes, or until the surface is set and the bottom crust is light golden brown.
11. Top the par-baked pizza crust with tomato sauce spread over the entire surface including the crust.
12. Season with salt and pepper, add fresh basil leaves, a fresh grating of Parmesan cheese, and drizzle with olive oil.
13. Sprinkle with mozzarella cheese, taking it to the corners and sides for crispy edges, and tuck any remaining cheese cubes into the corners.
14. Bake at 450 F on the middle or top rack until the cheese is melted and broiled in some spots, about 12-15 minutes, or place under the broiler to crisp it up further.
