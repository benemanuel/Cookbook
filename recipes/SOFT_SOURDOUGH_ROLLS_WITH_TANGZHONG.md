# Soft Sourdough Rolls with Tangzhong

## Ingredients

- 145 g whole wheat flour
- 160 g כפרי flour
- 500 g bread flour
- 35 g all-purpose flour
- 530 g water
- 175 g milk
- 220 g levain (100% hydration)
- 45 g butter
- 30 g honey
- 16 g salt

## Instructions

1. Combine 35 g flour and 175 g milk, then cook until thick pudding consistency and cool completely for the tangzhong.
2. Mix the remaining 805 g flour, 355 g water, and all tangzhong until shaggy and rest for 1-4 hours for the autolyse.
3. Add 220 g levain, 16 g salt, and 30 g honey to the autolyse and mix until combined and moderately developed.
4. Add 45 g butter in pieces during the first fold, then perform 2-3 more folds every 30 minutes and bulk until approximately 40-50% rise.
5. Divide dough into 15 portions of approximately 120 g each, shape into balls, and refrigerate overnight covered.
6. Bake at 200°C for 15-18 minutes, optionally brushing with egg wash before baking and butter after baking.
