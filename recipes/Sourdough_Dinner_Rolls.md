# Soft Sourdough Discard Dinner Rolls

*Source: Scotch & Scones*

## Ingredients

- 2¾ cups all-purpose flour or bread flour
- 240ml milk, warmed between 38°C to 43°C
- 120ml sourdough starter discard, at room temperature
- 60ml potato flour
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 90ml unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 30ml granulated sugar
- 1½ teaspoons instant yeast or rapid rise
- 1½ teaspoons kosher salt

## Instructions

1. In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook, combine all-purpose flour, milk, sourdough starter discard, potato flour, egg, unsalted butter, granulated sugar, instant yeast, and kosher salt.
2. Knead on medium speed until a soft, slightly sticky dough forms, adding flour or water in 15ml increments until the dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl.
3. Keep the dough in the mixer and knead for an additional 2 minutes or by hand on a lightly floured surface until the dough is soft and tacky.
4. Lightly spray a large bowl with canola oil spray, place the dough in the bowl, turning it to coat all sides in the oil, cover the bowl, and allow the dough to rise in a warm place for 1 to 2 hours or until double in size.
5. Spray two 9-inch round cake pans with canola oil spray.
6. When the dough has risen, knead it gently to deflate it, divide the dough into 16 equal pieces with a bench scraper, shape each piece into a smooth ball, and place 8 in each prepared baking pan, spacing them out evenly.
7. Cover the shaped rolls and let them rise until they're puffy, about 1 hour, while adjusting the oven rack to a lower third placement and preheating the oven to 177°C.
8. Bake the rolls for 20 to 25 minutes or until they're golden brown on top, rotating the pan halfway through, and loosely cover the pans with aluminum foil if the tops are browning too quickly.
9. Remove the pans from the oven and allow the rolls to cool for a few minutes before serving.
