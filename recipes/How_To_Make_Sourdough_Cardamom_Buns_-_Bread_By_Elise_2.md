# Sourdough Cardamom Buns

*Source: Bread By Elise*

## Ingredients

- 45 g active sourdough starter 100% hydration, white
- 45 g whole milk cold
- 45 g all-purpose or bread flour
- 10 g sugar
- 550 g bread flour 11-13% protein
- 90 g sugar
- 10 g salt
- 2 g ground cardamom (1 tsp)
- 250 g whole milk cold
- 2 eggs small-medium sized
- 145 g sweet levain
- 110 g unsalted butter softened, cut into cubes
- 100 g unsalted butter softened
- 90 g brown sugar
- 2.5 g ground cardamom (1.5 tsp)
- 50 g brown sugar
- 50 g water
- 1 g ground cardamom (0.5 tsp)

## Instructions

1. Mix active sourdough starter, milk, flour, and sugar in a medium-sized bowl, cover, and rise in a warm spot for 4 hours or until the levain has doubled or tripled in size.
2. Add all main dough ingredients except the butter to the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook and mix on medium speed until the dough comes together.
3. Cover and autolyse the dough for 30 minutes.
4. Start the mixer again and knead on medium speed for 5-10 minutes.
5. Slowly add in the softened butter, one cube at a time, while the mixer is running.
6. Continue kneading at medium speed for 15-25 minutes until the dough passes the window-pane test.
7. Place the dough in a clean bowl and proof in a warm spot at around 78°F/26°C for two hours.
8. Transfer the dough to the fridge to cold-proof for a minimum of six hours.
9. Mix softened butter, brown sugar, and cardamom in a bowl until you have a spreadable paste and leave at room temperature until ready to shape the buns.
10. Transfer the dough to a lightly floured work surface and leave at room temperature for 10-15 minutes if it is very cold and difficult to roll out.
11. Roll the dough into a 19x12in/50x30cm rectangle with a rolling pin.
12. Evenly spread out the cardamom filling using an offset spatula.
13. Grab one of the long sides of the rectangle and fold it down to cover 2/3 of the dough, then grab the other side and fold it over the top.
14. Go over the dough a couple of times with a rolling pin to even out the sides.
15. Use a sharp knife to cut the dough into 12 strips, roughly 1.5-2 inches each.
16. Split each strip into three, leaving one side joined, and braid the strips to create a three-stranded plait.
17. Grab the end of the plait and roll it up to create a bun, then place all the shaped buns in a greased muffin tin.
18. Loosely cover the muffin tin with cling film and proof at around 78°F/26°C for 9-12 hours until the buns have doubled in size.
19. Preheat your oven to 355°F/180°C and bake the cardamom buns for 18-20 minutes until golden brown.
20. While the buns are baking, add brown sugar, water, and ground cardamom to a small pot and heat over medium heat until all the sugar is dissolved.
21. When the buns are out of the oven, immediately brush over the syrup using a pastry brush.
22. Transfer to a cooling rack and cool for 20-30 minutes before serving.
