# How I Bake Sourdough Bread

## Ingredients

- 55 gr flour
- 55 ml water
- sourdough starter
- 388 gr flour
- 8 g salt
- 257 ml water
- rice flour

## Instructions

1. At 21:00 in the evening, add 55 gr flour and 55 ml water to your starter jar.
2. Put Dutch Oven in oven to turn on automatically at 06:15 for 1 hour at 220°C.
3. Prepare and premix 388 gr flour and 8 g salt in a bowl, keeping 257 ml water ready for the morning.
4. At 07:15, take dough from fridge and banneton, make a cut, and put it into the oven covered.
5. Mix the pre-prepared flour, water, starter, and salt to make dough.
6. At 07:35, uncover bread in oven and do a quick knead for about a minute.
7. At 08:05, take out the loaf and give the dough one major stretch and fold before shaping it into a dough ball and placing it back in its bowl.
8. At 13:20, do a quick pre-shape and rest for 10 minutes.
9. At 13:30, shape for the basket, quick roll in rice flour, and place it in a dusted banneton.
10. At 15:00, once dough is expanding and feels springy, pop it into the fridge.
11. Wash and dry the bowl and prepare 55 gr flour and 55 ml water.
