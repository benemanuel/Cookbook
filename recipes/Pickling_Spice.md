# Pickling Spice

## Ingredients

- 30ml black peppercorns
- 30ml mustard seeds
- 30ml coriander seeds
- 30ml crushed red pepper flakes
- 30ml allspice berries
- 15ml ground mace
- 2 small cinnamon sticks, crushed or broken into pieces
- 2 to 4 bay leaves, crumbled
- 30ml whole cloves
- 15ml ground ginger

## Instructions

1. Combine all spices together in a container for use in pickling recipes.
