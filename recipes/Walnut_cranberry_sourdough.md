# Walnut Cranberry Sourdough

## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 489 g bread flour
- 61 g dark rye flour
- 61 g spelt flour
- 122 g sourdough starter
- 444 g water
- 93 g toasted walnuts
- 23 g walnut oil
- 13 g salt

**Soaker**
- 93 g dried sweet cranberries
- 80 g bourbon

## Instructions

1. Add bread flour, dark rye flour, spelt flour, sourdough starter, water, and walnut oil to a bowl, then mix with your hands until no dry flour remains.
2. Leave the dough covered for an hour to develop the gluten.
3. Add cranberries and bourbon to a small bowl and leave them to infuse until needed, or use warm water for an alcohol-free version.
4. Heat the oven to 180°C/350°F with fan assist and line a baking sheet with baking paper.
5. Toast the walnuts in the oven until fragrant and light brown, then chop them coarsely.
6. Over the next 1½ hours, stretch and fold the dough every 30 minutes.
7. During the second set of stretch and folds, include a quarter of the drained cranberries and a quarter of the walnuts, repeating this with each fold and using Rubaud mixing after the second set to ensure they are properly mixed in.
8. After the third set, check gluten development using the windowpane test and add another set after 30 minutes if needed.
9. Add the entire dough to a see-through container with straight sides and level the top.
10. Mark the top of the dough on the outside of the container and mark where the dough will have increased by 25%.
11. Let the dough grow by 25% at 30°C/86°F.
12. When the dough has grown, divide it into two equally sized pieces.
13. Shape the balls into bâtards or boules and place them in floured bannetons.
14. Place the shaped bread in the fridge for at least 8 hours but up to 48 hours at 2°C/36°F.
15. Preheat the oven to 260°C/500°F with a baking steel and dutch oven inside for one hour.
16. Flour the bottom of the first dough with rice flour, flip it onto a peel, and score it with a lame or sharp knife.
17. Put the dough into the bottom of the dutch oven, cover with the lid, and bake for 20 minutes.
18. Remove the lid, turn down the oven to 230°C/450°F, and bake for another 25 minutes until golden and crispy.
19. Remove the bread from the oven and cool on a wire rack for a couple of hours until it reaches room temperature.
20. Bake the second loaf the same way.
