# Pickled Tongue Recipe

*Source: seriouseats.com*

## Ingredients

- 1 calves or beef tongue
- 3 quarts water
- 9 ounces brown sugar
- 27 ounces coarse sea salt
- 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
- 1 teaspoon juniper berries
- 2 cloves
- 1 bouquet garni
- 1 carrot, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 celery stalk, chopped
- 1 garlic bulb, halved

## Instructions

1. Put all the ingredients from the brine into a large saucepan and stir well over low heat until the sugar and salt have dissolved.
2. Bring the brine to a boil, then turn off the heat and leave to cool.
3. Place the tongue in a plastic container and pour the brine over the tongue, using something heavy such as a ceramic bowl to weigh down the tongue in the liquid.
4. Refrigerate for 4 to 5 days.
5. On the fifth day, remove the tongue from the brine and soak in fresh water overnight in the refrigerator.
6. Place the salted tongue in a pot along with the simmering ingredients.
7. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low and simmer for 3.5 to 5 hours, depending on the kind of tongue and the size.
8. Peel away the rubbery lining when the tongue is very soft.
9. Serve the tongue, both hot and cold, with horseradish and mustard.
