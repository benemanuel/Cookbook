# English Muffins

## Ingredients

- 250g bread flour
- 100g AP flour
- 50g semolina flour
- 50g whole wheat flour
- 50g spelt flour
- 310g water
- 100g levain
- 20g salt

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, water, levain and salt for 5 minutes in a mixer or by hand.
2. Ferment at room temperature for 6 hours.
3. Cold ferment for 15 hours or more.
4. Turn the dough onto a floured work surface.
5. Stretch or roll the dough to a 1cm thickness.
6. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper for proofing and top with semolina or corn meal.
7. Use a baking ring 7.5-10cm in diameter and cut out pieces.
8. Put leftover dough back together, roll out and cut out additional muffins.
9. Proof for 1 hour.
10. Heat a cast iron skillet on low to medium heat.
11. Dust the cast iron skillet with semolina or corn meal.
12. Bake each muffin 3-4 minutes per side.
