# Sourdough Bread

*Source: jamieoliver.com*

## Ingredients

- 50g mature sourdough starter
- 50g strong white bread flour
- 100g levain starter
- 400g organic strong white bread flour
- 100g organic whole wheat flour
- ½ tablespoon fine salt
- fine semolina flour, for dusting
- semolina, for dusting

## Instructions

1. The night before you want to bake, make the levain starter by pouring 50ml of tepid water into a large mixing bowl and gently stirring in the mature sourdough starter until fully dissolved, then repeat with the flour until smooth and combined.
2. Leave the levain covered in a warm place for at least 8 hours until lots of bubbles appear on the surface and the dough has a milky-sweet aroma.
3. The next day, pour 325ml of tepid water into a large mixing bowl and add 100g of the levain, which should float, then gently stir it into the water until fully dissolved, and repeat with both flours, covering with a damp cloth and resting in a warm place for 1 hour.
4. Add the salt and 25ml of tepid water, scrunching them into the dough until fully combined, then set aside covered in a warm place for 30 minutes.
5. Wet your hand slightly and give the dough four folds in the bowl, one at each corner, and repeat this process another three times at 30-minute intervals, turning the dough four times in total across 2 hours, then cover and leave to rise in a warm place for another 2 hours.
6. Tip the dough onto a clean surface and gently dust the top with a handful of semolina flour, then roughly shape into a round while keeping as much air in the dough as possible, and rest covered for 30 minutes.
7. Dust a basket or cane banneton with semolina flour or use a medium bowl lined with a clean floured tea towel, then lightly flour the dough and flip it over so the flour side is on the worktop, gently shape into a round, and place it in your floured basket, banneton or tea towel-lined bowl.
8. Cover the dough with a shower cap or oiled cling film and allow to rest for 1 to 2 hours until increased in size by a quarter and looking bubbly, then transfer your dough in its basket or bowl to the fridge to rise for another 12 to 16 hours until bubbly and risen by another quarter.
9. Place a heavy lidded casserole pot on the bottom shelf of the oven and preheat to 240°C/464°F/gas 9.
10. Bring your loaf out of the fridge and scatter semolina over the top, then carefully remove the hot pot from the oven, take off the lid and gently tip your dough out into the pot so the pattern is the right way up, score it with a sharp knife held at a 40 degree angle to the surface of the dough, and carefully cover and return to the oven.
11. Reduce the temperature to 230°C/446°F/gas 8 and bake for 30 minutes, then carefully remove the lid and bake for a final 20 minutes until a malted golden brown, and remove to a wire rack to cool.
