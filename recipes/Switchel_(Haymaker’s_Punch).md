# Switchel (Haymaker's Punch)

*Source: null*

## Ingredients

- 1.5 liters cold water
- 30 ml raw apple cider vinegar with mother
- 10-15 grams fresh ginger, grated (or 1-2 grams ground ginger)
- 30 ml blackstrap molasses or pure maple syrup or raw honey
- Juice of ½ lemon or lime, optional (approximately 15 ml)
- 1-2 pinches sea salt, optional (for electrolytes)
- Herbs such as mint or basil, optional
- Spices such as cinnamon or turmeric, optional

## Instructions

1. Boil 125 ml of water and add grated ginger, simmering for 2-5 minutes.
2. Let the ginger brew cool slightly and strain if desired.
3. In a large pitcher or bottle, combine 30 ml apple cider vinegar, 30 ml molasses, syrup, or honey, optional lemon or lime juice, optional herbs or spices, optional salt, and the strained ginger tea.
4. Add the remaining 1375 ml of cold water to the pitcher.
5. Stir or shake the mixture well.
6. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour or overnight for full flavor development.
7. Shake or stir before pouring and serve cold over ice, or mix with sparkling water if desired.
