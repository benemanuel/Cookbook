# Sourdough Glass Bread (Pan de Cristal)

*Source: breadtopia.com*

## Ingredients

- 400g bread flour
- 360g water
- 120g ripe sourdough starter
- 14g olive oil
- 11g salt

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour and water together in a bowl, cover, and let sit for 1-2 hours.
2. Add the sourdough starter, salt, and oil to the dough and fold the dough over itself, pressing and stretching to work in these ingredients.
3. Give the dough a few minutes of Rubaud mixing to strengthen and fully mix it, then cover and let it rest 30 minutes.
4. Do four rounds of gluten development such as coil folding, with 30-minute rests in between, transferring the dough to a straight-walled container to track the rise.
5. Let the dough rise until it has expanded by 70-80%, about 5 hours after mixing in the sourdough starter at warm temperatures.
6. Optionally retard the dough by refrigerating it overnight at about 60% dough expansion, then let it warm up for about an hour in the morning before shaping.
7. Prep a piece of parchment paper the size of your baking stone or steel and heavily flour your work surface.
8. Gently scrape the dough onto the floured surface and use a bench knife to stretch the dough into a rectangle about 1 inch thick, ensuring none of it is stuck.
9. Heavily flour the top of the dough and cut it into two long narrow strips.
10. Sprinkle flour on the cut surfaces and ensure they are not sticking to your work surface.
11. Use a bench knife and dough scraper to scoop up both ends of a dough piece and transfer it to the parchment paper, stretching it out like an accordion as you lay it down.
12. Repeat with the other dough piece, flour the tops, and cover with a tea towel.
13. Let proof for about 1 hour or longer if your dough is cool.
14. Set up your oven with a baking stone or steel on the second-to-lowest shelf and a cast iron pan on the highest possible shelf.
15. Preheat this oven setup for about 45 minutes at 500F.
16. Brush off any excess flour on the doughs and slide the parchment paper onto a peel or baking sheet with open sides.
17. Boil 1 cup of water and set it near your oven.
18. Load the doughs onto your baking stone, pour the boiling water into the cast iron pan wearing oven mitts, and close the oven door.
19. Bake for 10 minutes at 500F.
20. Lower the oven temperature to 410F and bake for 20 minutes more.
21. Turn off the oven, crack open the oven door, and leave the bread inside for another 10 minutes to help the crust get more crunchy.
