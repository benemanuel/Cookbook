# Sourdough Cardamom Rolls

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 150g stiff starter (≈50% hydration, cold is fine)
- 400g bread flour
- 50g rustic / higher extraction flour
- 280g whole milk (room temp)
- 100g sugar
- 1 egg
- 100g soft butter
- 8g salt
- 3g freshly ground cardamom

### Filling
- 100g soft butter
- 100g sugar
- 4g freshly ground cardamom
- Pinch salt

### Optional Rescue
- 3g instant yeast
- 20g warm milk (≈35°C)

### Optional Syrup
- 100g sugar
- 100g water

## Instructions

1. Mix milk, starter (cut small), and all flour until no dry flour remains, then rest 30 minutes.
2. Add egg, sugar, and cardamom to the dough and mix to combine.
3. Add salt and knead for 5 minutes.
4. Add butter gradually and knead until smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky but not sticky, for a total of 10–12 minutes.
5. Let the dough rise 5–7 hours at 22–23°C room temperature until 60–70% risen with a smoother surface and slight gas visible, giving one fold after 2–3 hours.
6. If fermentation stalls, dissolve 3g yeast in 20g warm milk, knead gently into dough, and ferment at 26–27°C until 60–70% risen.
7. Roll dough to a rectangle approximately 5mm thick.
8. Spread filling over the dough.
9. Fold in thirds in a letter fold, then roll slightly thinner.
10. Cut strips and twist into knots, then place in pan with space.
11. Let sit 45–60 minutes at room temperature until slight puffing begins.
12. Refrigerate 8–14 hours at 4°C for overnight final proof.
13. Let the dough sit 1–2 hours at room temperature the next day until visibly puffy and airy.
14. Bake in a preheated 190°C oven for 18–25 minutes until internal temperature reaches 93–94°C, tenting loosely with foil and reducing heat to 170–175°C if tops darken early.
15. Boil 100g sugar and 100g water together, then brush immediately after baking.
