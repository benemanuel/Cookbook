# Coffee Liqueur

*Source: America's Test Kitchen*

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup medium-roast coffee beans
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cacao nibs
- 1/4 vanilla bean, halved lengthwise
- 8 ounces vodka
- 4 ounces brandy
- 4 ounces simple syrup

## Instructions

1. Place coffee beans, cacao nibs, vanilla bean, vodka, and brandy in a pint-size glass jar, cover tightly, and shake to combine.
2. Store the jar in a cool, dark place for 1 week, shaking the mixture once every other day.
3. Set a fine-mesh strainer in a medium bowl and line it with a triple layer of cheesecloth.
4. Strain the vodka mixture through the prepared strainer and discard the solids.
5. Return the infused strained vodka mixture to a clean jar and add the simple syrup.
6. Cover and gently shake to combine.
7. Store in a cool, dark place for up to 1 year, shaking gently before using.
