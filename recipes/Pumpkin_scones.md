# Pumpkin Scones

## Ingredients

- 250g pumpkin or butternut squash, peeled and seeded
- 400g plain flour
- 20ml baking powder
- 2ml salt
- 85g butter
- 80g parmesan, grated
- 100ml milk
- 100ml water
- 10ml grain mustard
- 1 egg, beaten, for glazing
- Black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Cut the pumpkin into large chunks and steam for 7-10 minutes until tender.
2. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
3. Cut the butter into small chunks and rub it in with your fingertips until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.
4. Stir in the grated parmesan.
5. Roughly crush the cooked pumpkin with a fork or potato masher, taking care not to reduce it to a smooth purée.
6. Lightly stir in the grain mustard and a grinding of black pepper to the pumpkin.
7. Add the pumpkin mixture to the flour mixture, then pour in the milk and water.
8. Stir the liquids into the flour, season with black pepper and mix the ingredients carefully, taking great care not to overmix.
9. Turn the dough out onto a floured board and pat it into a circle roughly 3cm thick.
10. Using a 5-6cm biscuit cutter, make 12 to 14 scones and place them well apart from one another on a baking sheet.
11. Brush the top of each scone with beaten egg, taking care not to let any drip down the sides.
12. Bake for 12-14 minutes until they are golden.
13. Remove the scones from the oven and let them rest briefly before transferring them to a cooling rack.
14. Serve warm, preferably with quince jelly.
