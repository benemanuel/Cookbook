# Apple Cream Pie

## Ingredients

- 9-inch unbaked pie crust
- 5-6 medium apples, pared and quartered
- 15ml lemon juice
- 240ml brown sugar
- 60ml flour
- 2ml cinnamon
- Pinch salt
- 240ml cream

## Instructions

1. Sprinkle lemon juice over apples and place apples rounded side up in pastry-lined pie pan.
2. Combine brown sugar, flour, cinnamon and salt.
3. Sprinkle 180ml of the mixture over the apples.
4. Pour cream over the apples.
5. Sprinkle with remaining brown sugar mixture.
6. Bake at 400° for 10 minutes, then reduce heat to 350° and bake until the apples are tender, 45 to 60 minutes.
