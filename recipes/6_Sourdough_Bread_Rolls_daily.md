# 6 Sourdough Bread Rolls

## Ingredients

- 165 gr sourdough starter (83 gr flour + 83 ml water)
- 582 gr flour (80% whole wheat, bread flour, or similar blend)
- 12 gr salt
- 385 ml water
- Rice flour for dusting

## Instructions

1. At 21:00 in the evening, add 83 gr flour and 83 ml water to your sourdough starter jar to activate it.
2. Place a Dutch Oven in the oven and set it to turn on automatically at 06:15 and preheat to 220°C for 1 hour.
3. Premix the 582 gr flour and 12 gr salt in a bowl and prepare 385 ml water overnight to allow chlorine to dissipate.
4. At 07:15, remove the dough from the fridge, score it, place it in the Dutch Oven covered, and bake covered for 20 minutes.
5. Mix the pre-prepared flour, water, sourdough starter, and salt together to form the dough.
6. At 07:35, uncover the bread in the oven and give the dough a quick 1-minute knead.
7. Bake uncovered for 20 to 30 minutes until the loaf is golden and baked through, then remove from the oven.
8. Perform a lamination by giving the dough one major stretch and fold before shaping it into a dough ball and returning it to the bowl.
9. At 13:20, quick pre-shape the dough and divide it into 6 equal rolls, then let them rest for 10 minutes.
10. At 13:30, shape each roll and roll in rice flour, then place in a dusted banneton basket.
11. At 15:00, once the dough has expanded and feels springy, place the rolls in the fridge until ready to bake.
