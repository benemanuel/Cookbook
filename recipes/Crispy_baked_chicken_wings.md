# Crispy Baked Chicken Wings

*Source: kitchensanctuary.com*

## Ingredients

- 998g (1 kg) chicken wings
- 2 level tbsp aluminium-free baking powder
- 2ml salt
- 2ml pepper
- 300ml (300g) tomato ketchup
- 60ml white wine vinegar
- 75ml dark brown sugar
- 30ml black treacle or molasses
- 45ml honey
- 30ml vegetable oil
- 15ml Worcestershire Sauce
- 2ml mustard powder
- 8ml smoked paprika
- 2ml black pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 120C/121°C and place a rack on a large baking tray.
2. Cut each wing at the joint so you have a mini wing and a drumette.
3. Dry the wings with paper towels, then place in a large bowl and add the baking powder, salt and pepper.
4. Toss to combine and discard any remaining coating once tossed together.
5. Place in a single layer on the rack on the lower shelf of the oven for 30 minutes.
6. After 30 minutes, turn the oven up to 220C/218°C and rotate the tray for even cooking.
7. Cook for a further 45-50 minutes until the wings are lightly golden and crispy.
8. Whilst the wings are baking, place all barbecue sauce ingredients in a saucepan and mix together.
9. Heat over a medium-high heat until boiling, then simmer for 5 minutes and turn off the heat.
10. Toss the barbecue sauce with the wings and serve straightaway.
