# How To Make the Very Best Sweet Cornbread

*Source: kitchn.com*

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (225g) + 2 Tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- 2 cups yellow cornmeal
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 2 large eggs
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup + 2 Tbsp honey, divided

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F and melt 2 Tbsp butter in a 9x13 baking pan in the oven while it preheats.
2. Melt the remaining 2 sticks of butter in a saucepan or microwave and let cool slightly.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together cornmeal, flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together buttermilk, eggs, brown sugar, and ¼ cup honey, then whisk in the melted butter.
5. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and stir until just combined, being careful not to overmix.
6. Pour batter into the hot buttered pan and drizzle remaining 2 Tbsp honey over the top.
7. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
