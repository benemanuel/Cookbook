# Margarita

## Ingredients

- 50 cc Blanco tequila
- 30 cc Lime juice
- 20 cc Triple Sec

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in shaker tin.
2. Add ice to small shaker tin.
3. Shake vigorously.
4. Strain into chilled rocks glass with a salt rim over fresh ice.
5. Garnish with lime and enjoy.
