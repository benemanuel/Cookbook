# Kasha

## Ingredients

- 1 cup kasha (buckwheat groats), coated in 1 egg
- 2 cups boiling liquid (water and Osem seasoning or broth)
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 200–250 g mushrooms, sliced
- 100–120 g egg noodles (dry)
- Olive oil or schmaltz
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a wide pan and cook onions over medium-low heat until deeply golden.
2. Add mushrooms and sauté until their liquid evaporates and they brown, then push to the side.
3. Add egg-coated kasha to the pan, stir and toast for 1–2 minutes until dry, fragrant, and grains separate.
4. Pour in 2 cups boiling liquid, stir once, cover, and cook on low for 10–12 minutes until absorbed.
5. Meanwhile, boil egg noodles until just tender and drain well.
6. Turn off heat, let kasha rest 5 minutes covered, then gently fold in noodles.
7. Add salt as needed, lots of black pepper, and a drizzle of olive oil, then toss gently.
