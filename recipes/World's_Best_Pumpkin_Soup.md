# World's Best Pumpkin Soup

A tried-and-tested favorite from New Zealand, where pumpkin soup is an everyday staple — incredibly easy and fast, and great in an extra-large batch (freeze the extra for an instant meal later).

## Ingredients

- 45 ml butter
- 1 onion, roughly chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/2 of a small pumpkin, peeled & chopped into large cubes
- 1 sweet potato or kumara, peeled & cubed
- 2 stalks celery, chopped
- 4 cups chicken stock

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in the bottom of a big stock pot. Add onion and cook over low heat until soft, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cumin and stir continuously for another minute or so.
2. Add pumpkin, sweet potato, celery, and stock, and bring to a simmer. Cover partially and cook on the stovetop about 20 minutes, until the veggies are soft.
3. Let it cool slightly, then puree in batches in a blender or with an immersion blender. Return to the pot and season to taste.
