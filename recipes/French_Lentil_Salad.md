# French Lentil Salad

## Ingredients

- 200g French green lentils (Puy lentils)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 shallot, finely chopped
- 1–2 carrots, finely diced
- 30ml olive oil
- 15ml red wine vinegar
- 5ml Dijon mustard
- 30ml chopped parsley
- Salt and black pepper
- Optional: celery or fennel, extra herbs

## Instructions

1. Rinse the lentils. Put them in a saucepan with the bay leaf and cover with plenty of water.
2. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently for 20–25 minutes until tender but not mushy.
3. Drain well and discard the bay leaf.
4. While the lentils cook, finely chop the shallot and carrots (and fennel/celery if using).
5. Whisk together olive oil, red wine vinegar, Dijon mustard, salt, and pepper to make the dressing.
6. Toss the warm lentils with the vegetables, parsley, and dressing.
7. Let sit at least 15–30 minutes before serving — it improves as it rests.

## Notes

- Puy lentils are preferred: they stay firm and nutty.
- Some versions lightly sauté the vegetables first in olive oil for a sweeter, softer flavour.
- Excellent with feta, goat cheese, salmon, or a poached egg.
