# Sourdough Cheesy Garlic Pull Apart Bread

*Source: myloveofbaking.com*

## Ingredients

### Levain
- 50g all-purpose flour
- 40g water
- 10g starter

### Dough
- 400g all-purpose flour
- 15ml sugar
- 5ml salt
- 110g salted butter, softened
- 80ml milk, room temperature
- 2 medium eggs, room temperature
- 100g mature levain

### Filling
- 5–6 cloves garlic
- Handful of basil (or other herbs)
- 30ml olive oil
- 60g cheddar, grated
- 60g monterey jack, grated

### Assembly
- 1 egg, for egg wash

## Instructions

1. Make levain 4–6 hours ahead and let it mature.
2. Place all dough ingredients in mixing bowl; mix with paddle at lowest speed until hydrated, then switch to dough hook and knead at medium speed for 3–4 minutes.
3. Cover and bench rest 10–15 minutes, then knead by hand until smooth and elastic.
4. Place in greased bowl, cover, and bulk ferment in a warm place (~27°C) until doubled, about 4–5 hours.
5. Punch down, round up, and refrigerate in airtight container overnight (or at least 2–3 hours for same-day baking).
6. Allow cold dough to soften slightly at room temperature.
7. Blend garlic, basil, and olive oil into a paste; grate cheese.
8. Roll dough into a 30×40cm rectangle on a floured surface.
9. Spread garlic paste evenly; sprinkle cheese on top.
10. Roll lengthwise into a log and place diagonally on a parchment-lined tray.
11. Cut 1-inch sections along the log and fan into a zigzag pattern, keeping pieces connected.
12. Cover and proof in a warm place (~27°C) until puffed, about 3 hours.
13. Preheat oven to 200°C toward end of proof.
14. Egg wash lightly when puffed and nearly doubled.
15. Bake 25–30 minutes until evenly golden brown, rotating the tray at 20 minutes.
