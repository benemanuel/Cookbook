# Slow Cooker Beef Bone Broth

*Source: thehealthyfoodie.com*

## Ingredients

- 3-4 lbs of mixed beef bones (oxtail, knuckles, neckbones and/or short ribs)
- 2  medium carrots
- 3  celery stalks
- 2  medium onions
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1  bay leaf
- 10-12 cups water

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Place the bones in a single layer on a baking sheet or roasting pan; roast for 60 minutes, flipping each bone over half way through cooking time.
3. Place chopped vegetables (don&#39;t even bother peeling them), bay leaf, and vinegar in 7-quart slow cooker. Add roasted bones and cover completely with water.
4. Cook on LOW for 18-36 hours, until deep brown and aromatic.
5. Carefully remove the larger pieces with a slotted spoon and place them in a colander set over a large bowl to collect the excess broth. Then, strain the broth through a fine meshed sieve, or through a strainer that&#39;s been lined with cheesecloth.
6. Transfer the bone broth into jars and refrigerate promptly. When cooled, remove fat layer and save it for cooking.
7. Reheat before serving and season to taste.
