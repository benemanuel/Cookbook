# Classic Moussaka Without Potatoes

## Ingredients

- 500g ground lamb or beef
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- ½ cup red wine (optional)
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp ground allspice
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 2-3 large eggplants (aubergines), sliced into 1 cm thick rounds
- Olive oil for frying or roasting
- 4 tbsp butter
- 4 tbsp flour
- 3 cups milk, warmed
- 2 egg yolks
- ½ cup grated Parmesan or Kefalotyri cheese
- Nutmeg, grated (optional)

## Instructions

1. Salt the eggplant slices and let them sit for about 30 minutes to release excess moisture, then rinse and pat them dry.
2. Brush the eggplant with olive oil and either roast in a preheated oven at 200°C (400°F) for 20-25 minutes until golden, or fry them in olive oil until soft and browned, then set aside.
3. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat and sauté the onions and garlic until softened.
4. Add the ground meat and cook until browned.
5. Stir in the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, red wine (if using), cinnamon, allspice, cumin, bay leaf, salt, and pepper, and let it simmer for 20-30 minutes until the sauce thickens.
6. Remove from heat and stir in chopped parsley, then discard the bay leaf.
7. In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat and whisk in the flour, cooking for 2-3 minutes to make a roux.
8. Gradually add warm milk, whisking constantly to avoid lumps, and continue stirring until the sauce thickens.
9. Remove from heat, then whisk in the egg yolks, cheese, salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
10. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and layer half of the roasted eggplant slices on the bottom of a greased baking dish.
11. Spread the meat sauce evenly over the eggplants.
12. Layer the remaining eggplants on top of the meat sauce.
13. Pour the béchamel sauce over the top, spreading it evenly.
14. Bake in the preheated oven for 45-50 minutes until the top is golden and bubbly.
15. Let it cool for about 10-15 minutes before serving.
