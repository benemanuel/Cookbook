# Sourdough Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Ingredients

- 113 grams (120ml) unsalted butter
- 160 grams brown sugar
- 140 grams granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 80 grams sourdough discard, room temperature (less than 5 days old preferred)
- 11ml pure vanilla extract
- 235 grams all-purpose flour
- 2ml baking soda
- 2ml baking powder
- 4ml fine salt
- 170g chopped dark or semisweet chocolate
- Flaky salt, optional

## Instructions

1. In a small saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat and cook for several minutes until browned, watching for brown flecks and a nutty aroma, then pour the browned butter and all bits into your mixing bowl.
2. Add the sugars and stir to coat, then add sourdough discard and stir to incorporate, followed by the egg and switch to whisking until cohesive.
3. Add vanilla and whisk again.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
5. Add half the flour mixture to the wet mixture and stir with a spatula about 15 times, then add the remaining flour and stir gently until almost all flour is absorbed but not overworked.
6. Add the chopped chocolate and finish mixing the dough.
7. Cover and refrigerate the dough for 30 minutes.
8. Preheat your oven to 191°C and prepare a sheet pan with parchment paper.
9. Scoop 55 gram dough balls onto the prepared pan and bake for about 13 minutes until edges are lightly brown and middles are just set.
10. Remove from oven and tap the pan firmly on the counter twice to deflate the cookies slightly.
11. Let cookies cool on the pan for 5 minutes to finish baking, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
12. Top with a light sprinkle of flaky salt.
