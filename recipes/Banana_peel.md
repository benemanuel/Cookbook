# Banana Peel Flour

## Ingredients

- Ripe banana peels, washed and dried

## Instructions

1. Wash and dry the banana peels.
2. Cut the peels into small pieces.
3. Spread the peel pieces on a dehydrator tray or a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
4. Dehydrate the peels in a dehydrator at 57°C (57°C) for 8-12 hours or in an oven at the lowest setting for 4-6 hours, until the peels are dry and crisp.
5. Grind the dried peels in a blender or food processor until they become a fine powder.
6. Sift the powder through a fine-mesh sieve to remove any large pieces.
7. Store the banana peel flour in an airtight container in a cool, dry place.
