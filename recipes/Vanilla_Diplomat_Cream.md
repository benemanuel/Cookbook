# Vanilla Diplomat Cream

## Ingredients

### Pastry Cream
- 2 cups (480ml) whole milk
- 75g (⅓ cup) sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 35g (¼ cup) cornstarch
- 3 tsp vanilla extract
- 3 egg yolks
- 43g (3 Tbsp) unsalted butter

### Stabilized Chantilly
- 1 tsp unflavored gelatin powder
- 2 Tbsp cold water
- 2 Tbsp + 1 cup heavy cream, divided
- 28g (3½ Tbsp) confectioner's sugar

## Instructions

### Make the Pastry Cream
1. In a saucepan, heat milk with half the sugar and salt over medium heat until steaming.
2. Whisk egg yolks with remaining sugar, cornstarch, and vanilla until pale. Slowly temper in the hot milk, whisking constantly.
3. Return mixture to saucepan and cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until thick and bubbling. Remove from heat, stir in butter.
4. Press plastic wrap directly on the surface and refrigerate until completely cold (at least 2 hours, or overnight).

### Make the Stabilized Chantilly
5. Sprinkle gelatin over 2 Tbsp cold water. Let bloom 5 minutes, then melt gently (microwave 10 seconds or over hot water). Stir in 2 Tbsp heavy cream to cool slightly.
6. Whip 1 cup heavy cream with confectioner's sugar to soft peaks. Drizzle in gelatin mixture and continue whipping to medium-firm peaks.

### Combine
7. Whisk the chilled pastry cream until smooth. Gently fold in the whipped cream in thirds until fully incorporated and fluffy. Use immediately or refrigerate up to 2 days. Serves 16.

Cream needs to be chilled overnight before doing whipped cream
