# Vanilla Diplomat Cream

## Ingredients

- 480ml (480ml) whole milk
- 75g (80ml) sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 35g (60ml) cornstarch
- 15ml vanilla extract
- 3 egg yolks
- 43g (45ml) unsalted butter
- 5ml unflavored gelatin powder
- 30ml cold water
- 30ml heavy cream
- 240ml heavy cream
- 28g (3½ Tbsp) confectioner's sugar

## Instructions

1. Heat milk with half the sugar and salt in a saucepan over medium heat until steaming.
2. Whisk egg yolks with remaining sugar, cornstarch, and vanilla until pale, then slowly temper in the hot milk while whisking constantly.
3. Return mixture to saucepan and cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until thick and bubbling, then remove from heat and stir in butter.
4. Press plastic wrap directly on the surface and refrigerate until completely cold, at least 2 hours or overnight.
5. Sprinkle gelatin over cold water and let bloom for 5 minutes, then melt gently by microwave or over hot water and stir in 30ml heavy cream to cool slightly.
6. Whip 240ml heavy cream with confectioner's sugar to soft peaks, then drizzle in the gelatin mixture and continue whipping to medium-firm peaks.
7. Whisk the chilled pastry cream until smooth and gently fold in the whipped cream in thirds until fully incorporated and fluffy.
8. Use immediately or refrigerate up to 2 days.
