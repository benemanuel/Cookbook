# Lekvar (Prune Filling or Prune Butter)

## Ingredients

- 2 cups dried prunes
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 dash clove
- 1 dash cinnamon
- 1/3 cup brown sugar

## Instructions

1. Combine prunes, water, orange juice, lemon juice, salt, clove, and cinnamon into a saucepan and cook on low heat, stirring every few minutes until the mixture reaches a boil.
2. Lower heat to simmer, cover the pan, and cook for 20 minutes, stirring every few minutes.
3. Remove the lid from the pan and add brown sugar, then cook while reducing the liquid and stirring every few minutes, being careful not to scorch the mixture.
4. Mash the mixture with a wooden spoon until the prunes are soft and broken up and the consistency resembles oatmeal, about 20 more minutes, then allow to cool as it will thicken further.
