# Salt Brine Curing Calculator

*Source: genuineideas.com*

This is a technical reference tool and calculator for salt brining, not a traditional recipe. The page explains two brining methods:

## Equilibrium Brining

Meat is placed in a low concentration brine until the meat's salt level and the brine's salt level average out. This process might take a few days or weeks and is often used for chicken wings or meat slices for grilling. A quarter to half percent salt level is typical for equilibrium brining.

## Gradient Brining

Meat is placed in a 6% or higher salt solution which is strong enough to kill bacteria on the meat's surface. An hour or two in a strong brine acts as a disinfectant and flavors the meat quickly. For example, a large turkey might be brined in a 6% solution for 24 hours.

## Salt Concentration Guidelines

- Below 0.5%: Seasoned perfectly for eating, but too low to kill most bacteria
- 0.5% to 2%: Unwinds, densifies and dries out muscle fibers (classic cured meat texture)
- Above 5%: High enough to kill most bacteria, but too salty to eat without washing or diluting
- Above 10%: Used for longer-term preservation
