# Gluten-Free Vanilla Cake

*Source: Ricardo Cuisine — SPRING 2016*

What gives this almond cake wonderful elasticity is the mixture of egg whites beaten into stiff peaks. Plus, it's so moist no one will ever believe it's gluten-free.

## Ingredients

### Cake
- 150g gluten-free all-purpose flour
- 65g ground almonds
- 3ml baking powder
- 4 eggs, separated
- 1 whole egg
- 125ml canola oil
- 125ml milk
- 10ml vanilla extract
- 210g sugar

### Buttercream
- 4 egg whites
- 5ml vanilla extract
- 265g sugar
- 60ml water
- 340g unsalted butter, cut into cubes, at room temperature

## Instructions

### Cake

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C with the rack in the middle position. Line a 20cm springform pan with parchment paper. Do not butter.
2. In a bowl, combine the gluten-free flour, ground almonds and baking powder. Set aside.
3. In another bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, whole egg, oil, milk, vanilla and 55g of the sugar. Stir in the dry ingredients and mix until smooth.
4. In a third bowl, beat the egg whites with an electric mixer until soft peaks form. Gradually add the remaining sugar and beat until stiff peaks form.
5. Fold half the meringue at a time into the batter, stirring vigorously. Spread into the prepared pan.
6. Bake for about 70 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean. Cool completely on a wire rack before unmoulding.

### Buttercream

1. Place the egg whites and vanilla in a large bowl. Set aside.
2. In a small pot over medium-high heat, bring the sugar and water to a boil. Simmer until a candy thermometer reads 116°C. Remove from heat.
3. Beat the egg whites with an electric mixer on medium speed until frothy. With the machine running, slowly drizzle the hot syrup over the whites (avoiding the beaters). Beat until completely cooled and stiff peaks form.
4. Add the butter 1–2 cubes at a time, beating until smooth and creamy, scraping the bowl regularly.

### Assembly

1. Run a knife between the cake and pan and unmould. Cut the cake horizontally into 2 layers.
2. Frost one layer with one-third of the buttercream. Cover with the second layer.
3. Apply a crumb coat (thin layer of buttercream over the entire cake) and refrigerate for 20 minutes.
4. Completely frost the cake with the remaining buttercream.

## Notes

- No need to be delicate when folding the meringue — it gives the cake its spring, while the baking powder helps it rise.
- Freezes well.
