# Oatmeal Creme Pies

## Ingredients

- 300ml room temperature butter
- 240ml packed dark brown sugar
- 120ml granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 10ml vanilla extract
- 15ml dark molasses
- 360ml all purpose flour
- 5ml baking soda
- 2ml salt
- 4ml cinnamon
- 1ml ground cloves, nutmeg, or pumpkin spice
- 720ml quick cooking oats
- 180ml room temperature butter (for filling)
- 720ml sifted confectionery sugar
- 8ml vanilla extract (for filling)
- Up to 45ml heavy cream
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring the butter to room temperature, or place it in a warm location to soften if you forgot to do so overnight.
2. In one bowl, cream together the 300ml room temperature butter, 240ml packed dark brown sugar, and 120ml granulated sugar.
3. Whip for about a minute and a half until the mixture becomes fluffy and lighter in color.
4. Add one large egg, two teaspoons vanilla extract, and 15ml dark molasses to the butter mixture.
5. In another bowl, sift together 360ml all purpose flour, 5ml baking soda, 2ml salt, 4ml cinnamon, and 1ml ground cloves, nutmeg, or pumpkin spice.
6. Add 720ml quick cooking oats to the dry ingredients and whisk to combine.
7. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients a little at a time, mixing by hand and lightly folding until just combined without overworking the dough.
8. Form equal 85g balls of dough.
9. Bake at 375 degrees for 11 minutes.
10. Remove from the oven and leave the cookies on the baking sheet for 3 minutes before transferring to a cooling rack.
11. For the filling, combine 180ml room temperature butter and 720ml of sifted confectionery sugar in a bowl and mix.
12. Add 8ml vanilla extract and mix.
13. Add up to 45ml heavy cream to reach your preferred consistency.
14. Whip the filling for 4 minutes until fluffy.
15. Add salt to taste, about a pinch or two.
16. Once the cookies are cooled, assemble them into sandwiches with the filling.
