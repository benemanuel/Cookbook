# Fudge Brownies

## Ingredients

- 230 grams chocolate (half semi sweet and half dark)
- 170 grams butter
- 200 grams sugar (half white and half brown)
- 1 tsp salt
- 3 large eggs
- 90 grams all purpose flour
- Vanilla extract (optional)
- 30 grams Dutch processed cocoa powder
- 1 tsp espresso powder
- 100 grams chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Cut the 230 grams of chocolate into chunks.
2. Using a double boiler with simmering water, melt the chocolate along with the butter until it becomes a shiny glossy mixture.
3. Set the chocolate and butter mixture aside to cool down for 30 minutes.
4. Beat the 200g of sugar with salt and add the eggs incrementally, continuing to beat for 5 minutes.
5. Add the chocolate and butter mixture and beat for another minute until everything is combined.
6. Fold in the flour, cocoa powder and espresso powder, folding until you no longer see visible dry mixture.
7. Fold in the chocolate chips and gently transfer the batter into the rectangular pan.
8. Leave the batter in the fridge for at least 24 hours, preferably 48 hours for best results, keeping it in the baking pan.
9. Bake the brownies for 25 minutes at 180°C.
10. After 23 minutes, insert a skewer or toothpick in the middle of the brownies to check doneness.
11. The skewer should come out a little wet with some crumbs clinging to it, which normally happens around the 25 minute mark.
12. If the skewer comes out completely clean, the brownies are overbaked.
13. Allow the brownies to cool down for at least 1 hour before cutting and serving.
