# Early 18th-Century Batter Cake

## Ingredients

- 237.5 g flour
- 142.5 g dark raisins or sultanas
- 83 g melted butter
- 100 g mature stiff sourdough starter (60% hydration)
- 2 large egg yolks
- 100 ml 38% cream
- 35.5 g candied citron, lemon, and orange peel
- 25 ml sack (sherry or sweet cooking wine)
- 24 g sugar
- 1.5 g ground mace
- 1 g ground clove
- 1.5 g grated nutmeg
- pinch of beaten cinnamon
- 2 tbls honey water

## Instructions

1. Soak raisins and candied peel in the sack for 15–30 minutes to enhance flavor and texture.
2. In a large bowl, mix the flour, sugar, spices, and soaked fruits.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the melted butter, cream, egg yolks, and honey water.
4. Add the wet mixture and the mature stiff sourdough starter to the dry ingredients and stir until a thick, cohesive batter forms, adjusting with a small amount of cream or water if needed.
5. Cover and let rise in a warm place for 3–5 hours, or until visibly puffed.
6. Stir down or gently fold the batter to redistribute air pockets and fruit.
7. Line the bottom of a 6-inch baking hoop or springform pan with parchment paper and grease sides if needed.
8. Pour in the batter and smooth the surface.
9. Bake at 180°C (177°C) for 40–50 minutes, or until a skewer inserted in the center comes out clean.
10. Let cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then remove and cool completely on a wire rack.
