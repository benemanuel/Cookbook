# Curd Meat Basturma

## Instructions

1. Hang the meat in the refrigerator on a wire rack at temperatures between 34-40°F (1-4°C) to allow airflow.
2. Check for firmness, which indicates the meat is drying well.
3. Continue the dry curing phase for 10 to 14 days, or until the surface is dry and the meat feels firmer to the touch.
4. Once the meat has fully cured and dried in the refrigerator, cold smoke it at around 70-90°F (21-32°C) for 12-24 hours depending on the smokiness you prefer.
5. Keep the smoking chamber's temperature low to avoid cooking the meat while infusing it with smoke.
6. Ensure the meat hangs freely with good air circulation on all sides throughout the process.
7. Monitor the meat's weight before and after curing to check for moisture loss, aiming for about a 30% weight reduction.
