# Lemon Pound Cake

*Source: Once Upon a Chef*

## Ingredients

- 3 cups all-purpose flour, spooned into measuring cup and leveled-off
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 2 tablespoons packed grated lemon zest
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 2 1/4 cups granulated sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 2 tablespoons water
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice
- 1 cup confectioners' sugar
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and set an oven rack in the middle position.
2. Spray two 8½ x 4½-inch loaf pans with nonstick cooking spray and line the long sides with parchment paper slings, then spray again.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt, then set aside.
4. In another bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, lemon zest, and lemon juice, then set aside.
5. In the bowl of an electric mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, cream the butter and sugar on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 3 to 4 minutes.
6. Beat in the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition and scraping down the sides of the bowl.
7. With the mixer on low speed, alternate adding the flour and buttermilk mixtures, beginning and ending with the flour, then scrape down the sides and mix to ensure all ingredients are well-incorporated.
8. Divide the batter into the prepared pans and smooth with a rubber spatula.
9. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes, or until the top is golden and a tester comes out clean.
10. Cool the cakes in the pans on a wire rack for 10 minutes, then carefully lift them out using the parchment slings and cool for about 1 hour more.
11. Combine the water and sugar in a saucepan and bring to a boil, then remove from heat and stir in the lemon juice.
12. In a medium bowl, whisk together the confectioners' sugar and lemon juice until smooth, adjusting with more sugar or juice as needed for your preferred consistency.
13. Brush the warm syrup all over the cooled cakes, including the sides, letting it soak in as you go.
14. Spoon the glaze over the top of the cakes, letting it drip down the sides, and let sit for about 1 hour to allow the glaze to set before serving.
