# World's Best Pumpkin Soup

*Source: laurelofleaves.com*

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1 onion, roughly chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 teaspoons cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/2 of a small pumpkin, peeled and chopped into large cubes
- 1 sweet potato or kumara, peeled and cubed
- 2 stalks celery, chopped
- 4 cups chicken stock

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in the bottom of a big stock pot.
2. Add onion and cook over low heat until the onion is soft, about 5 minutes.
3. Add garlic and cumin and stir continuously for another minute or so.
4. Add pumpkin, sweet potato, celery, and stock and bring to a simmer.
5. Cover partially and let it cook on the stovetop for about 20 minutes, or until the vegetables are soft.
6. Let it cool slightly, then pour it into the blender in batches or use an immersion blender to puree the soup.
7. Return to the pot and season to taste.
