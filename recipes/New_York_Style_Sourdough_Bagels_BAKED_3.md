# New York Style Sourdough Bagels

*Source: BAKED*

## Ingredients

- 1 cup active sourdough starter
- 1 ¼ cups cold water
- 2 tablespoons barley malt syrup or maple syrup
- 5 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons fine salt
- Flour or semolina for proofing
- 1 tablespoon baking soda
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- ½ cup toppings (poppy seeds, dried onion flakes, dried garlic flakes, sesame seeds, flaky salt, or za'atar)

## Instructions

1. In a stand mixer bowl fitted with a paddle attachment, mix the active starter with water and maple syrup until well combined.
2. Add the flour and salt and switch to a dough hook, kneading on medium until the dough is smooth and pliable, about 6-7 minutes.
3. Shape the dough into a ball, cover with a towel, and rest for 20 minutes.
4. Divide the dough into 8 pieces for large bagels or 10 pieces for smaller ones, shaping each into a ball with surface tension.
5. Cover the dough balls and rest for another 15 minutes.
6. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and sprinkle generously with flour or semolina.
7. Cover the shaped bagels with a towel and let them proof at room temperature for 2.5 to 4.5 hours until puffy, or perform a float test in cold water to check readiness.
8. Once proofed, cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 12 to 24 hours.
9. Preheat the oven to 450°F and place toppings on flat plates.
10. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and add the baking soda and brown sugar.
11. Gently place the bagels a couple at a time into the boiling water and boil for 15 to 20 seconds on each side.
12. Remove boiled bagels with a slotted spoon and immediately dip the top into desired toppings.
13. Return the bagels to the baking sheet and repeat with the remaining bagels.
14. Place the baking sheet in the center of the oven and reduce temperature to 400°F, then bake for 18 to 22 minutes until golden on top.
15. Cool on a wire rack until ready to serve.
