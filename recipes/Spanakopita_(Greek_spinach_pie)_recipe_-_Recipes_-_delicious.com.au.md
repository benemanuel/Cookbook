# Spanakopita (Greek spinach pie) recipe - Recipes - delicious.com.au

## Ingredients

- 1kg frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
- ¾ cup olive oil, divided
- 1 leek, white and light green parts, finely sliced
- 4 green shallots, finely sliced
- 400g feta cheese, crumbled
- 50g rocket (arugula), roughly chopped
- 1 bunch fresh dill, fronds chopped
- 1 bunch fresh mint, leaves chopped
- ½ bunch fresh flat-leaf parsley, leaves chopped
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 3 eggs, lightly beaten
- 15 sheets filo pastry
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Lemon wedges, to serve

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Cook leek and shallots until softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Combine squeezed spinach, leek mixture, feta, rocket, dill, mint, parsley, lemon zest, and eggs in a large bowl. Mix well and season with pepper.
3. Brush a large round oven-proof pan or springform tin (about 28cm) with olive oil. Lay one sheet of filo in the pan, letting excess hang over the edge. Brush with olive oil. Continue layering 7–8 filo sheets, brushing each with oil, rotating each sheet slightly so overhang covers all sides.
4. Spoon the spinach filling into the lined pan and spread evenly.
5. Fold overhanging filo back over the filling. Layer remaining filo sheets on top, brushing each with oil. Tuck edges in neatly. Score the top lightly with a knife. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.
6. Bake for 45 minutes until golden and crisp. Rest 10 minutes before slicing. Serve with lemon wedges. Serves 8.
