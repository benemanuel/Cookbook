# Latvian Cold Beet Soup

## Ingredients

- 3 medium beets, cooked, peeled, and grated or diced
- 3–4 cucumbers, diced
- 4–5 green onions, sliced
- Bunch of fresh dill, chopped
- 1 liter kefir or buttermilk
- 1 Tbsp horseradish or mustard
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 2–4 hard-boiled eggs, halved
- Cold boiled water, to thin as needed
- 2 Tbsp lemon juice
- 100g sour cream

## Instructions

1. Cook beets until tender by boiling or roasting, then let cool, peel, and grate or dice.
2. Combine beets, cucumbers, green onions, and dill in a large bowl.
3. Pour in kefir or buttermilk and stir in horseradish or mustard, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
4. Add cold boiled water to reach desired consistency and stir well, tasting for seasoning.
5. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled and serve cold, topped with hard-boiled egg halves and a dollop of sour cream.
