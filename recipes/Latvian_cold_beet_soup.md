# Latvian cold beet soup

## Ingredients

- 3 medium beets, cooked, peeled, and grated or diced
- 3–4 cucumbers, diced
- 4–5 green onions, sliced
- Bunch of fresh dill, chopped
- 1 liter kefir or buttermilk
- 1 Tbsp horseradish or mustard
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 2–4 hard-boiled eggs, halved
- Cold boiled water, to thin as needed
- 2 Tbsp lemon juice
- 100g sour cream

## Instructions

1. Cook beets until tender (boil or roast). Let cool, then peel and grate or dice.
2. Combine beets, cucumbers, green onions, and dill in a large bowl.
3. Pour in kefir or buttermilk. Stir in horseradish or mustard, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
4. Add cold boiled water to reach desired consistency. Stir well and taste for seasoning.
5. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled. Serve cold, topped with hard-boiled egg halves and a dollop of sour cream.
