# Sweet Potato Sourdough Cinnamon Rolls

*Source: instagram.com*

## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 350 g sweet potato blended with milk
- 50 g flour for tangzhong
- 250 g milk for tangzhong
- 2 eggs
- 360 g stiff sourdough starter
- 950 g bread flour
- 225 g sugar
- 115 g soft butter
- 9 g salt
- 80–120 ml milk for dough

**Filling**
- 345 g butter
- 270 g demerara sugar
- 3 tsp cinnamon
- 2 tsp nutmeg
- 2 tsp ginger
- 2 tsp cloves

**Frosting**
- 230 g cream cheese
- 230 g mascarpone
- 30 g butter
- 60 g powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Instructions

1. Make tangzhong by heating 50 g flour and 250 g milk to 65°C.
2. Blend sweet potato with milk.
3. Mix tangzhong, sweet potato, flour, sugar, and eggs together and let autolyse for 30 minutes.
4. Add stiff starter and milk, resting for 30 minutes (add more milk only if dough is dry).
5. Add salt and butter and knead for 5–10 minutes.
6. Perform 3 folds every 40 minutes during a 4-hour bulk fermentation.
7. Refrigerate overnight.
8. Roll dough to 0.5 cm thickness, spread filling evenly, roll up, and cut into 5 cm pieces.
9. Proof for 3–5 hours.
10. Bake at 175°C for 25–30 minutes.
11. Frost the warm rolls with the cream cheese mixture.
