# Hungarian Plum Cake

*Source: cookpad.com*

## Ingredients

- 4 medium eggs
- 190g butter
- 190g cane sugar
- 1 large pinch salt
- 125g plain yogurt
- 12g baking powder
- 190g whole grain spelt flour
- 5ml ground cinnamon
- 350g plum preserve
- Ground cinnamon for topping
- 100g granulated sugar
- 30g coarsely chopped walnuts

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter slightly, add the sugar and mix, then add the egg yolks and yogurt and mix well.
2. Add the cinnamon and the flour mixed with sifted baking powder and mix together.
3. Beat the egg whites until stiff and fold them into the batter carefully little by little.
4. Pour the batter into a buttered and floured 21x31 cm baking pan, then place the halved plums on top and sprinkle with cinnamon.
5. Bake in a preheated oven at 180 degrees C for 40-45 minutes until a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean, covering the top with foil halfway through to prevent over-browning.
6. While the cake bakes, prepare the praline by melting the sugar in a pot over heat, stirring constantly until it becomes light brown, then add the walnuts and stir.
7. Pour the hot praline onto an oiled board, roll it thinly with an oiled rolling pin, cut it into pieces, and sprinkle over the baked cake brushed with plum jam.
