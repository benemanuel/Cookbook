# Tourlou Briam

## Ingredients

- 2 potatoes, sliced
- 1 large sweet potato, sliced
- 1 large eggplant, diced
- 1 brown onion, sliced
- 1 large zucchini, diced thick
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 400g crushed tomato
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 3/4 cup water
- Feta cheese for sprinkling (optional)
- Fresh parsley to garnish

## Instructions

1. Arrange vegetables into a large baking tray.
2. Combine all sauce ingredients in a bowl and pour over the vegetables.
3. Mix all ingredients through thoroughly and cover with a layer of baking paper then tightly with aluminium foil.
4. Bake at 180C fan forced for 40 minutes or until potatoes are cooked.
5. Uncover and cook for an additional 15-20 minutes to develop colour.
6. Once ready, drizzle extra olive oil over the top, sprinkle with feta cheese, and garnish with fresh parsley.
