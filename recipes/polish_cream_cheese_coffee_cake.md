# Polish Cream Cheese Coffee Cake

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup (115g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 2 packages (450g total) cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 large egg
- ½ cup chopped pecans
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- ⅓ cup (75g) unsalted butter, cold and cubed

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13 baking pan.
2. Beat butter and sugar until light, then add egg, sour cream, and vanilla; mix until combined.
3. Stir in flour, baking soda, and baking powder until a soft dough forms.
4. Beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth, then mix in egg.
5. Combine pecans, brown sugar, flour, and cold butter, then mix with fingertips until crumbly.
6. Press two-thirds of the cake dough into the prepared pan.
7. Spread cream cheese filling evenly over the dough.
8. Drop spoonfuls of remaining dough over the filling.
9. Sprinkle streusel over the top.
10. Bake for 45 minutes until golden and set, then cool before slicing.
