# Pipitada (Melon Seed Drink)

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sweet melon seeds
- 3 cups water
- 2 tablespoons caster (superfine) sugar
- 1 tablespoon orange blossom water

## Instructions

1. Grind the seeds to a fine powder in a food processor a day before you want to serve.
2. Put the ground seeds in cheesecloth, bring the corners together and tie securely.
3. Fill a bowl with about 3 cups of water and immerse the ground seeds in their cheesecloth bag in the water.
4. Leave in the fridge overnight, squeezing the cheesecloth from time to time to release the milky essence from the seeds into the water.
5. Squeeze the cloth tightly to extract as much moisture as possible out of the seeds and discard the seeds.
6. Add the sugar and orange blossom water to the milky liquid and refrigerate in a pitcher.
7. Serve chilled.
