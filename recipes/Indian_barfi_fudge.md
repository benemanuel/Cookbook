# Indian Barfi Fudge

## Ingredients

- 720ml milk powder
- 360ml powdered sugar
- 120ml plus 45ml milk
- 45ml butter or ghee
- 1/2 to 5ml ground cardamom
- 1ml salt
- 5ml vanilla extract
- Approximately 30ml slivered pistachios
- Saffron, rose buds, and silver leaf for garnish

## Instructions

1. Grease a 9×6 baking pan.
2. Heat a heavy bottom pan on medium to low heat and add the butter and 120ml of milk, allowing the butter to melt.
3. Once the butter melts, add the milk powder while stirring constantly, then gradually add the powdered sugar.
4. If dry powder bits remain, add the three tablespoons of milk one tablespoon at a time until the mixture reaches a paste-like consistency.
5. While continuously stirring, cook the mixture for 8–9 minutes until it easily leaves the surface of the pan and resembles dough.
6. Turn off the heat and add vanilla extract, cardamom, and a tablespoon of pistachios, then mix well.
7. Spread the mixture evenly on the greased pan and top with the remaining tablespoon of pistachios.
8. Let the barfi set for at least three hours before serving or storing in an airtight container at room temperature for up to seven days.
