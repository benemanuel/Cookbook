# Smoked Salt Cured Lox | Traeger Grills

## Ingredients

- 125mL (½ cup) kosher salt
- 100g (½ cup) sugar
- ½ Tbsp cracked black pepper
- Zest of ½ lemon
- Zest of ½ orange
- ½ pack fresh dill, roughly chopped
- 450g (1 lb) salmon fillet, skin-on

## Instructions

1. Mix together the kosher salt, sugar, cracked pepper, lemon zest, orange zest, and dill to form the cure.
2. Place the salmon fillet skin-side down on a large sheet of plastic wrap. Coat the flesh side generously with the cure mixture, pressing it in.
3. Wrap the salmon tightly in the plastic wrap. Place in a baking dish and refrigerate for 20 hours, flipping the fish at the 10-hour mark.
4. After curing, unwrap the salmon and rinse off the cure under cold water. Pat dry with paper towels.
5. Set your smoker (e.g., Traeger) to 180°F. Place a tray of ice on the grill grate to keep the salmon cold while smoking.
6. Lay the salmon on the ice tray. Smoke for 30 minutes until lightly smoked on the outside but still silky and cured inside.
7. Slice thinly on the bias and serve. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 1 week.
