# Glyko Karydaki (Green Walnuts Preserve)

*Source: kopiaste.org*

## Ingredients

- 2 kilos (about 50) big, fresh green walnuts
- Pickling lime and water to cover them (1 heaped tablespoon lime for every litre of water)
- 1 cup lemon juice, divided (reserve 1/4 cup lemon to add at the end)
- 1 kilo sugar
- 5 cups water
- 1 piece of cinnamon stick
- 6-7 cloves
- 2-3 fragrant geranium leaves (optional)
- 50 blanched and roasted almonds

## Instructions

1. Thinly peel the outer skin of the walnuts with a sharp knife or a vegetable peeler, wearing latex gloves to prevent your hands from turning black.
2. Cut off the top part where the fruit is attached to the tree, as it is woody.
3. Take a skewer or a knitting needle and prick the walnuts horizontally and vertically, then put them in a bowl with water.
4. Change the water for six days, once in the morning and once in the evening.
5. On the sixth day, drain the walnuts and put some pickling lime in the bowl with water and mix.
6. Put the walnuts in the lime water and let them soak for 3-4 hours to help keep them firm.
7. Wash the walnuts thoroughly two or three times, then put them into a large pot with water to cover them.
8. Boil for 3 minutes and discard the water.
9. Boil again for 2-3 minutes, drain and add cold water.
10. Boil for a third time until soft, then drain and add cold water.
11. Test if the walnuts are ready by pricking them with a skewer or needle; if it passes through easily, they are ready for the final process.
12. Put the blanched almonds in a baking tray and roast them for 15-20 minutes, then set aside to cool and sterilize the jars while the oven is hot.
13. When the walnuts are soft, add the lemon juice.
14. Leave them in the water for 1 hour to make them crunchy and shiny.
15. Drain them and put them back in the pot, then add the sugar, water and spices.
16. Leave them for a while for the sugar to dissolve, then boil the preserve until the temperature reaches 105°C.
17. Add the leftover lemon juice and remove from the heat.
18. When it cools, store in clean and sterilized jars.
19. Store in a cupboard.
