# Sandwich Sourdough

## Ingredients

### Overnight Sourdough Levain
- 12 g sourdough starter
- 64 g water at 77°F/25°C
- 64 g bread flour

### Main Dough
- 600 g bread flour
- 100 g whole wheat flour
- 490 g water at 77°F/25°C
- 140 g levain
- 14 g salt
- 40 g maple syrup or honey
- 80 g softened butter or 65 g olive oil
- 1 egg plus equal weight of flour

### Topping
- Mixed seeds such as sesame, pumpkin, chia, poppy, and sunflower seeds
