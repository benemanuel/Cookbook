# Sourdough Teff Lachuch (Yemenite-Style Pancakes)

## Ingredients

- 140g Teff Flour (Brown or Ivory)
- 125g Active Sourdough Starter (100% hydration)
- 210ml Water (Room temperature)
- 2g Fine Sea Salt (About ½ tsp)
- 1 tsp Sugar (Optional, to help yeast activity and browning)

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the teff flour, sourdough starter, and salt.
2. Gradually whisk in the water until the batter is smooth with the consistency of heavy cream, runny enough to spread itself but thick enough to coat a spoon.
3. Cover the bowl and let it ferment at room temperature for 4 to 8 hours or overnight in a cool spot until you see tiny active bubbles on the surface.
4. Place your skillet over medium heat, keeping it just warm enough to start the bubbles but not so hot that it sets the bottom immediately.
5. Ladle about 60ml to 80ml of batter into the center of the pan.
6. Use the back of the ladle to gently spread the batter in a spiral motion toward the edges.
7. Leave the pancake uncovered for the first minute to allow bubbles to form from the outside in.
8. Once the surface is full of holes but still looks slightly wet, place the lid on the pan.
9. Steam for 30–60 seconds until the top is set and matte, without flipping the pancake.
10. Remove the pancake and place it on a clean kitchen towel.
11. Before cooking the next pancake, take the pan off the heat and wipe the surface with a very wet cloth or briefly run the bottom under cold water to lower the temperature.
12. Serve warm with Hilbeh, Skhug, melted butter, or honey.
