# Cookies

## Ingredients

- 240ml white sugar
- 240ml brown sugar
- 240ml oleo (margarine)
- 240ml oil
- 1 egg
- 5ml vanilla
- 5ml cream of tartar
- 5ml baking soda
- 240ml rice crispies
- 240ml quick oatmeal
- 5ml salt
- 240ml flaked coconut
- 240ml chopped nuts
- 840ml flour

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients in order.
2. Drop mixture by the tablespoonful onto a baking sheet.
3. Press down with fingers.
4. Bake at 350 degrees for 13 minutes.
