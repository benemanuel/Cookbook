# Tuna Steak

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp orange juice
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1.5 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 1/3 clove garlic
- 1/4 tsp chopped fresh oregano
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- 2 tuna steaks (115 g each)

## Instructions

1. Marinate the tuna steaks in a mixture of orange juice, soy sauce, olive oil, lemon juice, parsley, garlic, and oregano for no more than 20 minutes.
2. Breadcrumb the tuna steaks before cooking.
3. Heat a skillet over high heat and cook the tuna steaks for 3-4 minutes on each side.
