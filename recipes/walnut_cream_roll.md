# Walnut Cream Roll

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs, separated
- 2ml salt
- 5ml vanilla
- 1/4 c flour
- 1/2 c finely chopped walnuts
- 1 1/2 c heavy whipping cream
- 45ml powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. Whip egg whites until soft peaks form, add salt and vanilla and beat 2 minutes more.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk egg yolks until thick and lemon colored.
3. Fold a small portion of egg white into the yolk mixture to lighten.
4. Fold the yolk mixture into the egg whites.
5. Gently fold in flour and walnuts.
6. Pour into a parchment lined 15x10" pan and spread evenly.
7. Bake at 191°C for 12 minutes.
8. Let cool for 5 minutes.
9. Loosen edges and turn onto a towel sprinkled with powdered sugar.
10. Peel off the parchment paper.
11. Roll the towel and cake together from the short side.
12. Cool completely.
13. Whip cream until soft peaks form, add sugar and beat until it holds shape.
14. Unroll the cake and spread filling evenly.
15. Reroll the cake and use the remainder of filling to cover the outside.
16. Sprinkle with cinnamon or chocolate if desired.
17. Keep refrigerated.
