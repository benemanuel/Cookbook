# Pretzel

## Instructions

1. Working one piece at a time, roll the dough into a long rope shape and set it on a clean surface in the shape of a "U".
2. Lift the ends of the rope and twist 2 times.
3. Fold the twist over and press the ends on the dough.
4. Cover the dough with a towel and let rise for 30-60 minutes or until puffy.
5. Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C) making sure that the oven rack is in the center position.
6. Boil the water in a large stockpot and add 2 tablespoons baking soda and 1 tablespoon of dark brown sugar.
7. Boil the pretzels, 3-4 at a time, on both sides for 30 seconds each, making sure not to crowd the pot.
8. Use a mesh strainer to remove the pretzels and place back on the parchment paper.
9. Brush each pretzel with egg wash and sprinkle with salt.
10. Bake the pretzels at 425°F (218°C) for 12-14 minutes or until golden brown.
