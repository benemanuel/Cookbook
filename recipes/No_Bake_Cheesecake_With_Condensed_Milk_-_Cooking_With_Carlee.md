# No Bake Cheesecake With Condensed Milk

*Source: cookingwithcarlee.com*

## Ingredients

- Graham cracker crust
- 454g cream cheese (softened)
- 397g sweetened condensed milk
- 80ml sour cream
- 10ml lemon juice (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, mix 454g cream cheese on low speed until it is creamy and lump free. Be sure to scrape the sides of the bowl and the beater a few times along the way.
2. Slowly add the 397g sweetened condensed milk, mixing on low after each addition until it is incorporated fully.
3. Mix in 80ml sour cream until incorporated. If using 10ml lemon juice, add now.
4. Spoon the cream cheese mixture into the prepared Graham cracker crust.
5. Chill for at least 4 hours, though longer is better, before serving.
