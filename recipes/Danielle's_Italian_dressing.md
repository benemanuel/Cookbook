# Danielle's Italian Dressing

## Ingredients

- 240ml extra virgin olive oil
- 100ml white wine vinegar
- 30g minced garlic
- 1/2 lemon
- 5ml dried oregano
- 1ml chilli flakes
- 40g minced shallots or onion

## Instructions

1. Finely chop the garlic and shallots, or use onion instead of shallots if preferred.
2. Sizzle the garlic and shallots in the olive oil over low heat for five minutes.
3. Add in the chilli and oregano and sizzle for one more minute.
4. Pour this combined mixture into a jar and allow to cool before adding the vinegar.
5. Juice half a lemon and add salt to the jar.
6. Shake well to combine.
7. Pour onto the salad and enjoy.
