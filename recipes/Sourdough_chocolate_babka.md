# Sourdough Chocolate Babka

## Ingredients

### Starter
- 80g white wheat flour (bread flour)
- 20g brown sugar
- 25g active mother sourdough starter
- 35g water

### Dough
- 350g white wheat flour (bread flour)
- All of the above starter (approximately 160g)
- 2 eggs (approximately 100g)
- 50g brown sugar
- 85g unsalted softened butter
- 7g salt
- 110g milk

### Chocolate Filling
- 130g melted dark chocolate (70%)
- 120g melted butter
- 10g cocoa powder
- 10g powdered brown sugar
- 50g roughly chopped chocolate or chocolate chips (optional)

## Instructions

1. In the morning, prepare your sourdough starter by mixing the white wheat flour, brown sugar, active mother starter, and water, then leave it to ferment until doubled in volume, which may take 4–12 hours depending on temperature and starter strength.

2. In the late afternoon, mix milk, starter, sugar, eggs, flour, and salt together in a large bowl, adding additional liquid only if necessary to incorporate all the flour, then knead for 3–4 minutes and rest for 15–20 minutes.

3. Knead in half of the butter quantity, then add and knead in the other half, kneading for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic, then shape into a ball and place in a clean bowl covered with plastic wrap.

4. Ferment the dough at room temperature for 2 hours, then place the bowl in the fridge overnight.

5. In the morning, prepare the filling by mixing melted chocolate, melted butter, cocoa powder, and powdered sugar together, then refrigerate for at least half an hour until the mixture thickens to a spreadable consistency.

6. Prepare two lined tins and remove the dough from the fridge onto a lightly floured surface.

7. Roll the dough into a square measuring approximately 45×45 cm and 4–5 mm thick, then spread the filling across the dough leaving a 1–1.5 cm edge, optionally sprinkle chopped chocolate or chips, and tightly roll into a log.

8. Cut the log in half lengthwise, place one half over the other, and braid the two halves together, then cut the braided dough to fit the tins.

9. Leave the babkas to rise until approximately doubled in volume, which may take 4–7 hours depending on temperature.

10. Preheat the oven to 220°C/428°F and bake the babkas for 10 minutes at this temperature, then decrease to 200°C/392°F and bake for another 30–40 minutes until nicely baked.

11. Remove the babkas from the tins and leave them to cool on a cooling rack before serving.
